## 1️⃣ Week 2 문법 총정리 (15분)

### Day 1: 현재완료 (Present Perfect)
```
형태: have/has + 과거분사

경험: I have worked in 5 companies.
     (5개 회사에서 일한 경험이 있다)

완료: I have finished the report.
     (보고서를 끝냈다 - 지금 완성됨)

계속: I have worked here for 5 years.
     (5년 동안 여기서 일해왔다 - 지금도 일함)
```

### Day 2: 미래형 (Future)
```
Will: 즉석 결정, 예측, 약속
→ I'll send it now. (지금 막 결정)
→ I think it will succeed. (예측)

Be going to: 계획, 의도, 증거 있는 예측
→ I'm going to meet a client. (계획)
→ It's going to rain. (구름 보임)

현재진행형: 확정된 일정
→ I'm meeting the CEO at 3pm. (약속 잡힘)
```

### Day 3: 비교급/최상급 (Comparatives/Superlatives)
```
비교급: A is -er/more ~ than B
→ This is cheaper than that.
→ This is more efficient than that.

최상급: A is the -est/most ~
→ This is the cheapest option.
→ This is the most important project.

불규칙: good-better-best, bad-worse-worst
```

### Day 4: 전치사 (Prepositions)
```
시간: at (시각), on (날짜), in (기간)
→ at 2pm, on Monday, in March

장소: at (지점), on (층/표면), in (공간 안)
→ at the office, on the 5th floor, in Seoul

비즈니스: by (방법), for (대상), with (함께)
→ by email, for Samsung, with the team
```

### Day 4: 접속사 (Conjunctions)
```
등위: and (그리고), but (하지만), or (또는), so (그래서)

종속: because (왜냐하면), if (만약), when (언제),
     although (비록~이지만), before/after (전/후)

→ I'm late because of traffic, so I'll join online.
→ If you need help, call me.
```

## 2️⃣ 고급 비즈니스 표현 30개 (15분)

### 프레젠테이션 표현
```
1. Let me start by introducing...
   (~를 소개하는 것부터 시작하겠습니다)

2. I'd like to talk about...
   (~에 대해 이야기하고 싶습니다)

3. As you can see from the data...
   (데이터에서 보시다시피...)

4. This shows that...
   (이것은 ~을 보여줍니다)

5. Moving on to the next point...
   (다음 요점으로 넘어가서...)

6. To summarize...
   (요약하자면...)

7. In conclusion...
   (결론적으로...)
```

### 의견 표현
```
8. In my opinion...
   (제 의견으로는...)

9. I think/believe that...
   (~라고 생각합니다)

10. From my experience...
    (제 경험상...)

11. I agree/disagree with...
    (~에 동의합니다/동의하지 않습니다)

12. That's a good point, but...
    (좋은 지적이지만...)
```

### 제안/추천 표현
```
13. I suggest/recommend that...
    (~를 제안/추천합니다)

14. We should consider...
    (~를 고려해야 합니다)

15. It would be better to...
    (~하는 것이 더 나을 것입니다)

16. Why don't we...?
    (~하는 게 어떨까요?)

17. How about...?
    (~는 어떤가요?)
```

### 협상/논의 표현
```
18. Can we discuss...?
    (~를 논의할 수 있을까요?)

19. I'd like to negotiate...
    (~를 협상하고 싶습니다)

20. We're willing to...
    (~할 의향이 있습니다)

21. That's acceptable/unacceptable.
    (받아들일 수 있습니다/없습니다)

22. Let's find a compromise.
    (절충안을 찾아봅시다)
```

### 요청/확인 표현
```
23. Could you please...?
    (~해주시겠어요?)

24. Would it be possible to...?
    (~하는 것이 가능할까요?)

25. I was wondering if...
    (~인지 궁금합니다)

26. Just to confirm...
    (확인하자면...)

27. Let me make sure I understand...
    (제가 이해한 게 맞는지 확인하겠습니다...)
```

### 문제 해결 표현
```
28. We're facing a challenge with...
    (~에 어려움을 겪고 있습니다)

29. The issue is that...
    (문제는 ~입니다)

30. We need to address...
    (~를 해결해야 합니다)
```

## 3️⃣ 통합 실전 대화 (10분)

### 대화 1: 프로젝트 현황 보고 (모든 문법 통합)
```
Manager: How is the project going?
        (프로젝트는 어떻게 되고 있어요?)

Employee: We have completed 80% of the work so far.
         I'm meeting the client tomorrow at 2pm to show
         the progress. The project is going well, although
         we have had some technical issues.
         (지금까지 작업의 80%를 완료했습니다.
         내일 2시에 고객을 만나 진행 상황을 보여줄 거예요.
         프로젝트는 잘 진행되고 있지만 몇 가지 기술적 문제가
         있었습니다.)

Manager: What kind of issues?
        (어떤 종류의 문제인가요?)

Employee: We had some compatibility problems, but we have
         solved them all. In my opinion, the new system is
         much better than the old one. It's faster and more
         efficient.
         (몇 가지 호환성 문제가 있었지만 모두 해결했습니다.
         제 의견으로는 새 시스템이 기존 것보다 훨씬 좋습니다.
         더 빠르고 효율적입니다.)

Manager: Excellent! When will you finish the project?
        (훌륭해요! 프로젝트를 언제 끝낼 건가요?)

Employee: We'll finish by the end of this month. I'll send you
         the final report as soon as we complete everything.
         (이번 달 말까지 끝낼 거예요. 모든 것을 완료하는 대로
         최종 보고서를 보내드리겠습니다.)
```

### 대화 2: 제품 제안 및 협상
```
Sales: Thank you for meeting with me today. I'd like to
      talk about our new product.
      (오늘 만나주셔서 감사합니다. 새 제품에 대해 이야기하고
      싶습니다.)

Client: Sure. I've heard good things about your products.
       (물론이죠. 귀사 제품에 대해 좋은 이야기를 들었습니다.)

Sales: Our new product is the most advanced in the market.
      It's more efficient than our competitors' products,
      and it's also more affordable. We have sold over
      1,000 units since we launched it in January.
      (우리 신제품은 시장에서 가장 선진적입니다.
      경쟁사 제품보다 더 효율적이고 더 저렴합니다.
      1월에 출시한 이후 1,000개 이상 판매했습니다.)

Client: That sounds interesting. What's the price?
       (흥미롭네요. 가격이 어떻게 되나요?)

Sales: The regular price is $500, but if you order 100 units,
      we can offer a 20% discount. We're willing to negotiate
      on larger orders.
      (정가는 500달러이지만 100개를 주문하시면 20% 할인을
      제공할 수 있습니다. 대량 주문에 대해서는 협상할 의향이
      있습니다.)

Client: Let me discuss this with my team, and I'll get back
       to you by next week.
       (팀과 논의하고 다음 주까지 연락드리겠습니다.)

Sales: Perfect. If you have any questions, please contact me
      by email or phone.
      (완벽합니다. 질문이 있으시면 이메일이나 전화로
      연락주세요.)
```

## 4️⃣ 비즈니스 이메일 작성 연습 (10분)

### 이메일 템플릿

#### 1) 미팅 요청 이메일
```
Subject: Meeting Request - New Project Discussion

Dear Mr./Ms. [Name],

I hope this email finds you well.

I'm writing to request a meeting to discuss the new project
we are going to launch next month. I have worked on the
proposal for the past two weeks, and I'd like to share it
with you.

Would it be possible to meet on Tuesday at 2pm? We can meet
at my office on the 5th floor, or I can come to your office
if that's more convenient.

Please let me know if this works for you. If not, I'm
available on Wednesday afternoon as well.

I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

#### 2) 프로젝트 업데이트 이메일
```
Subject: Project Update - Week 2 Progress

Dear Team,

I wanted to update you on our project progress.

We have completed the following tasks:
- Finished the design phase
- Completed 80% of development
- Conducted initial testing

We are on schedule, although we have encountered some minor
technical issues. However, we have solved them all, and the
project is going well.

The next steps are:
- Complete the remaining development by Friday
- Conduct final testing next week
- Launch the product by the end of this month

If you have any questions or concerns, please let me know.
I'll be happy to discuss them at our meeting on Thursday at 10am.

Thank you for your continued support.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## 5️⃣ 종합 연습 문제 (10분)

### 문제 1: 문법 선택 (빈칸 채우기)
1. I __________ (work) here for 5 years, and I'm __________ (meet) the CEO tomorrow.
2. This option is __________ (cheap) than that one, but that one is the __________ (good).
3. The meeting is __________ Monday __________ 2pm __________ the conference room.
4. We need to hurry __________ the deadline is tight, __________ I'll finish it __________ Friday.
5. I __________ (already/complete) the report, __________ I __________ (send) it to you now.

### 문제 2: 문장 번역
1. 저는 3년 동안 삼성에서 일해왔고, 내일 새 프로젝트를 시작할 예정입니다.
2. 이 제품이 저것보다 더 비싸지만, 품질이 가장 좋습니다.
3. 월요일 10시에 5층 회의실에서 만나요.
4. 비가 올 것 같으니까 우산을 가져가세요.
5. 비록 어렵지만 제 생각에는 가능합니다.

1. have worked, meeting

1. I have worked for Samsung for 3 years, and I'm going to start a new project tomorrow.
