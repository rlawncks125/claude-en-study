## 📚 Project Overview

### The Comprehensive Business Challenge:

You are **Alex Martinez**, newly appointed **Chief Operating Officer (COO)** of **GlobalTech Industries**, a mid-size technology manufacturing company with 2,000 employees across 6 countries.

**Company Background:**
- **Revenue:** $500M annually
- **Employees:** 2,000 across US, Mexico, Poland, India, China, Singapore
- **Products:** Enterprise hardware solutions and IoT devices
- **Recent Performance:** Declining—revenue down 12%, profitability down 18%
- **Market Position:** Losing ground to more agile competitors

**Your Mission:**
The CEO hired you to lead a comprehensive operational transformation. You have 100 days to assess the situation, develop a strategy, and begin execution.

## 🎯 Part 1: Situation Analysis (45 minutes)

### The Challenges You Face:

Read through these detailed scenarios. You'll need to address ALL of them in your project.

#### **Challenge 1: Operational Inefficiency**

**Situation:**
The CFO shared alarming data: operational costs are 23% higher than industry benchmarks. Manufacturing facilities are running at 62% capacity utilization. Lead times have increased from 45 to 78 days over the past two years. Quality defects have risen 31%.

**Email from CFO:**
```
From: Jennifer Chen, CFO
To: Alex Martinez, COO
Subject: Urgent: Q3 Financial Performance

Alex,

I need to make you aware of some concerning trends in our operational metrics:

• Manufacturing costs per unit: UP 18% YoY
• Overhead allocation: 34% vs. 22% industry average
• Inventory carrying costs: $45M (should be ~$28M)
• Facility utilization: 62% average across all sites
• Working capital tied up: $120M (excessive)

We're burning cash at current run rate. Board is demanding answers.
Can we meet Monday 8 AM to discuss?

Jennifer
```

**Data you've collected:**
- Mexico facility: 78% utilization, lowest cost, highest quality
- Poland facility: 45% utilization, highest cost, outdated equipment
- China facility: 61% utilization, quality issues (8% defect rate)
- US facilities (2): 58% average utilization, mid-range cost

#### **Challenge 2: Leadership Team Dysfunction**

**Situation:**
Your leadership team is fractured. The VP of Manufacturing (based in US) and VP of Supply Chain (based in Singapore) haven't spoken directly in 6 months—they communicate only through their teams. The Chief Technology Officer wants to automate everything immediately. The Chief Human Resources Officer is concerned about layoffs and morale.

**Text message from CEO:**
```
Alex - heads up, the exec team meeting is going to be rough tomorrow.
Manufacturing and Supply Chain are at war over the Poland facility.
CTO wants to spend $50M on automation. CHRO is worried about
retention (turnover up to 28%). Everyone's protecting their turf.
This is why I brought you in. Fix it. -David
```

**What you've observed:**
- Meetings are combative and political
- Decisions get revisited constantly
- Finger-pointing when things go wrong
- Information hoarding between departments
- Several "shadow org charts" operating informally

#### **Challenge 3: Digital Transformation Resistance**

**Situation:**
The previous COO attempted to implement a new ERP system. Two years and $15M later, the project is stalled at 30% completion. Adoption is abysmal. Employees are using workarounds and shadow systems. The IT department is demoralized.

**Email from CTO:**
```
From: Michael Zhang, CTO
To: Alex Martinez, COO
Subject: ERP Implementation Status

Alex,

As you requested, here's the situation with our ERP project:

Started: Jan 2022
Budget: $15M (spent $12M so far)
Timeline: Originally 18 months (now 30 months, still incomplete)
Current Status: 30% deployed, 23% user adoption, 47% system accuracy

Root causes of failure:
• Inadequate change management
• Poor requirements gathering
• Resistance from middle management
• Lack of executive alignment
• Vendor relationship issues

Recommendation: Either commit $8M more and 18 additional months,
or cut losses and abandon. Can't continue in current state.

Michael
```

—to InnovateTech. That's the third major loss this quarter. Customers are

**Market Research Data:**

- Your time-to-market: 18 months average

- Your customer satisfaction: 67%
- Industry leader: 89%
