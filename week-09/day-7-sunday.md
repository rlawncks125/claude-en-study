   a) I work here for five years.
   b) I have worked here for five years.
   c) I am working here for five years.
   d) I worked here for five years.

2. Identify the sentence with correct conditional structure:
   a) If I would have known, I would tell you.
   b) If I knew, I would have told you.
   c) If I had known, I would have told you.
   d) If I know, I would tell you.

3. Which sentence correctly uses passive voice?
   a) The report was completed by the team yesterday.
   b) The team was completing the report yesterday.
   c) The report completed by the team yesterday.
   d) The team has completed the report yesterday.

   a) The team are working on the project.
   b) The team is working on the project.
   c) The teams is working on the project.
   d) The team were working on the project.

5. Which sentence correctly uses a gerund?
