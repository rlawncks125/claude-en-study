## Consulting Frameworks

### Classic Frameworks | 클래식 프레임워크

#### McKinsey 7-S Framework

| Element | Korean | Definition | Key Questions |
|---------|--------|------------|---------------|
| Strategy | 전략 | Plan to achieve goals | Where are we going? How do we get there? |
| Structure | 구조 | Organizational design | How is the company organized? Reporting lines? |
| Systems | 시스템 | Processes and procedures | What are the key processes? How do they work? |
| Shared Values | 공유가치 | Core beliefs and culture | What does the company stand for? |
| Style | 스타일 | Leadership approach | How do leaders behave? What's encouraged? |
| Staff | 인력 | Human capital | What competencies do we have/need? |
| Skills | 역량 | Organizational capabilities | What are we really good at? |

**Usage Example:**
"Let's apply the 7-S framework to diagnose the organizational issues. We've identified misalignment between strategy and structure."
"조직 문제를 진단하기 위해 7-S 프레임워크를 적용합시다. 전략과 구조 간 불일치를 확인했습니다."

#### Porter's Five Forces

| Force | Korean | Description | Example Questions |
|-------|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Competitive Rivalry | 경쟁 강도 | Intensity of competition | How many competitors? Market share distribution? |
| Threat of New Entrants | 신규진입위협 | Ease of market entry | What are entry barriers? Capital requirements? |
| Bargaining Power of Suppliers | 공급자 협상력 | Supplier influence | How concentrated are suppliers? Switching costs? |
| Bargaining Power of Buyers | 구매자 협상력 | Customer influence | How concentrated are buyers? Price sensitivity? |
| Threat of Substitutes | 대체재 위협 | Alternative products/services | What alternatives exist? Price-performance trade-off? |

**Usage Example:**
"Our Five Forces analysis reveals that supplier power is high due to concentration, while buyer power is increasing."
"파이브 포스 분석 결과, 공급자의 집중도로 인해 공급자 협상력이 높고, 구매자 협상력도 증가하고 있습니다."

#### BCG Growth-Share Matrix

| Quadrant | Korean | Characteristics | Strategy |
|----------|--------|-----------------|----------|
| Stars | 스타 | High growth, high share | Invest for growth |
| Cash Cows | 캐시카우 | Low growth, high share | Harvest cash |
| Question Marks | 물음표 | High growth, low share | Invest selectively or divest |
| Dogs | 개 | Low growth, low share | Divest or maintain |

**Usage Example:**
"Product line A is a cash cow generating strong cash flow, while product line B is a question mark requiring investment decisions."
"제품군 A는 강한 현금 흐름을 창출하는 캐시카우이고, 제품군 B는 투자 결정이 필요한 물음표입니다."

#### SWOT Analysis

| Category | Korean | Focus | Examples |
|----------|--------|-------|----------|
| Strengths | 강점 | Internal advantages | Strong brand, proprietary technology, skilled workforce |
| Weaknesses | 약점 | Internal disadvantages | Limited distribution, high costs, outdated systems |
| Opportunities | 기회 | External positive factors | Market growth, regulatory changes, new technologies |
| Threats | 위협 | External negative factors | Competition, economic downturn, substitute products |

**Usage Example:**
"The SWOT analysis highlights our strength in innovation but reveals weakness in go-to-market capabilities."
"SWOT 분석은 혁신에서의 강점을 부각하지만 시장 진출 역량의 약점을 드러냅니다."

## Client Communication

### Professional Communication Phrases | 전문 커뮤니케이션 표현

#### Opening Meetings | 회의 시작

**Formal:**
- "Thank you all for making time for this important discussion"
- "이 중요한 논의를 위해 시간 내주셔서 감사합니다"
- "I'd like to start by outlining the agenda for today"
- "오늘의 안건을 개요하는 것으로 시작하고 싶습니다"
- "Before we dive in, let me provide some context"
- "본격적으로 들어가기 전에, 맥락을 설명하겠습니다"

**Building Rapport:**
- "I hope everyone had a good weekend"
- "모두 좋은 주말 보내셨기를 바랍니다"
- "Thank you for your warm welcome to the team"
- "팀에 따뜻하게 맞이해주셔서 감사합니다"

#### Presenting Findings | 결과 발표

**Structuring:**
- "Let me walk you through our key findings"
- "주요 발견사항을 설명하겠습니다"
- "Our analysis reveals three critical insights"
- "분석 결과 세 가지 중요한 인사이트가 드러났습니다"
- "I'll structure this around four themes"
- "네 가지 주제로 구성하겠습니다"

**Highlighting:**
- "The most striking finding is..."
- "가장 두드러진 발견은..."
- "What stands out in the data is..."
- "데이터에서 두드러지는 것은..."
- "Particularly noteworthy is..."
- "특히 주목할 만한 것은..."

#### Making Recommendations | 권고사항 제시

**Strong:**
- "We strongly recommend..."
- "강력히 권장합니다..."
- "Based on our analysis, you should..."
- "분석에 근거하여, ...해야 합니다"
- "The clear path forward is..."
- "명확한 앞으로의 길은..."

**Moderate:**
- "We suggest considering..."
- "...을 고려하기를 제안합니다"
- "You may want to explore..."
- "...을 탐색하고 싶으실 수 있습니다"
- "It would be prudent to..."
- "...하는 것이 현명할 것입니다"

#### Handling Pushback | 반박 대응

**Acknowledging:**
- "That's a valid concern"
- "타당한 우려입니다"
- "I appreciate that perspective"
- "그 관점을 높이 평가합니다"
- "You raise an important point"
- "중요한 점을 제기하셨습니다"

**Responding:**
- "Let me address that directly"
- "직접적으로 답변하겠습니다"
- "Here's how we thought about that"
- "우리가 그것을 어떻게 생각했는지 말씀드리겠습니다"
- "The data actually suggests..."
- "데이터는 실제로 ...을 시사합니다"

#### Closing Meetings | 회의 마무리

**Summarizing:**
- "To recap, we've covered three key areas"
- "요약하자면, 세 가지 주요 영역을 다뤘습니다"
- "In summary, our recommendations are..."
- "요약하면, 우리의 권고사항은..."
- "Let me quickly summarize our discussion"
- "논의를 간단히 요약하겠습니다"

**Next Steps:**
- "The immediate next steps are..."
- "즉각적인 다음 단계는..."
- "Moving forward, we'll focus on..."
- "앞으로 나아가며, ...에 집중하겠습니다"
- "I'll follow up with a detailed action plan"
- "상세한 실행 계획으로 후속 조치하겠습니다"

## Case Interview Terminology

### Case Interview Frameworks | 케이스 인터뷰 프레임워크

#### Market Entry Case

**Structure:**
```
1. Market Attractiveness
   - Market size and growth
   - 시장 규모 및 성장
   - Customer needs and segments
   - Competitive landscape

2. Company Fit
   - Core competencies
   - Resources and capabilities
   - Strategic fit

3. Entry Mode
   - Organic vs. acquisition
   - Partnership options
   - Timing considerations

4. Financial Analysis
   - Investment required
   - Expected returns
   - Break-even timeline

5. Risks and Mitigations
   - Key risks
   - Mitigation strategies
```

#### Profitability Case

**Framework:**
```
Profit = Revenue - Costs

Revenue = Volume × Price
- Volume drivers: Market size, market share, repeat purchases
- Price drivers: Willingness to pay, competition, positioning

Costs = Fixed Costs + Variable Costs
- Fixed: Overhead, facilities, salaries
- Variable: Materials, direct labor, commissions
```

#### Pricing Case

**Considerations:**
```
1. Cost-Based
   - What are our costs?
   - 비용은 얼마인가?
   - What margin do we need?

2. Value-Based
   - What value do we provide to customers?
   - What are they willing to pay?

3. Competition-Based
   - How are competitors priced?
   - What's our positioning?

4. Strategic Considerations
   - Penetration vs. skimming
   - Market share goals
   - Brand implications
```

- "What's the primary objective here - growth or profitability?"
- "여기서 주요 목표는 무엇인가요 - 성장인가요 수익성인가요?"

#### Structuring Your Approach | 접근법 구조화

**Framework Introduction:**
- "I'd like to structure my analysis into four buckets"
- "분석을 네 개의 범주로 구조화하고 싶습니다"

- "제 프레임워크를 설명하겠습니다"

**MECE Check:**
- "These categories are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive"
- "이 범주들은 상호배타적이며 전체포괄적입니다"

- "What do we know about the competitive landscape?"
- "경쟁 환경에 대해 무엇을 알고 있습니까?"

#### Doing Math Out Loud | 계산 설명

**Explaining Calculations:**
- "Let me calculate the market size. If the population is 300 million..."
- "시장 규모를 계산하겠습니다. 인구가 3억 명이라면..."
- "So that gives us approximately..."
- "그래서 대략...을 얻습니다"

- "간단히 하기 위해 반올림하겠습니다"

#### Synthesizing | 종합

**Summarizing:**
- "To synthesize what we've learned..."
- "배운 것을 종합하면..."
- "Pulling this together, I would recommend..."
- "이것을 종합하면, ...을 권장합니다"

### Sample Case Interview Dialogue | 케이스 인터뷰 대화 샘플

**Interviewer (I):** Our client is a major US retailer considering whether to expand into online grocery delivery. Should they do it?
**인터뷰어:** 고객사는 온라인 식품 배송으로 확장을 고려하는 주요 미국 소매업체입니다. 해야 할까요?

**Candidate (C):** That's an interesting question. Before I structure my approach, let me clarify a few things. First, what's driving this consideration - are they seeing declining foot traffic or is this a growth opportunity? Second, do they currently have any online presence? And third, what's the primary objective - is it market share growth, profitability, or defending their position?
**응시자:** 흥미로운 질문입니다. 접근법을 구조화하기 전에 몇 가지를 명확히 하겠습니다. 첫째, 이 고려 사항을 이끄는 것은 무엇입니까 - 매장 방문 감소를 보고 있습니까 아니면 성장 기회입니까? 둘째, 현재 온라인 존재가 있습니까? 셋째, 주요 목표는 무엇입니까 - 시장 점유율 성장, 수익성, 또는 포지션 방어입니까?

**I:** Good questions. They're seeing competitors enter this space successfully and want to defend market share. They have a basic e-commerce site but not for groceries. The goal is profitable growth.
**인터뷰어:** 좋은 질문들입니다. 경쟁자들이 이 공간에 성공적으로 진입하는 것을 보고 있고 시장 점유율을 방어하고 싶어합니다. 기본적인 전자상거래 사이트가 있지만 식품용은 아닙니다. 목표는 수익성 있는 성장입니다.

**C:** Perfect. I'd like to structure my analysis into four main areas. First, market attractiveness - is this a compelling market to enter? Second, company capabilities - do we have what it takes to compete? Third, economic viability - can we make money? And fourth, implementation - how would we execute? Does that sound reasonable?
**응시자:** 완벽합니다. 분석을 네 가지 주요 영역으로 구조화하고 싶습니다. 첫째, 시장 매력도 - 이것이 진입하기 매력적인 시장입니까? 둘째, 회사 역량 - 경쟁할 수 있는 것이 있습니까? 셋째, 경제적 타당성 - 돈을 벌 수 있습니까? 넷째, 실행 - 어떻게 실행할 것입니까? 합리적으로 들립니까?

**I:** Yes, that works. Where would you like to start?
**인터뷰어:** 네, 그것은 효과적입니다. 어디서 시작하고 싶습니까?

**C:** Let's start with market attractiveness. Can you tell me about the size and growth rate of the online grocery market?
**응시자:** 시장 매력도로 시작합시다. 온라인 식품 시장의 규모와 성장률에 대해 말씀해주시겠습니까?

*[Continues with deeper analysis]*
*[더 깊은 분석으로 계속]*

## Slide Writing

### Slide Titles Best Practices | 슬라이드 제목 모범 사례

#### Action Titles (Preferred in Consulting)

**Not Recommended (Topic):**
- "Market Analysis"
- "시장 분석"

**Recommended (Insight):**
- "The market is growing at 15% annually, driven by demographic shifts"
- "시장은 인구통계학적 변화에 의해 연간 15% 성장하고 있습니다"

**More Examples:**
```
Instead of: "Cost Structure"
Use: "Labor costs represent 60% of total costs, presenting the biggest opportunity"

Instead of: "Competitive Landscape"
Use: "Three players control 75% of the market, limiting our negotiating power"

Instead of: "Customer Segments"
Use: "High-value customers (20% of base) generate 80% of profits"
```

### Slide Layout Principles | 슬라이드 레이아웃 원칙

#### The Pyramid Principle

**Structure:**
```
1. Start with the answer (top of pyramid)
   - 답으로 시작 (피라미드 상단)
2. Support with 3-5 key arguments
   - 3-5개의 주요 논거로 지원
3. Back each argument with data
   - 각 논거를 데이터로 뒷받침
```

**Example:**
```
Title: We should enter the market now
답변: 지금 시장에 진입해야 합니다

Supporting Points:
1. Market is large and growing (15% CAGR)
2. We have competitive advantages in technology
3. Financial returns are attractive (25% IRR)
4. Window of opportunity is closing (2 new entrants expected)
```

## Executive Summary Writing

### Executive Summary Structure | 요약문 구조

#### Standard Format

```
1. Situation / Context (2-3 sentences)
   - What's the current state?
   - 현재 상태는?

2. Complication / Problem (2-3 sentences)
   - What's the issue or opportunity?
   - 문제나 기회는?

3. Question (1 sentence)
   - What are we trying to answer?
   - 무엇을 답하려고 하는가?

4. Answer / Recommendation (3-5 bullets)
   - What should be done?
   - 무엇을 해야 하는가?

5. Supporting Rationale (3-5 bullets)
   - Why these recommendations?
   - 왜 이 권고사항들인가?

6. Expected Impact (2-3 bullets)
   - What are the outcomes?
   - 결과는?
```

### Sample Executive Summaries | 요약문 샘플

#### Strategy Executive Summary

```
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: GROWTH STRATEGY FOR COMPANY X

Situation:
Company X is a leading manufacturer of industrial equipment with $2B in revenue and 15% EBIT margins. After years of steady growth, revenue has plateaued, and the company is seeking strategies to reignite growth while maintaining profitability.

상황:
X사는 20억 달러 매출과 15% EBIT 마진을 가진 선도적 산업 장비 제조업체입니다. 수년간의 꾸준한 성장 후, 매출이 정체되었고, 회사는 수익성을 유지하면서 성장을 재점화할 전략을 찾고 있습니다.

Complication:
The core market is mature and growing at only 2% annually. New digital competitors are disrupting traditional business models. Customer preferences are shifting toward integrated solutions rather than standalone products.

문제점:
핵심 시장은 성숙하여 연간 2%만 성장하고 있습니다. 새로운 디지털 경쟁자들이 전통적 비즈니스 모델을 파괴하고 있습니다. 고객 선호가 독립 제품이 아닌 통합 솔루션으로 이동하고 있습니다.

Key Question:
How can Company X achieve 8-10% annual revenue growth over the next five years?

Recommendations:
1. Shift from products to solutions: Bundle equipment with services and software to create recurring revenue streams
2. Expand into adjacent markets: Leverage core competencies to enter $500M adjacent equipment market
3. Pursue strategic acquisitions: Acquire 2-3 companies with complementary technologies to accelerate digital capabilities
4. Optimize core business: Implement operational improvements to fund growth investments

Supporting Rationale:
• Solutions approach increases customer lifetime value by 3x and improves retention from 70% to 90%
• Adjacent markets growing at 12% annually with attractive margins (18-20%)
• Build vs. buy analysis shows acquisition is 2x faster and 30% less risky than internal development
• $150M in operational improvements can fund 50% of growth investments

Expected Impact:
• Revenue growth from 2% to 9% annually
• EBIT margin improvement from 15% to 17%
• Total shareholder return of 15-20% annually
• Transformation of business model from transactional to strategic partnerships
```

#### Operational Excellence Executive Summary

```
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE PROGRAM

Situation:
Company Y operates 50 distribution centers across North America, processing $5B in annual shipments. Operating costs have increased 20% over the past three years, eroding margins and making the company less competitive.

Complication:
Each distribution center operates independently with different processes, systems, and performance levels. The top quartile centers operate at 40% lower cost than bottom quartile. Lack of standardization prevents scaling of best practices. Customer service is inconsistent.

Key Question:
How can Company Y reduce operating costs by 15-20% while improving service quality?

Recommendations:
1. Standardize core processes: Implement best practice SOPs across all centers
2. Optimize network: Consolidate from 50 to 42 centers, improving utilization
3. Automate material handling: Deploy automated storage and retrieval systems in top 15 centers
4. Enhance workforce management: Implement labor planning tools and performance management

Supporting Rationale:
• Process standardization enabled 25% productivity improvement in pilot centers
• Network optimization eliminates $40M in fixed costs with minimal service impact
• Automation reduces labor costs 30% in high-volume centers, with 3-year payback
• Workforce management tools improve utilization from 65% to 82%

Expected Impact:
• $200M in annual cost savings (18% reduction)
• Service level improvement from 94% to 98% on-time delivery
• Working capital reduction of $100M through inventory optimization
• 24-month implementation timeline with first benefits in Month 6
```

```
Profit = Revenue - Costs

   - Corporate overhead

   - Customer acquisition
   - Bad debt provisions

I would prioritize investigating:
• Why net interest margin may be compressed
• Whether digital competitors are taking market share
• If branch costs are too high relative to value delivered
```
