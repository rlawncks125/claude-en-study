# Industry-Specific English Learning Modules

## Overview | 개요

This directory contains specialized English learning modules tailored for different industries. Each module provides comprehensive vocabulary, communication patterns, and practical scenarios specific to that industry.

이 디렉토리는 다양한 산업 분야에 특화된 영어 학습 모듈을 포함하고 있습니다. 각 모듈은 해당 산업에 특화된 포괄적인 어휘, 커뮤니케이션 패턴, 실전 시나리오를 제공합니다.

## How to Use These Modules | 사용 방법

### 1. Self-Assessment | 자가 진단
First, identify your industry and current English proficiency level:
- **Beginner (초급)**: Limited industry-specific vocabulary
- **Intermediate (중급)**: Can understand basic industry terms
- **Advanced (고급)**: Need refinement in specialized communication

먼저 자신의 산업 분야와 현재 영어 수준을 파악하세요.

### 2. Study Path | 학습 경로

#### For Beginners | 초급자용
1. Start with vocabulary sections
2. Listen to provided example dialogues
3. Practice reading industry documents
4. Complete basic exercises

#### For Intermediate Learners | 중급자용
1. Review vocabulary quickly
2. Focus on phrases and expressions
3. Practice dialogues with partners
4. Complete intermediate exercises
5. Read real-world case studies

#### For Advanced Learners | 고급자용
1. Skim vocabulary for gaps
2. Focus on complex scenarios
3. Practice presentations and writing
4. Complete advanced exercises
5. Analyze real industry communications

### 3. Practice Schedule | 연습 일정

**Daily (매일) - 30-45 minutes:**
- 10 min: Vocabulary review (10-20 new terms)
- 15 min: Read industry articles or documents
- 10 min: Practice dialogues or role-play
- 10 min: Writing practice or exercises

**Weekly (주간) - 2-3 hours:**
- Complete one module section
- Review all vocabulary from the week
- Complete practice exercises
- Write industry-specific documents
- Record yourself in role-play scenarios

**Monthly (월간) - Self-assessment:**
- Test your progress with assessment quizzes
- Compare your writing to templates
- Record presentations and review
- Seek feedback from colleagues or teachers

### 4. Integration with Main Program | 메인 프로그램과의 통합

These industry-specific modules are designed to complement the main 9-week Business English program:

이 산업별 모듈은 메인 9주 비즈니스 영어 프로그램과 함께 사용하도록 설계되었습니다.

**Integration Strategy:**

#### Week 1-3: Foundation + Industry Basics
- Main program: Focus on core business English
- Industry modules: Learn 20-30 key terms per week
- Practice: Use industry examples in email writing

#### Week 4-6: Intermediate Skills + Industry Application
- Main program: Presentations, meetings, negotiations
- Industry modules: Practice industry-specific scenarios
- Practice: Present industry topics, conduct industry meetings

#### Week 7-9: Advanced Communication + Mastery
- Main program: Advanced business writing, leadership
- Industry modules: Master complex industry communications
- Practice: Write industry reports, lead industry discussions

**Example Integration:**

If you're working through Week 4 (Presentations) and you're in Finance:
1. Learn presentation skills from main program
2. Study financial presentation vocabulary from finance-english.md
3. Practice presenting a quarterly earnings report
4. Use templates from both modules
