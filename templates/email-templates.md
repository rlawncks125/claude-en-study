## 1. Meeting Requests | 회의 요청

### 1.1 Initial Meeting Request
**초기 회의 요청**

```
Subject: Meeting Request: [TOPIC]

Dear [NAME],

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to request a meeting to discuss [SPECIFIC TOPIC/PURPOSE].

I believe a conversation about [BRIEF CONTEXT] would be valuable for [REASON/BENEFIT]. I would appreciate the opportunity to [SPECIFIC GOAL].

Would you be available for a [DURATION]-minute meeting sometime during [TIME FRAME]? I am flexible and happy to work around your schedule.

Some potential topics we could cover:
- [TOPIC 1]
- [TOPIC 2]
- [TOPIC 3]

Please let me know your availability, and I will send a calendar invitation.

Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR TITLE]
[CONTACT INFORMATION]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 명확한 회의 목적 제시
- 상대방의 일정을 존중하는 태도
- 구체적인 안건 미리 제공

---

### 1.2 Urgent Meeting Request
**긴급 회의 요청**

```
Subject: Urgent: Meeting Request Regarding [ISSUE]

Dear [NAME],

I need to discuss an urgent matter regarding [ISSUE/SITUATION] that requires immediate attention.

The issue is: [BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF THE URGENT MATTER]

I would greatly appreciate if we could meet as soon as possible. Would you be available for a brief call today or tomorrow? The meeting should take no more than [DURATION] minutes.

If these times don't work, please suggest an alternative:
- [TIME OPTION 1]
- [TIME OPTION 2]
- [TIME OPTION 3]

I apologize for the short notice and appreciate your understanding.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- "Urgent"를 제목에 명시하여 긴급성 표현
- 긴급한 이유를 간략하게 설명
- 짧은 통지에 대한 사과 포함

---

### 1.3 Meeting Reschedule Request
**회의 일정 변경 요청**

```
Subject: Request to Reschedule Our Meeting on [DATE]

Dear [NAME],

I hope this message finds you well. Unfortunately, due to [REASON], I need to request rescheduling our meeting currently scheduled for [DATE/TIME].

I sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause. The matter is important to me, and I want to ensure we have adequate time to discuss [TOPIC].

Would any of the following alternative times work for you?
- [OPTION 1: DATE/TIME]
- [OPTION 2: DATE/TIME]
- [OPTION 3: DATE/TIME]

If none of these work, please suggest times that suit your schedule, and I will do my best to accommodate.

Thank you for your understanding and flexibility.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 일정 변경이 필요한 구체적인 이유 제시
- 진심 어린 사과
- 대체 시간 옵션 제공

```
- [OPTION 3: DATE/TIME + TIMEZONE]

[YOUR NAME]
```

- 다음 단계 안내

## 2. Follow-ups | 후속 조치

### 2.1 General Follow-up Email
**일반 후속 이메일**

```
Subject: Following Up: [ORIGINAL SUBJECT]

Dear [NAME],

I hope this email finds you well. I wanted to follow up on my previous email sent on [DATE] regarding [TOPIC].

I understand you have a busy schedule, but I wanted to check if you had a chance to review my request about [BRIEF REMINDER OF REQUEST].

[OPTIONAL: ADDITIONAL CONTEXT OR NEW INFORMATION]

If you need any additional information or clarification, please don't hesitate to let me know. I'm happy to provide any details that would be helpful.

I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 이전 이메일 날짜 명시
- 상대방의 바쁜 일정에 대한 이해 표현
- 추가 정보 제공 의사 표명

---

### 2.2 Second Follow-up (Polite Persistence)
**두 번째 후속 조치 (정중한 재요청)**

```
Subject: Re: [ORIGINAL SUBJECT] - Second Follow-up

Dear [NAME],

I hope you're doing well. I'm following up on my emails from [DATE 1] and [DATE 2] regarding [TOPIC].

I realize this may have gotten buried in your inbox, so I wanted to bring it back to your attention. [BRIEF RECAP OF REQUEST/ISSUE].

This matter is [TIME-SENSITIVE/IMPORTANT] because [REASON], and I would greatly appreciate your input by [DATE IF APPLICABLE].

Would it be helpful if we scheduled a brief call to discuss this? I'm available [TIME OPTIONS].

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 이전 이메일들의 날짜 참조
- 중요성/긴급성 설명
- 통화 옵션 제안으로 대안 제시

---

### 2.3 Follow-up After No Response
**무응답 후 최종 후속 조치**

```
Subject: Final Follow-up: [TOPIC]

Dear [NAME],

I've reached out a few times regarding [TOPIC] but haven't heard back from you. I completely understand that priorities change and schedules can be overwhelming.

If this is no longer a priority or if the timing isn't right, please let me know and I'll be happy to follow up at a more convenient time.

Alternatively, if there's someone else I should be connecting with regarding this matter, I would appreciate if you could point me in the right direction.

If I don't hear back by [DATE], I'll assume this isn't a priority at the moment and will close this loop.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 상대방에게 "나가는 길" 제공
- 다른 담당자 문의
- 마감 기한 설정하여 대화 종료

---

### 2.4 Follow-up After Meeting
**회의 후 후속 조치**

```
Subject: Great Meeting You - Next Steps

Dear [NAME],

It was a pleasure meeting with you [TODAY/YESTERDAY] to discuss [TOPIC]. I appreciated your insights on [SPECIFIC POINT DISCUSSED].

As discussed, I wanted to follow up with:
- [PROMISED ITEM 1]
- [PROMISED ITEM 2]
- [ATTACHED DOCUMENT/INFORMATION]

Based on our conversation, the next steps are:
1. [YOUR ACTION ITEM] - I will complete this by [DATE]
2. [THEIR ACTION ITEM] - You mentioned you would [ACTION]
3. [MUTUAL ACTION ITEM]

Please let me know if I've missed anything or if you'd like to add anything to this list.

I look forward to [NEXT MILESTONE/MEETING].

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 회의에서 논의된 구체적 내용 언급
- 약속한 사항 이행
- 상호 실행 항목 명확화

---

### 2.5 Follow-up After Sending Proposal

```
- [BENEFIT 1]
- [BENEFIT 2]
- [BENEFIT 3]

I'm confident that [YOUR SOLUTION] will help you achieve [THEIR GOAL]. Would you be available for a brief call this week to discuss next steps?

I'm available:
- [TIME SLOT 1]
- [TIME SLOT 2]
- [TIME SLOT 3]

Looking forward to hearing your thoughts.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 제안서의 주요 이점 재강조
- 질문에 답변할 준비가 되어 있음을 표현
- 구체적인 통화 시간 제안

### 2.6 Follow-up to Connect Two People
**두 사람 연결 후속 조치**

```
Subject: Connecting [NAME 1] and [NAME 2]

Dear [NAME 1] and [NAME 2],

I'm delighted to introduce you to each other.

[NAME 1], meet [NAME 2]. [NAME 2] is [BRIEF BACKGROUND AND RELEVANT EXPERTISE].

[NAME 2], meet [NAME 1]. [NAME 1] is [BRIEF BACKGROUND AND RELEVANT EXPERTISE].

I thought you two should connect because [SPECIFIC REASON FOR CONNECTION]. I believe you could [MUTUAL BENEFIT].

I'll leave it to you both to take it from here. [OPTIONAL: SUGGESTION FOR NEXT STEP]

Best of luck with your conversation!

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 양측 모두에게 상대방 소개
- 연결의 이유와 목적 명확히
- 이후 진행은 당사자들에게 맡김

## 3. Apologies and Corrections | 사과 및 정정

### 3.1 Apology for Delay
**지연에 대한 사과**

```
Subject: Apology for Delay in [TASK/RESPONSE]

Dear [NAME],

I sincerely apologize for the delay in [GETTING BACK TO YOU/DELIVERING THE PROJECT/etc.]. I know you were expecting [DELIVERABLE] by [ORIGINAL DATE].

The delay was due to [BRIEF, HONEST EXPLANATION without making excuses].

I take full responsibility for this situation. Here's what I'm doing to address it:
- [ACTION STEP 1]
- [ACTION STEP 2]
- [REVISED TIMELINE]

I understand this may have caused inconvenience, and I want to assure you that [WHAT YOU'RE DOING TO PREVENT FUTURE OCCURRENCES].

The revised delivery date is [NEW DATE]. If this creates any issues, please let me know and we can discuss alternatives.

Thank you for your patience and understanding.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 진심 어린 사과
- 변명이 아닌 간단한 설명
- 해결책과 새로운 일정 제시
- 재발 방지 대책

---

### 3.2 Apology for Mistake
**실수에 대한 사과**

```
Subject: Correction and Apology: [ISSUE]

Dear [NAME],

I am writing to apologize for the error in [DOCUMENT/EMAIL/REPORT] that I sent on [DATE].

I made a mistake regarding [SPECIFIC ERROR]. The correct information should be:
- [CORRECTION 1]
- [CORRECTION 2]

This error occurred because [BRIEF EXPLANATION], and I take full responsibility.

I have taken the following steps to prevent this from happening again:
- [PREVENTIVE MEASURE 1]
- [PREVENTIVE MEASURE 2]

Please find attached the corrected [DOCUMENT] for your records. I sincerely apologize for any confusion or inconvenience this may have caused.

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact me.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 구체적으로 무엇이 잘못되었는지 명시
- 정확한 정보 제공
- 재발 방지 조치 설명

---

### 3.3 Apology for Missing Meeting
**회의 불참에 대한 사과**

```
Subject: Sincere Apology for Missing Our Meeting

Dear [NAME],

I am writing to sincerely apologize for missing our scheduled meeting on [DATE] at [TIME]. There is no excuse for my absence, and I understand the value of your time.

[BRIEF HONEST EXPLANATION - optional, only if appropriate]

I would very much like to reschedule at your earliest convenience. I am available:
- [OPTION 1]
- [OPTION 2]
- [OPTION 3]

Alternatively, I'm happy to work around your schedule entirely.

I have also [COMPLETED ANY PREPARATORY WORK/REVIEWED MATERIALS] to ensure our rescheduled meeting is as productive as possible.

Again, I sincerely apologize for any inconvenience I caused. Thank you for your understanding.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 변명하지 않고 책임 인정
- 적극적으로 재일정 제안
- 생산적인 회의를 위한 준비 완료

---

### 3.4 Apology for Miscommunication
**잘못된 의사소통에 대한 사과**

```
Subject: Clarification and Apology Regarding [TOPIC]

Dear [NAME],

I want to apologize for the confusion regarding [ISSUE/TOPIC]. Upon reflection, I realize my previous communication was not clear and may have led to misunderstanding.

What I intended to communicate was:
[CLEAR EXPLANATION OF INTENDED MESSAGE]

However, I can see how my message could have been interpreted as:
[ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF HOW IT WAS MISUNDERSTOOD]

To clarify:
- [CLEAR POINT 1]
- [CLEAR POINT 2]
- [CLEAR POINT 3]

Moving forward, [HOW YOU'LL ENSURE CLEAR COMMUNICATION].

I apologize for any confusion this may have caused and appreciate your patience.

Please let me know if you need any further clarification.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 오해가 발생한 부분 인정
- 본래 의도 명확히 설명
- 향후 명확한 소통 방법 제시

---

```
This is not the level of service we strive to provide, and I understand your frustration. You deserved better, and we fell short.

Here's what happened:
[BRIEF EXPLANATION]

Here's what we're doing to make it right:

2. [ADDITIONAL COMPENSATION if appropriate]
3. [LONG-TERM FIX to prevent recurrence]

[OPTIONAL: DIRECT CONTACT INFORMATION]
If you have any questions or concerns, please contact me directly at [PHONE/EMAIL]. I will personally ensure this is resolved to your satisfaction.

We value your business and hope to have the opportunity to restore your confidence in [COMPANY].

Sincerely,
[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR TITLE]
```

**한국어 설명:**
- 고객 경험에 공감
- 구체적인 해결책 제시
- 개인적인 연락처 제공으로 책임감 표현

```
Grateful for [SPECIFIC CONTRIBUTION]
```

### Subject Line Best Practices | 제목 모범 사례

✅ **DO:**
- Keep it under 50 characters
- Be specific and descriptive
- Use action verbs when appropriate
- Include dates/deadlines when relevant
- Personalize when possible
- Use clear, direct language

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use all caps (unless URGENT and truly urgent)
- Be vague ("Meeting" vs "Meeting Request: Q4 Planning")
- Use too many special characters (!!!, ???)
- Mislead about content
- Use clickbait tactics
- Write overly long subjects

**한국어 팁:**
- 제목만 보고 이메일 내용을 파악할 수 있어야 함
- 긴급한 경우에만 "Urgent" 사용
- 날짜와 시간 포함 시 명확하게
- 개인화하되 과하지 않게

## 11. Email Etiquette Guide | 이메일 에티켓 가이드

### Structure | 구조

**1. Subject Line | 제목**
- Clear and specific
- Reflects email content
- Includes deadline if applicable

**2. Greeting | 인사**
- Formal: "Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name],"
- Semi-formal: "Dear [First Name],"
- Casual: "Hi [First Name],"
- Team: "Hi Team," or "Hello everyone,"

**3. Opening | 시작**
- Formal: "I hope this email finds you well."
- Direct: "I'm writing to..."
- Casual: "Hope you're doing well!"

**4. Body | 본문**
- Get to the point in first paragraph
- Use short paragraphs
- Bullet points for clarity
- Bold key information
- One main topic per email

**5. Closing | 마무리**
- Clear call-to-action
- Next steps
- Thank you
- Availability for questions

**6. Sign-off | 맺음말**
- Formal: "Sincerely," "Respectfully,"
- Semi-formal: "Best regards," "Kind regards,"
- Casual: "Best," "Thanks,"
- Internal: "Thanks," "Cheers,"

**7. Signature | 서명**
```
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Company Name]
[Phone Number]
[Email Address]
[Optional: LinkedIn/Website]
```

### Tone Guidelines | 톤 가이드

**Formal Tone | 격식체**
When to use:
- First contact with senior executives
- Legal or official matters
- External stakeholders you don't know
- Sensitive topics

Language:
- "I would like to..."
- "I would appreciate..."
- "Could you please..."
- "Thank you for your consideration"

**Semi-formal Tone | 준격식체**
When to use:
- Regular business contacts
- Colleagues in other departments
- Established clients
- Most professional situations

Language:
- "I'd like to..."
- "I appreciate..."
- "Can you..."
- "Thanks for your help"

**Casual Tone | 비격식체**
When to use:
- Close colleagues
- Team members
- Informal updates
- Internal quick messages

Language:
- "Want to..."
- "Thanks!"
- "Let me know"
- "Sounds good"

### Response Times | 응답 시간

**Expected Response Times:**
- Urgent: Within 2-4 hours
- Important: Within 24 hours
- Routine: Within 48 hours
- FYI/No action needed: No response necessary

**If You Can't Respond Fully:**
Send a quick acknowledgment:
"Thanks for your email. I'm looking into this and will get back to you by [DATE]."

### Common Mistakes to Avoid | 피해야 할 실수

❌ **Reply All Abuse**
- Only use "Reply All" when everyone needs to see your response
- Remove people from CC if no longer relevant

❌ **Unclear Requests**
- Be specific about what you need
- Include deadlines
- Specify format/method of delivery

❌ **Poor Grammar/Spelling**
- Always proofread
- Use spell check
- Read aloud before sending

❌ **Wrong Attachments**
- Double-check attachments before sending
- Mention attachments in email body
- Use descriptive file names

❌ **Emotion in Professional Emails**
- Don't send emails when angry
- Avoid sarcasm (doesn't translate well)
- Keep it professional even when frustrated

❌ **Too Long**
- Respect people's time
- Use bullets instead of long paragraphs
- Consider if a call would be better

❌ **CC/BCC Misuse**
- CC: When people need to be informed
- BCC: When protecting recipient privacy
- Don't use CC to passive-aggressively include others

### Email Security | 이메일 보안

**Before Sending:**
- Check recipient addresses carefully
- Verify confidential information
- Use BCC for mass emails
- Encrypt sensitive information
- Password-protect confidential attachments

**Sensitive Information:**
Never include in email:
- Passwords
- Credit card numbers
- Social security numbers
- Personal health information
- Confidential business data (without encryption)

### Professional Tips | 전문가 팁

**1. The 24-Hour Rule**
For emotional or sensitive emails, draft it, then wait 24 hours before sending.

**2. The 2-Minute Rule**
If an email can be answered in 2 minutes, do it immediately.

**3. The 5-Sentence Rule**
Try to keep emails to 5 sentences or less when possible.

**4. The Mobile Preview Test**
Check how your email looks on mobile devices.

**5. The Subject Line Test**
Can someone understand the email's purpose from the subject alone?

### Email Management | 이메일 관리

**Inbox Zero Strategy:**
- Process emails in batches
- Use folders/labels
- Unsubscribe from unnecessary emails
- Use filters and rules
- Schedule email time

**When to Use Email vs. Other Tools:**
- Email: Formal communication, documentation, external contacts
- Chat/Slack: Quick questions, team coordination
- Phone/Video: Complex discussions, sensitive topics, brainstorming
- Meeting: Multiple stakeholders, major decisions, complex projects
