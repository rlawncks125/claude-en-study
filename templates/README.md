## 🎯 How to Use These Templates | 템플릿 사용 방법

### Step 1: Choose the Right Template | 적절한 템플릿 선택
Browse the collection and select the template that best matches your situation.
상황에 가장 적합한 템플릿을 찾아 선택하세요.

### Step 2: Fill in the Blanks | 빈칸 채우기
Templates use this format:
- `[YOUR NAME]` - Replace with your information
- `[RECIPIENT NAME]` - Replace with recipient's details
- `[SPECIFIC DETAILS]` - Add context-specific information
- `[DATE/TIME]` - Add temporal information

### Step 3: Customize | 맞춤 설정
Adapt the template to your:
- Company culture | 회사 문화
- Relationship with recipient | 수신자와의 관계
- Specific situation | 구체적인 상황
- Regional preferences | 지역적 선호도

### Step 4: Review | 검토
Before sending, check:
- ✅ All placeholders replaced
- ✅ Tone is appropriate
- ✅ Grammar and spelling
- ✅ Attachments included (if any)
- ✅ Recipients are correct

## 💡 Template Usage Tips | 템플릿 사용 팁

### 1. **Personalization is Key** | 개인화가 핵심
Never send templates as-is. Add personal touches:
- Reference previous conversations | 이전 대화 참조
- Use recipient's name | 수신자 이름 사용
- Add relevant context | 관련 맥락 추가

### 2. **Adjust Formality Level** | 격식 수준 조정
Consider your relationship:
- **Formal:** New clients, executives, official documents
- **Semi-formal:** Regular colleagues, established clients
- **Casual:** Close team members, informal updates

### 3. **Cultural Sensitivity** | 문화적 민감성
Be aware of:
- Time zones | 시간대
- Holidays and working days | 휴일 및 근무일
- Communication preferences | 커뮤니케이션 선호도
- Cultural norms | 문화적 규범

### 4. **Keep It Concise** | 간결하게 유지
- Get to the point quickly
- Use short paragraphs
- Bullet points for clarity
- One main topic per communication

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid | 피해야 할 일반적인 실수

### 1. **Using Templates Without Customization**
❌ **Don't:** Send generic templates with obvious placeholders
✅ **Do:** Fully customize and personalize each communication

### 2. **Wrong Tone for Audience**
❌ **Don't:** Use casual language with new clients
✅ **Do:** Match formality to relationship and context

### 3. **Forgetting to Proofread**
❌ **Don't:** Send immediately after filling in blanks
✅ **Do:** Review thoroughly for errors and appropriateness

### 4. **Over-relying on Templates**
❌ **Don't:** Use templates for every situation
✅ **Do:** Use templates as starting points, add authentic voice

### 5. **Ignoring Context**
❌ **Don't:** Apply the same template to different situations
✅ **Do:** Consider specific circumstances and adjust accordingly

## 🎓 Best Practices | 모범 사례

### Email Best Practices:
1. **Clear subject lines** - Recipient should know the topic immediately
2. **Professional greeting** - Use appropriate salutation
3. **Concise body** - Get to the point in first paragraph
4. **Clear call-to-action** - State what you need clearly
5. **Professional closing** - Sign off appropriately

### Presentation Best Practices:
1. **Know your audience** - Tailor content to their needs
2. **Clear structure** - Use logical flow
3. **Visual hierarchy** - Important points stand out
4. **Practice delivery** - Rehearse before presenting
5. **Prepare for questions** - Anticipate Q&A

### Document Best Practices:
1. **Executive summary first** - Busy readers appreciate this
2. **Clear structure** - Use headings and sections
3. **Visual aids** - Charts, tables when appropriate
4. **Proofread multiple times** - Errors damage credibility
5. **Consistent formatting** - Professional appearance matters
