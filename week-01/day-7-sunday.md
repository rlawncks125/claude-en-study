### 문법 패턴 (Grammar Patterns)

#### 1. I am + 형용사/명사
```
I am happy.
I am a student.
I am good.
```

#### 2. This is / That is
```
This is my phone. (가까이)
That is a car. (멀리)
```

#### 3. I + 동사 + 명사
```
I eat food.
I drink water.
I like cats.
```

#### 4. I have + 숫자 + 명사
```
I have one book.
I have two friends.
I have three pens.
```

**최종 확인:** 모든 패턴 사용 가능 [ ]
