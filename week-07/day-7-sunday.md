### Part 3: Strategic Case Study (200 points)

**Background:**

MedTech Solutions is a $500M medical device company.

**Current Situation:**
- Core business: Traditional hospital equipment (75% of revenue)
- Growing but small: Remote patient monitoring (25% of revenue)
- Market trends: Shift to home healthcare, aging population
- Competition: Tech companies entering healthcare space
- Challenges: Regulatory constraints, long sales cycles

**Your Role:** Chief Strategy Officer presenting to the Board

**Financial Data:**
- Traditional equipment: $375M revenue, 15% growth, 40% margin
- Remote monitoring: $125M revenue, 60% growth, 25% margin
- R&D budget: $50M/year
- Cash: $200M

**Strategic Options:**

**Option A: Double Down on Core**
- Invest $150M in next-gen hospital equipment
- Leverage existing relationships
- Defend market share
- Lower risk, moderate returns

**Option B: Pivot to Digital Health**
- Invest $150M in remote monitoring platform
- Build software capabilities
- Pursue consumer market
- Higher risk, higher returns

**Option C: Hybrid Approach**
- Invest $75M in each segment
- Gradual transition
- Balanced risk
- Moderate returns

**Task:** Write a strategic recommendation (500 words)

