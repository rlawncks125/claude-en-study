## 1️⃣ Core Marketing Vocabulary (30분)

### A. Marketing Strategy & Planning

#### Marketing Framework (마케팅 프레임워크)
```
4Ps of Marketing:
├─ Product (제품)
├─ Price (가격)
├─ Place (유통)
└─ Promotion (프로모션)

7Ps (for services):
+ People (사람)
+ Process (프로세스)
+ Physical Evidence (물리적 증거)

SWOT Analysis:
├─ Strengths (강점)
├─ Weaknesses (약점)
├─ Opportunities (기회)
└─ Threats (위협)

Marketing Funnel (마케팅 퍼널):
1. Awareness (인지)
2. Interest (관심)
3. Consideration (고려)
4. Intent (의도)
5. Purchase (구매)
6. Loyalty (충성도)
7. Advocacy (옹호)

Customer Journey:
- Touchpoints (접점)
- Pain points (불편사항)
- Moments of truth (진실의 순간)
- Customer experience (고객 경험)
```

#### Market Research Terms (시장 조사 용어)
```
Market Analysis:
- Target market (목표 시장)
- Market segmentation (시장 세분화)
- Target audience (타겟 오디언스)
- Demographics (인구통계)
- Psychographics (심리통계)
- Market size (시장 규모)
- Market share (시장 점유율)
- Market penetration (시장 침투)
- Market trends (시장 트렌드)

Research Methods:
- Focus group (포커스 그룹)
- Survey (설문조사)
- A/B testing (A/B 테스트)
- User research (사용자 조사)
- Competitive analysis (경쟁사 분석)
- Buyer persona (구매자 페르소나)
```

### B. Branding & Positioning

#### Brand Strategy (브랜드 전략)
```
Brand Elements:
- Brand identity (브랜드 아이덴티티)
- Brand positioning (브랜드 포지셔닝)
- Brand personality (브랜드 개성)
- Brand voice (브랜드 보이스)
- Brand messaging (브랜드 메시징)
- Brand guidelines (브랜드 가이드라인)
- Visual identity (시각 아이덴티티)
- Logo and colors (로고 및 컬러)

Value Proposition:
- Unique Selling Proposition (USP, 독특한 판매 제안)
- Competitive advantage (경쟁 우위)
- Differentiation (차별화)
- Brand promise (브랜드 약속)

Brand Metrics:
- Brand awareness (브랜드 인지도)
- Brand recall (브랜드 회상)
- Brand recognition (브랜드 인식)
- Brand equity (브랜드 자산)
- Brand loyalty (브랜드 충성도)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS, 순 추천 지수)
```

### C. Digital Marketing

#### Channels & Tactics (채널 및 전술)
```
Digital Channels:
- SEO (Search Engine Optimization, 검색엔진 최적화)
- SEM (Search Engine Marketing, 검색엔진 마케팅)
- PPC (Pay-Per-Click, 클릭당 지불)
- Social media marketing (소셜 미디어 마케팅)
- Content marketing (콘텐츠 마케팅)
- Email marketing (이메일 마케팅)
- Influencer marketing (인플루언서 마케팅)
- Affiliate marketing (제휴 마케팅)

Content Types:
- Blog posts (블로그 포스트)
- Whitepapers (백서)
- Case studies (사례 연구)
- Infographics (인포그래픽)
- Videos (비디오)
- Podcasts (팟캐스트)
- Webinars (웨비나)
- E-books (전자책)

Social Media:
- Organic reach (자연 도달)
- Paid reach (유료 도달)
- Engagement rate (참여율)
- Impressions (노출수)
- Reach (도달)
- Click-through rate (CTR, 클릭률)
- Virality (바이럴성)
- User-generated content (UGC, 사용자 생성 콘텐츠)
```

#### Marketing Analytics (마케팅 분석)
```
Key Metrics:
- ROI (Return on Investment, 투자수익률)
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend, 광고 지출 수익률)
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost, 고객 획득 비용)
- LTV (Lifetime Value, 고객 생애 가치)
- Conversion rate (전환율)
- Bounce rate (이탈률)
- Traffic sources (트래픽 소스)
- Funnel conversion (퍼널 전환)

Tools:
- Google Analytics
- Google Ads
- Facebook Ads Manager
- CRM (Customer Relationship Management)
- Marketing automation
- Attribution modeling (어트리뷰션 모델링)
```

### D. Sales Terminology

#### Sales Process (영업 프로세스)
```
Sales Stages:
1. Prospecting (잠재고객 발굴)
2. Qualification (자격 심사)
3. Needs analysis (니즈 분석)
4. Presentation (제안)
5. Objection handling (이의 처리)
6. Closing (계약 체결)
7. Follow-up (후속 조치)

Sales Metrics:
- Sales pipeline (영업 파이프라인)
- Lead (리드)
  ├─ MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead)
  └─ SQL (Sales Qualified Lead)
- Conversion rate (전환율)
- Win rate (승률)
- Average deal size (평균 거래 규모)
- Sales cycle (영업 주기)
- Quota (할당량)
- Revenue (매출)
- Churn rate (이탈률)

Sales Techniques:
- SPIN selling (상황-문제-영향-필요)
- Consultative selling (컨설팅 판매)
- Solution selling (솔루션 판매)
- Value-based selling (가치 기반 판매)
- Challenger sale (도전자 판매)
- Upselling (상향 판매)
- Cross-selling (교차 판매)
- Referral (추천)
```

### Example 1: Marketing Campaign Plan

```
CAMPAIGN PLAN: Q3 2024 Product Launch
PRODUCT: WorkFlow AI Premium

1. CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW

Objective: Launch WorkFlow AI Premium tier and acquire 5,000 new
enterprise customers in Q3 2024.

Target Audience:
- Primary: Mid-size companies (100-500 employees)
- Secondary: Enterprise (500+ employees)
- Industries: Technology, Finance, Professional Services
- Titles: CTO, VP of Operations, IT Directors

Budget: $500,000
Timeline: July 1 - September 30, 2024

2. CAMPAIGN STRATEGY

Positioning:
"The AI productivity platform that grows with your business"

Key Messages:
- 40% more efficient than competitors
- Enterprise-grade security
- Seamless integration with existing tools
- Scalable pricing for growing teams

Value Proposition:
Save 15 hours per week per team through AI-powered automation,
with ROI payback in under 6 months.

3. MARKETING MIX

Digital Marketing (60% = $300K):
- Google Ads (Search & Display): $120K
  * Target keywords: "enterprise productivity software",
    "AI workflow automation", "team collaboration tools"
  * Expected clicks: 50,000
  * Target CPA: $240
- LinkedIn Ads: $100K
  * Sponsored content + InMail campaigns
  * Target: Decision-makers at target companies
- Content Marketing: $50K
  * 20 blog posts
  * 5 whitepapers
  * 10 case studies
- Email Marketing: $30K
  * 8 email campaigns to existing database of 50,000 leads

Events & PR (25% = $125K):
- Virtual product launch event: $50K
- Industry conference sponsorships: $50K
- Press releases & media outreach: $25K

Partnerships (15% = $75K):
- Influencer partnerships (5 B2B influencers): $40K
- Affiliate program setup: $20K
- Integration partner co-marketing: $15K

4. CONTENT CALENDAR

Week 1-2 (Pre-launch):
- Teaser campaign on social media
- Email sequence to warm leads
- Press release distribution

Week 3-4 (Launch):
- Virtual launch event
- Product demo webinars (weekly)
- Blog post series: "10 Ways AI Transforms Your Workflow"

Week 5-12 (Post-launch):
- Customer success stories
- ROI calculator tool
- Competitive comparison content
- Free trial promotion

5. SUCCESS METRICS

Primary KPIs:
- New customers: 5,000 (target)
- CAC: < $100
- Conversion rate: 10% (trial to paid)
- Campaign ROI: 5:1

Secondary KPIs:
- Website traffic: 200,000 visits
- Trial signups: 50,000
- Content downloads: 10,000
- Social media engagement: 50,000 interactions
- Press mentions: 20+ articles

6. TRACKING & OPTIMIZATION

Weekly Reviews:
- Monitor campaign performance
- Adjust bids and targeting
- A/B test ad creative and messaging
- Optimize landing pages

Tools:
- Google Analytics
- HubSpot (CRM & Marketing Automation)
- LinkedIn Campaign Manager
- Mixpanel (Product Analytics)

7. RISK MITIGATION

Risks:
- Competitor launches similar product
- Lower than expected conversion rates
- Ad costs higher than projected

Mitigation:
- Maintain marketing reserve fund ($50K)
- Diversify channels
- Focus on differentiation
- Build moat through content & partnerships
```

### Example 2: Sales Pitch Deck Outline

```
SALES PITCH: WorkFlow AI Enterprise
PROSPECT: TechCorp Inc. (500 employees)
DURATION: 30 minutes

SLIDE 1: OPENING
"Thank you for your time. Today, I'll show you how WorkFlow AI
can save your team 200+ hours per month while reducing costs by 30%."

SLIDE 2-3: DISCOVER (Understanding their needs)
Questions asked during discovery call:
- Current workflow challenges?
- Tools currently using?
- Team productivity metrics?
- Integration requirements?

Their Pain Points Identified:
✗ Manual data entry consuming 50 hours/week
✗ Disconnected tools (using 15 different apps)
✗ Lack of visibility into team productivity
✗ Difficulty scaling processes

SLIDE 4-5: INTRODUCE (Problem + Solution)
The Challenge:
"Like many growing companies, you're losing productivity to
manual processes and tool fragmentation."

Industry Data:
- Average company uses 110 apps (source: Okta)
- 69% of workers waste up to 60 min/day on admin tasks
- Context switching reduces productivity by 40%

Our Solution:
"WorkFlow AI unifies your tools and automates repetitive tasks,
giving your team back 30% of their time."

SLIDE 6-8: DEMONSTRATE (Features + Benefits)
Feature 1: AI-Powered Automation
- Benefit: Eliminate 80% of manual data entry
- Your ROI: 50 hours/week saved = $65K/year

Feature 2: Universal Integration
- Benefit: Connect all 15 tools in one platform
- Your ROI: Reduce tool sprawl, cut costs 20%

Feature 3: Real-time Analytics
- Benefit: Visibility into team productivity
- Your ROI: Data-driven decisions, improve OKR achievement

SLIDE 9: PROOF (Social Proof)
Similar Companies:
- TechStartup Inc. (450 employees)
  * Result: 35% productivity increase in 3 months
- FinanceHub (600 employees)
  * Result: $250K annual savings, 90% user adoption

Industry Recognition:
- Gartner Cool Vendor 2024
- G2 Leader in Productivity Software
- 4.8/5 stars (1,200+ reviews)

SLIDE 10: PROPOSAL (Custom Solution)
For TechCorp:
- Enterprise Plan: 500 users
- Custom integrations: Salesforce, Jira, Slack
- Dedicated success manager
- Priority support (24/7)
- Quarterly business reviews

Investment: $25,000/month ($60/user/month)

SLIDE 11: VALUE (ROI Calculation)
Current State:
- Manual work: 200 hours/month
- Cost: $10,000/month (@ $50/hour)
- Tool subscriptions: $15,000/month

With WorkFlow AI:
- Time saved: 140 hours/month (70% reduction)
- Cost saved: $7,000/month
- Tool consolidation savings: $5,000/month
- Total monthly savings: $12,000

Net ROI: ($12K savings - $25K cost) = Break-even after
optimization. True value in productivity and scalability.

SLIDE 12: IMPLEMENTATION (Timeline)
Week 1-2: Setup & Integration
Week 3-4: Team Training
Week 5-6: Pilot (50 users)
Week 7-8: Full Rollout
Week 9+: Optimization & Support

Success Metrics:
- Week 4: 80% user adoption
- Week 8: 30% time savings
- Week 12: Measurable ROI

SLIDE 13: CLOSE (Next Steps)
Option 1: Start with 90-day pilot (100 users)
- Investment: $6,000/month
- Full refund if not satisfied
- Upgrade to full plan anytime

Option 2: Full enterprise rollout
- Annual contract: 15% discount
- Implementation included
- Dedicated success team

Question: "Which option works best for TechCorp?"

SLIDE 14: THANK YOU
Contact Information:
[Your name, email, phone]

"I'm confident WorkFlow AI will transform your team's productivity.
What questions can I answer?"
```

### Task 1: Write a Marketing Campaign Brief (15분 | 300단어)

**상황**:
새로운 B2B SaaS 제품 "DataSync Pro"를 런칭합니다. 마케팅 팀에게 Q4 캠페인 브리프를 작성하세요.

**제품 정보**:
```
Product: DataSync Pro
Category: Data Integration Platform
Target: SMB to Mid-Market (50-500 employees)
Price: $199-$999/month
USP: Real-time data sync across 100+ apps, no coding required
Competition: Zapier, Integromat, Workato
```

**포함 사항**:
```
1. Campaign Objective (목표 + 숫자)
2. Target Audience (구체적으로)
3. Key Messages (3-5개)
4. Marketing Channels (우선순위)
5. Budget Allocation
6. Success Metrics
7. Timeline
```

### Task 2: Write a Sales Email (15분 | 200단어)

**상황**:
잠재 고객이 제품 데모를 본 후 구매 결정을 미루고 있습니다. Follow-up 이메일을 작성하세요.

**컨텍스트**:
```
- 데모 날짜: 1주일 전
- 반응: 긍정적이었으나 "예산 검토 필요"
- 경쟁사: 2개 업체 비교 중
- Decision deadline: 2주 후
```

**이메일 구조**:
```
Subject: [Compelling subject line]

Opening: Reference demo conversation

Value Reminder: Key benefits that resonated

Address Concerns: Budget, timing, competition

Social Proof: Brief case study or testimonial

Urgency/Incentive: Limited-time offer or risk of delay

Clear CTA: Specific next step

Closing: Open door for questions
```

## 4️⃣ Role-Play Scenarios (15분)

### Scenario 1: Product Positioning Workshop (5분)

**상황**: 마케팅 팀이 신제품 포지셔닝 논의

```
You (Marketing Director):
"Let's define our positioning for the new product. Who are we targeting?"

Team Member 1:
"I think we should go after enterprise. Bigger deals, higher value."

You:
[Discuss target market, competition, resources]

Team Member 2:
"What makes us different from competitors?"

You:
[Define USP and competitive advantages]

Team Member 3:
"What should our key message be?"

You:
[Craft positioning statement]
```

**핵심 표현**:
```
Positioning:
- "Our target market is..."
- "We're positioned as..."
- "Our differentiation is..."
- "The key benefit we offer is..."

Analyzing competition:
- "Competitor X focuses on... while we focus on..."
- "Our competitive advantage is..."
- "We're the only solution that..."
- "Where they lack is..."

Messaging:
- "Our core message should be..."
- "The value proposition is..."
- "The elevator pitch: [30 seconds]"
- "Our tagline could be..."
```

### Scenario 2: Campaign Performance Review (5분)

**상황**: CMO와 월간 캠페인 성과 리뷰

```
CMO:
"Let's review last month's campaign performance."

You (Marketing Manager):
"We achieved $450K in pipeline with $100K spend. That's a 4.5:1 ROI."

CMO:
"Good, but we targeted 5:1. What happened?"

You:
[Analyze underperformance, explain factors]

CMO:
"Which channels performed best?"

You:
[Present channel breakdown, insights]

CMO:
"What's your plan to improve ROI next month?"

You:
[Propose optimization strategies]
```

**핵심 표현**:
```
Presenting results:
- "We generated X leads with a conversion rate of Y%..."
- "Our CAC came in at $X, which is Y% above/below target..."
- "The campaign reached X people with Y% engagement..."
- "Top performing channel was X with Y ROI..."

Explaining variance:
- "We fell short due to..."
- "Market conditions changed..."
- "We learned that our audience..."
- "The underperformance was driven by..."

Proposing improvements:
- "To improve results, we'll..."
- "I recommend reallocating budget from X to Y..."
- "We should double down on..."
- "Testing showed that X performs better, so..."
```

### Scenario 3: Sales Objection Handling (5분)

**상황**: 잠재 고객이 구매를 망설임

```
Prospect:
"Your solution looks good, but it's expensive compared to competitors."

You (Sales Rep):
[Handle price objection]

Prospect:
"We're not sure if our team will adopt it."

You:
[Address adoption concerns]

Prospect:
"Can we start with a smaller commitment?"

You:
[Offer alternative, but maintain value]

Prospect:
"Let me think about it and get back to you."

You:
[Create urgency, secure next step]
```

**핵심 표현**:
```
Handling objections:
Price:
- "I understand budget is a concern. Let's look at the ROI..."
- "While we're not the cheapest, we provide X additional value..."
- "What's the cost of not solving this problem?"

Risk:
- "We mitigate that risk by offering..."
- "Our customers typically see adoption rates of 90%+..."
- "We provide full training and support..."

Timing:
- "I understand you need time to decide. What specifically do you need to evaluate?"
- "Many customers who waited found that the cost of delay was..."
- "If we start now, you'll see benefits by..."

Closing:
- "Based on what we've discussed, what makes the most sense?"
- "Should we move forward with Option A or Option B?"
- "What needs to happen for you to feel comfortable proceeding?"
```
