## 1️⃣ Industry-Specific Projects (200점, 80분)

### Project 1: Financial Analysis & Investor Pitch (50점, 20분)

**Scenario**:
당신은 CFO입니다. Series B 투자를 유치하기 위해 투자자에게 재무 상황과 성장 전망을 프레젠테이션해야 합니다.

**Company Data**:
```
Company: CloudSync Inc. (SaaS Platform)
Current Status:
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): $10M
- Growth: 120% YoY
- Burn rate: $500K/month
- Current cash: $4M
- Gross margin: 75%
- Customer count: 500 (up from 200 last year)
- LTV/CAC: 4:1
- Churn: 5% monthly

Funding Request: $15M Series B

Use of Funds:
- Sales & Marketing: $7M
- Product Development: $5M
- Operations & Infrastructure: $2M
- Working Capital: $1M

Projections (with funding):
- Year 1: ARR $25M
- Year 2: ARR $50M
- Year 3: ARR $100M, profitable
```

**Deliverables** (400-500단어):

**Part A: Executive Summary Email (200단어)**
```
TO: Lead Investor
RE: Series B Investment Opportunity

Write an email that:
- Hooks with key traction metrics
- Highlights market opportunity
- States funding ask and use
- Teases strong ROI potential
- Requests meeting
```

**Part B: 5-Minute Pitch Script (300단어)**
```
Structure:
1. Problem & Market (60초)
2. Solution & Traction (90초)
3. Business Model & Unit Economics (60초)
4. Funding & Use of Funds (30초)
5. Vision & Exit (30초)
6. Ask (30초)

Include:
- Specific numbers
- Growth story
- Competitive advantage
- Path to profitability
```

**Grading Criteria**:
- Financial accuracy & clarity (15점)
- Compelling narrative (15점)
- Appropriate tone for investors (10점)
- Professional format (10점)

## 2️⃣ Integrated Case Study (100점, 25분)

### Global Crisis Management Simulation

**Scenario**:
당신은 글로벌 소비재 회사의 VP of Communications입니다. 위기 상황이 발생했습니다.

**Crisis Details**:
```
Company: GlobalTech Products (electronics manufacturer)
Markets: US, EU, Asia
Employees: 10,000 globally

INCIDENT:
Your factory in Vietnam had a fire. No deaths, but 20 workers injured
(5 seriously). Factory produces 30% of global supply. Production will
be halted for 2 months minimum.

COMPLICATIONS:
- Safety violations found during investigation
- Local media reporting "unsafe working conditions"
- Labor rights groups calling for boycott on social media
- Stock price dropped 15%
- Major retailer (30% of sales) threatening to drop your products
- Holiday season is 3 months away (40% of annual sales)

STAKEHOLDERS DEMANDING RESPONSES:
- Injured workers and their families
- Other factory workers (afraid)
- Local Vietnamese government
- US/EU regulators
- Investors
- Retail partners
- Customers
- Media (international)
```

**Deliverables** (600단어 total):

### Task 1: Press Release (250단어, 30점)
```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Write a press release that:
- Acknowledges incident factually
- Expresses concern for injured workers
- Takes appropriate responsibility
- Outlines immediate actions
- Commits to investigation
- Addresses safety concerns
- Provides supply chain update
- Offers contact information

Balance: Accountability but not legal liability, empathy but not weakness
```

### Task 2: Internal Email to All Employees (200단어, 25점)
```
TO: All GlobalTech Employees
FROM: CEO

Write email that:
- Informs about incident (they'll hear anyway)
- Expresses values and commitment to safety
- Addresses job security concerns
- Outlines company response
- Asks for patience and dedication
- Provides internal communication channel
```

### Task 3: Cultural Consideration Memo (150단어, 25점)
```
TO: Crisis Response Team
FROM: You (VP Communications)
RE: Cultural Considerations for Vietnam Response

Write memo covering:
- Vietnamese cultural context for response
- Face-saving considerations
- Relationship with local government
- How to work with Vietnamese media
- Community relations approach
- Differences from Western crisis response
```

### Task 4: Stakeholder Priority Matrix (20점)
```
Rank stakeholders 1-8 and justify (one sentence each):
- Injured workers
- Vietnamese government
- US/EU regulators
- Investors
- Retail partners
- Factory employees
- Customers
- Media

Explain your prioritization rationale.
```

**Grading Criteria**:
- Crisis communication best practices (30점)
- Cultural awareness (25점)
- Stakeholder management (25점)
- Strategic thinking (20점)
