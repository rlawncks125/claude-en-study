## 1️⃣ Core Financial Vocabulary (30분)

### A. Financial Statements (재무제표)

#### Balance Sheet (B/S, 재무상태표)
```
Assets (자산)
├─ Current Assets (유동자산)
│  ├─ Cash and cash equivalents (현금 및 현금성자산)
│  ├─ Accounts receivable (매출채권)
│  ├─ Inventory (재고자산)
│  └─ Prepaid expenses (선급비용)
│
├─ Non-current Assets (비유동자산)
   ├─ Property, Plant & Equipment (PP&E, 유형자산)
   ├─ Intangible assets (무형자산)
   ├─ Goodwill (영업권)
   └─ Long-term investments (장기투자자산)

Liabilities (부채)
├─ Current Liabilities (유동부채)
│  ├─ Accounts payable (매입채무)
│  ├─ Short-term debt (단기차입금)
│  ├─ Accrued expenses (미지급비용)
│  └─ Deferred revenue (선수수익)
│
├─ Non-current Liabilities (비유동부채)
   ├─ Long-term debt (장기차입금)
   ├─ Bonds payable (사채)
   └─ Pension liabilities (퇴직급여충당부채)

Equity (자본)
├─ Share capital (자본금)
├─ Retained earnings (이익잉여금)
└─ Treasury stock (자기주식)
```

#### Income Statement (P&L, 손익계산서)
```
Revenue/Sales (매출액)
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS, 매출원가)
= Gross Profit (매출총이익)

- Operating Expenses (영업비용)
  ├─ Selling expenses (판매비)
  ├─ General & Administrative (일반관리비)
  └─ R&D expenses (연구개발비)
= Operating Income (EBIT, 영업이익)

+ Other Income (영업외수익)
- Other Expenses (영업외비용)
- Interest expense (이자비용)
= Earnings Before Tax (EBT, 세전이익)

- Income tax (법인세)
= Net Income (당기순이익)

Per Share Data:
- Earnings Per Share (EPS, 주당순이익)
- Dividends Per Share (DPS, 주당배당금)
```

#### Cash Flow Statement (현금흐름표)
```
Operating Activities (영업활동 현금흐름)
├─ Net income (당기순이익)
├─ Depreciation & Amortization (감가상각비)
├─ Changes in working capital (운전자본 변동)
└─ = Cash from operations (영업활동현금흐름)

Investing Activities (투자활동 현금흐름)
├─ Purchase of PP&E (유형자산 취득)
├─ Sale of investments (투자자산 매각)
└─ = Cash from investing (투자활동현금흐름)

Financing Activities (재무활동 현금흐름)
├─ Proceeds from debt (차입금 증가)
├─ Repayment of debt (차입금 상환)
├─ Dividends paid (배당금 지급)
└─ = Cash from financing (재무활동현금흐름)

Net change in cash (현금 순증감)
```

### B. Key Financial Ratios (재무비율)

#### Profitability Ratios (수익성 비율)
```
Gross Profit Margin = (Gross Profit / Revenue) × 100
- 매출총이익률

Operating Profit Margin = (Operating Income / Revenue) × 100
- 영업이익률

Net Profit Margin = (Net Income / Revenue) × 100
- 순이익률

Return on Assets (ROA) = (Net Income / Total Assets) × 100
- 총자산이익률

Return on Equity (ROE) = (Net Income / Shareholders' Equity) × 100
- 자기자본이익률
```

#### Liquidity Ratios (유동성 비율)
```
Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities
- 유동비율 (2.0 이상이 양호)

Quick Ratio = (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities
- 당좌비율 (1.0 이상이 양호)

Cash Ratio = Cash / Current Liabilities
- 현금비율
```

#### Leverage Ratios (레버리지 비율)
```
Debt-to-Equity Ratio = Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity
- 부채비율

Debt-to-Assets Ratio = Total Debt / Total Assets
- 부채자산비율

Interest Coverage Ratio = EBIT / Interest Expense
- 이자보상배율 (높을수록 양호)
```

### C. Investment & Funding Vocabulary (투자 용어)

```
Funding Types:
- Seed funding (시드 투자)
- Series A/B/C funding (시리즈 투자)
- Venture capital (VC, 벤처캐피탈)
- Private equity (PE, 사모펀드)
- Angel investor (엔젤 투자자)
- IPO (Initial Public Offering, 기업공개)

Investment Terms:
- Valuation (기업가치 평가)
- Pre-money valuation (투자 전 가치)
- Post-money valuation (투자 후 가치)
- Equity stake (지분율)
- Dilution (지분 희석)
- Term sheet (투자계약서)
- Due diligence (실사)
- Cap table (자본구조표)

Returns:
- ROI (Return on Investment, 투자수익률)
- IRR (Internal Rate of Return, 내부수익률)
- NPV (Net Present Value, 순현재가치)
- Payback period (투자회수기간)
- Break-even point (손익분기점)
```

## 2️⃣ Reading Financial Documents (25분)

### Example 1: Quarterly Financial Report

```
TechNova Corporation
Q2 2024 Financial Results

SUMMARY:
Revenue for Q2 2024 increased 25% year-over-year to $50 million,
driven by strong demand in our enterprise software segment.

KEY METRICS:
- Revenue: $50M (↑25% YoY)
- Gross Profit: $35M (70% margin)
- Operating Income: $10M (20% margin)
- Net Income: $7.5M (15% margin)
- EPS: $0.75
- Cash on hand: $25M

BALANCE SHEET HIGHLIGHTS:
Total assets grew to $150M, with current assets of $80M and
non-current assets of $70M. Our current ratio stands at 2.5,
indicating strong liquidity.

Total liabilities decreased to $50M as we paid down $5M in
short-term debt. Shareholders' equity increased to $100M.

CASH FLOW:
Operating cash flow was $12M, up from $8M in Q1. We invested
$3M in new equipment and paid $2M in dividends.

OUTLOOK:
We expect Q3 revenue to grow 20-25% YoY, with continued
improvement in operating margins.
```

**분석 연습**:
```
Q1: What was the revenue growth rate?
A: 25% year-over-year

Q2: How profitable is the company?
A: Gross margin 70%, operating margin 20%, net margin 15%
   - All strong profitability indicators

Q3: Is the company's liquidity healthy?
A: Yes, current ratio of 2.5 (above 2.0 benchmark)
   and $25M cash on hand

Q4: What's the company's debt situation?
A: Improving - paid down $5M debt, total liabilities $50M
   vs $100M equity (0.5 debt-to-equity ratio - healthy)
```

### Example 2: Investment Proposal Summary

```
CONFIDENTIAL
Series B Funding Proposal
GreenTech Solutions Inc.

FUNDING REQUEST: $15 million

USE OF FUNDS:
- Product development: $6M (40%)

✓ Large addressable market ($5B)
✓ Proprietary technology
✓ 150% YoY growth
✓ 95% customer retention rate
```

## 3️⃣ Writing Financial Documents (30분)

### Task 1: Write a Budget Report (15분 | 300단어)

**상황**:
당신은 마케팅 부서장입니다. 2024년 Q4 예산을 CFO에게 요청하는 보고서를 작성하세요.

**포함 사항**:
```
1. Executive Summary
2. Q4 Budget Breakdown
   - Digital marketing: $200K
   - Events & conferences: $150K
   - Content creation: $100K
   - Tools & software: $50K
   - Total: $500K
3. Expected ROI
4. Comparison with Q3
5. Justification
```

**템플릿**:
```
TO: Chief Financial Officer
FROM: Marketing Director
DATE: [Date]
SUBJECT: Q4 2024 Marketing Budget Request

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
[2-3 sentences summary]

Q4 BUDGET REQUEST
We are requesting a total marketing budget of $500,000 for Q4 2024,
representing a [X%] increase from Q3.

Budget Breakdown:
• Digital Marketing: $200,000 (40%)
  - [details]
• Events & Conferences: $150,000 (30%)
  - [details]
• Content Creation: $100,000 (20%)
  - [details]
• Marketing Tools & Software: $50,000 (10%)
  - [details]

EXPECTED RETURN ON INVESTMENT
Based on Q3 performance, we project:
- Lead generation: [number]
- Conversion rate: [percentage]
- Expected revenue: $[amount]
- ROI: [X:1 ratio]

Q3 COMPARISON
[Compare with Q3 budget and results]

JUSTIFICATION
[Why this budget is necessary]

We believe this investment will [expected outcomes].

Please let me know if you need additional information.

Best regards,
[Your name]
```

### Task 2: Financial Performance Summary Email (15분 | 200단어)

**상황**:
당신은 CEO입니다. 임원진에게 월간 재무 성과를 요약하여 전달하세요.

**데이터**:
```
- 월 매출: $5M (목표 대비 +10%)
- 매출총이익률: 68%
- 영업비용: $2.5M (예산 대비 -5%)
- 당기순이익: $1M
- 현금: $10M
- 주요 성과: 신규 계약 3건 ($2M)
- 우려사항: 매출채권 회수 지연
```

**작성 요령**:
```
Subject: [Month] Financial Performance Summary

Opening: Brief positive or neutral overview

Key Metrics: Present data clearly
- Use bullet points
- Include percentages and comparisons
- Highlight achievements

Concerns: Address issues honestly

Action Items: What needs to be done

Closing: Forward-looking statement
```

## 4️⃣ Role-Play Scenarios (15분)

### Scenario 1: Budget Discussion (5분)

**상황**: CFO와 예산 승인 미팅

```
You (Department Head):
"Thank you for meeting with me. I'd like to discuss our Q4 budget proposal."

CFO:
"I've reviewed your proposal. The $500K request is 25% higher than Q3.
Can you justify this increase?"

You:
[Explain the ROI, market conditions, and strategic importance]

CFO:
"I understand, but we need to cut costs. Can we reduce it to $400K?"

You:
[Negotiate - what can be reduced, what's essential]

CFO:
"What guarantees can you give on the ROI?"

You:
[Provide data, past performance, risk mitigation]
```

**핵심 표현**:
```
Justifying:
- "Based on our Q3 performance, we achieved an ROI of 5:1..."
- "This investment is critical for..."
- "The data shows that..."
- "Without this budget, we risk..."

Negotiating:
- "I understand the concern. We could potentially reduce..."
- "However, cutting [X] would impact [Y]..."
- "The minimum viable budget would be..."
- "We're flexible on [X] but [Y] is essential..."

Providing assurance:
- "We have a proven track record of..."
- "Our KPIs show..."
- "We'll provide monthly updates on..."
- "If we don't meet targets, we'll adjust accordingly..."
```

### Scenario 2: Investment Pitch (5분)

**상황**: VC에게 투자 유치 피칭

```
You (CEO):
"Thank you for the opportunity to present. We're seeking $10M in Series A funding."

Investor:
"Walk me through your financials."

You:
[Present revenue, growth rate, unit economics]

Investor:
"You're pre-revenue. When will you break even?"

You:
[Explain path to profitability]

Investor:
"What's your burn rate?"

You:
[Explain monthly expenses and runway]

Investor:
"Why do you need $10M specifically?"

You:
[Break down use of funds]
```

**핵심 표현**:
```
Presenting financials:
- "Our current monthly recurring revenue (MRR) is..."
- "We're growing at X% month-over-month..."
- "Our customer acquisition cost (CAC) is $X with lifetime value (LTV) of $Y..."
- "Our gross margins are..."

Explaining burn rate:
- "Our current monthly burn rate is $X..."
- "With this funding, we'll have an 18-month runway..."
- "We expect to reach profitability by..."

Use of funds:
- "We'll allocate 40% to product development..."
- "This will enable us to..."
- "The investment will help us achieve..."
```

### Scenario 3: Financial Review Meeting (5분)

**상황**: 이사회에 분기 실적 보고

```
You (CFO):
"Let me present our Q2 financial results."

Board Member 1:
"Revenue is up but margins are down. Why?"

You:
[Explain cost structure changes]

Board Member 2:
"What's the outlook for Q3?"

You:
[Provide forecast with assumptions]

Board Member 3:
"Cash position seems tight. Do we need to raise capital?"

You:
[Assess liquidity and options]
```

**핵심 표현**:
```
Presenting results:
- "Revenue came in at $X, representing Y% growth..."
- "Operating expenses increased due to..."
- "Our EBITDA margin was X%, compared to Y% last quarter..."

Addressing concerns:
- "The margin compression was driven by..."
- "This is a temporary situation due to..."
- "We expect margins to improve because..."

Providing outlook:
- "Looking ahead to Q3, we anticipate..."
- "Our guidance is..."
- "Key assumptions include..."
- "Upside scenarios include... downside risks are..."
```
