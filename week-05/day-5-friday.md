## 1️⃣ Crisis Communication Fundamentals (25분)

### A. Types of Business Crises

```
Product/Service Crises:
- Product recall (제품 리콜)
- Service outage (서비스 중단)
- Quality issues (품질 문제)
- Safety concerns (안전 문제)
- Data breach (데이터 유출)
- Security incident (보안 사고)

Organizational Crises:
- Leadership scandal (리더십 스캔들)
- Financial misconduct (재무 부정)
- Bankruptcy / Insolvency (파산)
- Layoffs / Restructuring (정리해고 / 구조조정)
- Merger failure (합병 실패)
- Hostile takeover (적대적 인수)

Operational Crises:
- Workplace accident (작업장 사고)
- Natural disaster impact (자연재해)
- Supply chain disruption (공급망 중단)
- Factory fire/explosion (공장 화재/폭발)
- IT system failure (IT 시스템 장애)

Reputational Crises:
- Negative media coverage (부정적 언론 보도)
- Social media backlash (소셜 미디어 반발)
- Customer complaints going viral (바이럴 고객 불만)
- Employee misconduct (직원 부적절한 행동)
- Discrimination lawsuit (차별 소송)
- Environmental damage (환경 피해)
```

### B. Crisis Communication Framework

#### The 5Cs of Crisis Communication
```
1. Concern (관심)
   - Show empathy and care
   - "We understand the impact..."
   - "We take this matter seriously..."

2. Commitment (약속)
   - Promise to address the issue
   - "We are committed to..."
   - "We will not rest until..."

3. Competence (역량)
   - Show you have capability
   - "Our team of experts is..."
   - "We have implemented..."

4. Clarity (명확성)
   - Be transparent and clear
   - "Here are the facts..."
   - "What we know so far..."

5. Confidence (확신)
   - Project confidence (not arrogance)
   - "We are confident that..."
   - "We have a plan to..."
```

#### Crisis Response Timeline
```
Phase 1: Immediate Response (First 24 hours)
- Assess situation
- Activate crisis team
- Gather facts
- Issue initial statement
- "We are aware and investigating..."

Phase 2: Ongoing Updates (Days 2-7)
- Provide regular updates
- Share findings
- Outline action plan
- "Here's what we've learned..."

Phase 3: Resolution (Week 2-4)
- Announce solution
- Implement fixes
- Restore confidence
- "The issue has been resolved..."

Phase 4: Recovery (Month 2+)
- Prevent recurrence
- Rebuild reputation
- Share lessons learned
- "Here's how we've improved..."
```

### C. Key Stakeholders & Messages

```
Stakeholder Groups:

1. Customers
   - Primary concern: Safety, service restoration
   - Message: Apologize, reassure, provide updates
   - Channel: Email, website, social media

2. Employees
   - Primary concern: Job security, company stability
   - Message: Transparent, reassuring, involve them
   - Channel: All-hands meeting, internal email

3. Investors/Shareholders
   - Primary concern: Financial impact, leadership
   - Message: Honest, data-driven, action plan
   - Channel: Investor call, SEC filing, press release

4. Media/Press
   - Primary concern: Facts, accountability, story
   - Message: Factual, available, consistent
   - Channel: Press release, press conference, interviews

5. Regulators/Government
   - Primary concern: Compliance, public safety
   - Message: Cooperative, compliant, accountable
   - Channel: Official reports, meetings

6. Partners/Suppliers
   - Primary concern: Business continuity
   - Message: Impact on partnership, next steps
   - Channel: Direct communication, meetings

7. Community/Public
   - Primary concern: Broader impact, values
   - Message: Social responsibility, commitment
   - Channel: Public statement, community meetings
```

### D. Crisis Communication Vocabulary

```
Severity Levels:
- Minor incident (경미한 사건)
- Serious situation (심각한 상황)
- Major crisis (주요 위기)
- Catastrophic event (재앙적 사건)

Response Actions:
- Investigation (조사)
- Root cause analysis (근본 원인 분석)
- Containment (격리/억제)
- Mitigation (완화)
- Remediation (시정)
- Compensation (보상)
- Prevention (예방)

Communication Terms:
- Official statement (공식 성명)
- Press release (보도 자료)
- Press conference (기자회견)
- Media inquiry (언론 문의)
- Spokesperson (대변인)
- No comment (논평 불가)
- Off the record (비공식)
- On background (배경 설명)

Emotional Language:
- Apologize (사과하다)
- Regret (유감)
- Sympathize (공감하다)
- Take responsibility (책임을 지다)
- Make amends (보상하다)
- Rebuild trust (신뢰 회복)
```

### Example 1: Data Breach Press Release

```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

TechNova Corporation Announces Data Security Incident
Company Takes Swift Action to Protect Customer Data

SAN FRANCISCO, CA - June 15, 2024 - TechNova Corporation today announced
that it has identified and contained a data security incident that may
have affected approximately 50,000 customer accounts.

INCIDENT SUMMARY:
On June 10, 2024, our security team detected unusual activity in one of
our database systems. We immediately launched an investigation with the
help of leading cybersecurity experts. The investigation revealed that
an unauthorized party gained access to a database containing customer
names, email addresses, and encrypted passwords. No financial information,
such as credit card numbers or bank account details, was compromised.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS TAKEN:
• Contained the security incident within 4 hours of detection
• Engaged top cybersecurity firm to conduct forensic investigation
• Notified law enforcement and relevant regulatory authorities
• Implemented additional security measures to prevent future incidents
• Reset passwords for all potentially affected accounts
• Established dedicated support hotline for affected customers

CUSTOMER PROTECTION:
We have sent individual notifications to all potentially affected customers.
As a precautionary measure, we have:
• Reset passwords for affected accounts (customers will need to create
  a new password)
• Enabled multi-factor authentication for all accounts
• Offering 12 months of free credit monitoring services

ONGOING INVESTIGATION:
We are conducting a comprehensive review of our security infrastructure
and working closely with law enforcement. While our investigation is
ongoing, we have found no evidence that the compromised information has
been misused.

COMMITMENT TO SECURITY:
The security and privacy of our customers' data is our highest priority.
We deeply regret this incident and apologize for any concern it may cause.
We are committed to maintaining the trust our customers place in us.

ENHANCED SECURITY MEASURES:
In response to this incident, we are implementing:
• Advanced threat detection systems
• Enhanced encryption protocols
• More frequent security audits
• Additional cybersecurity personnel
• Improved access controls

Customers with questions can:

We will provide updates as our investigation progresses.

serving over 10,000 customers worldwide.
```

### Example 2: CEO Internal Email During Layoffs

```
TO: All Employees
FROM: Michael Chen, CEO
DATE: September 1, 2024
SUBJECT: Important Company Update

Dear Team,

I'm writing to share some difficult news. Today, we made the painful
decision to reduce our workforce by 15%, affecting approximately 150
of our colleagues.

This is the hardest decision I've had to make as CEO, and I want to
explain why we're taking this action and how we're supporting those
affected.

WHY THIS HAPPENED:
Over the past year, we've faced significant headwinds:
• Market downturn reduced customer spending by 30%
• Revenue declined 20% year-over-year
• Slower-than-expected product adoption
• Increased competition putting pressure on margins

We explored every alternative—cutting discretionary spending, pausing
hiring, reducing executive compensation—but these measures were not
sufficient to ensure our long-term viability.

WHO IS AFFECTED:
The reductions span all departments and levels. We based decisions on
business needs, not individual performance. Many talented people are
leaving through no fault of their own.

Affected employees are being notified individually today by their
managers. If you haven't heard from your manager by 2pm, you are not
affected by this change.

HOW WE'RE SUPPORTING DEPARTING COLLEAGUES:
We're providing:
• Minimum 8 weeks severance (more for longer tenure)
• 6 months of health insurance continuation
• Accelerated equity vesting (for those close to cliff)
• Career transition services and job search support
• Positive references
• Optional alumni network participation

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT:
For those staying:
• We're not planning additional reductions
• Your roles remain secure as we execute our plan
• Some teams will be restructured (details next week)
• We'll need everyone's best effort to rebuild

The path forward won't be easy, but we're confident in our strategy:
• Focus on our core product line
• Double down on our strongest customer segments
• Reduce operating costs by 25%
• Return to profitability by Q2 2025

TODAY'S PRIORITIES:
• Support your departing colleagues with grace and respect
• No questions about severance details (HR will handle privately)
• All-hands meeting at 4pm (attendance optional but encouraged)
• Take care of yourselves—this is hard for everyone

I TAKE FULL RESPONSIBILITY:
These are my decisions. We grew too fast without sufficient financial
cushion. I overestimated market demand. I should have acted sooner.
I'm deeply sorry to everyone affected.

To those leaving: Thank you for your contributions. You've helped build
something meaningful, and I'm grateful for your work. I'm personally
committed to helping you find your next opportunity.

To those staying: I need your continued dedication. We have difficult
work ahead, but I believe in our team and our mission.

RESOURCES:
• FAQ: [internal link]
• HR support: hr@company.com
• Confidential counseling: [EAP info]
• My door (virtual or physical) is open

This is a dark day, but not the end of our story. We'll emerge stronger.

With respect and gratitude,
Michael

---
Michael Chen
Chief Executive Officer
```

## 4️⃣ Crisis Scenarios & Responses (20분)

### Scenario 1: Press Conference Q&A (7분)

**상황**: 제품 리콜 후 CEO 기자회견

```
Reporter 1:
"How did this contamination happen? Was it negligence?"

CEO (You):
[Acknowledge, explain factually, take responsibility]

Reporter 2:
"How many people have been affected? Can you guarantee no deaths?"

You:
[Provide known facts, avoid speculation]

Reporter 3:
"Will heads roll? Who's responsible?"

You:
[Address accountability without scapegoating]

Reporter 4:
"Why should customers trust your products now?"

You:
[Rebuild confidence, cite safety measures]

Reporter 5:
"What's the financial impact? Will you survive this?"

You:
[Address business impact honestly]
```

**핵심 표현**:
```
Acknowledging:
- "We take full responsibility for..."
- "This should never have happened..."
- "We deeply regret..."
- "We understand your concerns..."

Explaining:
- "Based on our investigation, we found that..."
- "The root cause was..."
- "Here's what we know so far..."
- "We're still investigating [X], and we'll share updates..."

Taking Action:
- "We've immediately implemented..."
- "Steps we're taking include..."
- "We're working around the clock to..."
- "Our top priority is..."

Rebuilding Trust:
- "Our 50-year track record shows..."
- "We've invested $X million in safety..."
- "Independent audits confirm..."
- "We're committed to transparency going forward..."

Avoiding Pitfalls:
❌ "No comment" (sounds guilty)
❌ "It's not our fault" (deflecting responsibility)
❌ "This is no big deal" (dismissive)
❌ "We're the victims here" (tone-deaf)
✓ "We're investigating and will share what we learn"
✓ "We take full responsibility"
✓ "We understand the seriousness"
✓ "Here's how we're fixing this"
```

### Scenario 3: Customer Service Crisis Call (6분)

**상황**: 대규모 서비스 장애 중 화난 고객 응대

```
Customer (angry):
"I've been down for 6 hours! My business is losing money! This is unacceptable!"

You (Customer Service Manager):
[Acknowledge frustration, empathize]

Customer:
"What are you going to do about my losses? I want compensation!"

You:
[Explain what you can offer, avoid over-promising]

Customer:
"This isn't enough! I'm switching to your competitor!"

You:
[Recovery attempt, but respect their decision]

"Fine. But this better not happen again."

[Rebuild confidence, ensure follow-through]

```

```
- "This is not the experience we want to provide..."
- "We've let you down, and I'm sorry..."

Solution:
- "Here's what I can do for you right now..."
- "I'm authorized to offer..."
- "Let me make this right by..."

Follow-up:
- "I'm personally going to follow up with you..."
- "You have my direct contact..."
- "I'll check in tomorrow to ensure everything is working..."
```

## 📌 Crisis Communication Best Practices

### DO's (해야 할 것):
```
✓ Respond quickly (within 1-2 hours if possible)
✓ Tell the truth, even if it's painful
✓ Show empathy and concern
✓ Take responsibility
✓ Provide specific actions
✓ Keep stakeholders updated
✓ Have one spokesperson for consistency
✓ Prepare Q&A in advance
✓ Monitor social media
✓ Document everything
```

### DON'Ts (하지 말아야 할 것):
```
✗ Say "no comment" (sounds guilty)
✗ Speculate or guess
✗ Blame others
✗ Minimize the issue
✗ Make promises you can't keep
✗ Over-explain or make excuses
✗ Disappear or go silent
✗ Argue with critics publicly
✗ Use legal jargon excessively
✗ Seem defensive or arrogant
```
