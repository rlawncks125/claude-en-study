## 1️⃣ Core HR & Legal Vocabulary (30분)

### A. Recruitment & Hiring (채용)

#### Recruitment Process (채용 프로세스)
```
1. Workforce Planning (인력 계획)
   - Headcount (인원수)
   - Job requisition (채용 요청)
   - Budget approval (예산 승인)

2. Job Posting (채용 공고)
   - Job description (JD, 직무 기술서)
   - Job requirements (자격 요건)
   - Qualifications (학력/경력)
   - Responsibilities (책임사항)
   - Compensation range (급여 범위)

3. Sourcing (소싱)
   - Active candidates (적극적 지원자)
   - Passive candidates (잠재적 지원자)
   - Talent pipeline (인재 파이프라인)
   - Employee referral (직원 추천)
   - Headhunter / Recruiter (헤드헌터)

4. Screening (스크리닝)
   - Resume screening (이력서 스크리닝)
   - Phone screening (전화 스크리닝)
   - Background check (신원 조회)
   - Reference check (추천인 확인)

5. Interview Process (면접 과정)
   - Screening interview (1차 면접)
   - Technical interview (기술 면접)
   - Behavioral interview (행동 면접)
   - Panel interview (패널 면접)
   - Final interview (최종 면접)

6. Selection & Offer (선발 및 제안)
   - Shortlist (최종 후보자 명단)
   - Job offer (채용 제안)
   - Offer letter (채용 제안서)
   - Compensation package (보상 패키지)
     * Base salary (기본급)
     * Bonus (보너스)
     * Stock options (스톡옵션)
     * Benefits (복리후생)
   - Negotiation (협상)
   - Acceptance (수락)
   - Rejection (거절)

7. Onboarding (온보딩)
   - Orientation (오리엔테이션)
   - Training (교육)
   - Probation period (수습 기간)
   - 30-60-90 day plan (30-60-90일 계획)
```

#### Interview Questions Types
```
Behavioral Questions:
- "Tell me about a time when..."
- "Give me an example of..."
- "Describe a situation where..."

Situational Questions:
- "What would you do if...?"
- "How would you handle...?"

Technical Questions:
- "Explain how you would..."
- "Walk me through your approach to..."

Cultural Fit:
- "Why do you want to work here?"
- "What are your career goals?"
- "How do you handle conflict?"
```

### B. Employment & Compensation (고용 및 보상)

#### Employment Terms (고용 용어)
```
Employment Types:
- Full-time employee (정규직)
- Part-time employee (파트타임)
- Contract worker (계약직)
- Temporary worker (임시직)
- Freelancer / Consultant (프리랜서 / 컨설턴트)
- Intern (인턴)

Work Arrangements:
- Remote work (원격 근무)
- Hybrid work (하이브리드)
- Flexible hours (유연 근무)
- Shift work (교대 근무)

Compensation Structure:
- Fixed salary (고정급)
- Hourly wage (시급)
- Commission (커미션)
- Performance bonus (성과급)
- Equity compensation (주식 보상)
- Severance package (퇴직금)

Benefits:
- Health insurance (건강 보험)
- Dental insurance (치과 보험)
- Life insurance (생명 보험)
- Retirement plan / 401(k) (퇴직 연금)
- Paid time off (PTO, 유급 휴가)
- Sick leave (병가)
- Parental leave (육아 휴직)
- Professional development (전문성 개발)
- Wellness programs (웰니스 프로그램)
```

### C. Performance Management (성과 관리)

#### Performance Review Process
```
Performance Cycle:
1. Goal Setting (목표 설정)
   - OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)
   - KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)
   - SMART goals (구체적, 측정가능, 달성가능, 관련성, 기한)

2. Ongoing Feedback (지속적 피드백)
   - One-on-ones (1:1 미팅)
   - Check-ins (체크인)
   - Real-time feedback (실시간 피드백)

3. Performance Review (성과 평가)
   - Self-assessment (자기 평가)
   - Manager assessment (관리자 평가)
   - 360-degree feedback (360도 피드백)
   - Peer review (동료 평가)

4. Rating Scale (평가 등급)
   - Exceeds expectations (기대 초과)
   - Meets expectations (기대 충족)
   - Needs improvement (개선 필요)
   - Unsatisfactory (불만족)

5. Development Plan (개발 계획)
   - Strengths (강점)
   - Areas for improvement (개선 영역)
   - Development goals (개발 목표)
   - Action plan (실행 계획)

6. Compensation Review (보상 검토)
   - Merit increase / Raise (성과급 인상)
   - Promotion (승진)
   - Bonus (보너스)
```

#### Feedback Language
```
Positive Feedback:
- "You consistently deliver high-quality work..."
- "Your leadership on [project] was exceptional..."
- "You've shown strong growth in..."
- "I appreciate your [specific behavior]..."

Constructive Feedback:
- "I've noticed that [specific behavior]..."
- "One area for development is..."
- "Moving forward, I'd like to see..."
- "Let's work together on improving..."

Growth-Oriented:
- "You have the potential to..."
- "To reach the next level, focus on..."
- "A development opportunity is..."
```

### D. Legal & Compliance (법률 및 규정 준수)

#### Employment Law Terms
```
Key Regulations:
- Employment contract (고용 계약)
- Non-disclosure agreement (NDA, 비밀유지 계약)
- Non-compete agreement (경업 금지 계약)
- Intellectual property (IP, 지적 재산권)
- Termination clause (해지 조항)

Workplace Rights:
- Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO, 고용 평등 기회)
- Anti-discrimination (차별 금지)
- Harassment (괴롭힘)
- Sexual harassment (성희롱)
- Hostile work environment (적대적 근무 환경)
- Retaliation (보복)
- Whistleblower protection (내부 고발자 보호)

Leave & Accommodations:
- FMLA (Family and Medical Leave Act)
- ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act)
- Reasonable accommodation (합리적 편의)
- Protected leave (보호 휴가)

Termination:
- Resignation (사직)
- Voluntary termination (자발적 퇴사)
- Involuntary termination (비자발적 해고)
- Layoff (정리해고)
- Dismissal for cause (정당한 사유 해고)
- Wrongful termination (부당 해고)
- Exit interview (퇴사 면담)
```

#### Contract Terms
```
Standard Clauses:
- Parties (계약 당사자)
- Effective date (발효일)
- Term / Duration (기간)
- Scope of work (업무 범위)
- Deliverables (산출물)
- Payment terms (지불 조건)
- Confidentiality (기밀 유지)
- Indemnification (면책)
- Liability (책임)
- Dispute resolution (분쟁 해결)
- Governing law (준거법)
- Termination (해지)
- Force majeure (불가항력)
- Amendment (수정)
- Entire agreement (완전 합의)
- Signature (서명)

Common Legal Phrases:
- "Hereinafter referred to as..." (이하 ~로 지칭함)
- "Whereas..." (전제)
- "Provided that..." (~한다는 조건으로)
- "In accordance with..." (~에 따라)
- "Subject to..." (~을 조건으로)
- "Without prejudice to..." (~에 영향을 주지 않고)
```

Preferred:
• MBA or advanced degree
• Technical background or engineering experience
• Experience in enterprise software industry
• Familiarity with data analytics tools (Mixpanel, Amplitude)

COMPENSATION & BENEFITS:
• Base salary: $140,000 - $180,000 (commensurate with experience)

Please submit your resume and cover letter to careers@company.com

We do not discriminate on the basis of race, religion, color, national origin,
gender, sexual orientation, age, marital status, veteran status, or disability.

## 4️⃣ Role-Play Scenarios (15분)

### Scenario 1: Performance Review Discussion (5분)

**상황**: 매니저가 팀원과 연간 성과 평가 미팅

```
Manager (You):
"Thank you for meeting. Let's discuss your performance this year.
Overall, you've done excellent work."

Employee:
"Thank you. I feel like I've grown a lot this year."

You:
[Discuss specific accomplishments, provide positive feedback]

Employee:
"What about the Q3 project delay? I know that wasn't ideal."

You:
[Address constructively, focus on learning]

Employee:
"What are my chances for promotion this year?"

You:
[Set realistic expectations, outline path forward]

Employee:
"I was hoping for a larger raise given my performance."

You:
[Explain compensation decision, discuss other opportunities]
```

**핵심 표현**:
```
Opening:
- "Let's start by reviewing your accomplishments..."
- "I want to recognize your strong performance in..."
- "You've made significant contributions to..."

Positive Feedback:
- "You consistently demonstrate..."
- "Your work on [X] exceeded expectations by..."
- "I've been particularly impressed with..."
- "You've shown strong growth in..."

Constructive Feedback:
- "One area I'd like to see development in is..."
- "Moving forward, let's focus on..."
- "To reach the next level, you'll need to..."
- "Let's work together on improving..."

Discussing Compensation:
- "Based on your performance and market benchmarks..."
- "The merit increase reflects..."
- "While I recognize your strong performance, the budget..."
- "Let's discuss your career path and future compensation..."
```

### Scenario 2: Difficult Termination Conversation (5분)

**상황**: HR Manager가 성과 미달 직원 해고

```
You (HR + Manager):
"Thank you for coming. This is a difficult conversation.
We've decided to terminate your employment, effective today."

Employee:
"What? Why? I didn't see this coming."

You:
[Explain clearly, reference previous warnings]

Employee:
"Can I have another chance? I'll improve."

You:
[Firm but empathetic, explain decision is final]

Employee:
"What about severance? References?"

You:
[Explain severance package, transition support]

Employee (upset):
"This isn't fair! I'm going to sue!"

You:
[Remain calm, professional, document conversation]
```

**핵심 표현**:
```
Opening:
- "I need to inform you that we've made the difficult decision to..."
- "Today will be your last day with the company..."
- "We're ending your employment, effective immediately..."

Explaining Reasons:
- "As we've discussed in previous meetings..."
- "Despite multiple performance discussions and warnings..."
- "The decision is based on [specific reasons]..."
- "We've provided support and improvement plans, but..."

Discussing Severance:
- "We're offering a severance package of [X weeks]..."
- "You'll receive [benefits continuation details]..."
- "Your final paycheck will include..."
- "We'll provide a neutral reference confirming dates of employment..."

Maintaining Professionalism:
- "I understand this is difficult news..."
- "Let's focus on the transition process..."
- "I need to collect [company property]..."
- "HR will walk you through the exit process..."
```
