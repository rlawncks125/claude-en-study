# 일반동사 과거형 (Simple Past Tense)

## 📖 개념

과거형은 이미 끝난 행동이나 상태를 표현할 때 사용합니다.

---

## 📊 과거형 형태

### 규칙 동사 (-ed)

| 동사 끝 | 변화 | 예시 |
|---------|------|------|
| 일반 | +ed | work → worked, start → started |
| -e | +d | live → lived, use → used |
| 자음+y | y→ied | study → studied, try → tried |
| 모음+y | +ed | play → played, enjoy → enjoyed |
| 단모음+단자음 | 자음 중복+ed | stop → stopped, plan → planned |

| 현재형 | 과거형 | 뜻 |
|--------|--------|-----|
| go | went | 가다 |

| make | made | 만들다 |
| get | got | 얻다 |
| see | saw | 보다 |
| come | came | 오다 |
| take | took | 가져가다 |

## 💡 사용법

### 1. 완료된 과거 행동
```
I worked at Google last year.
(작년에 구글에서 일했다 - 지금은 아님)

She finished the project yesterday.
(어제 프로젝트를 끝냈다)

They launched the product in 2022.
(2022년에 제품을 출시했다)
```

### 2. 과거의 습관
```
I studied English every day when I was in college.
(대학 때 매일 영어 공부했다)

He always arrived early at the office.
(그는 항상 일찍 출근했다)
```

### 3. 과거의 상태
```
I was a student.
(학생이었다)

The meeting was productive.
(미팅이 생산적이었다)
```

## 📝 문장 구조

### 평서문
```
주어 + 동사 과거형 + 목적어/보어

I worked on the project.
She completed the report.
They attended the conference.
```

### 부정문
```
주어 + didn't + 동사원형

I didn't work yesterday.
She didn't complete the report.
They didn't attend the conference.
```

**주의:** 과거형이 아닌 원형!
- ❌ I didn't worked
- ✅ I didn't work

### 의문문
```
Did + 주어 + 동사원형?

Did you work yesterday?
Did she complete the report?
Did they attend the conference?
```

### 의문문 답변
```
Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.
Yes, they did. / No, they didn't.
```

---

## 🕐 시간 표현

과거형과 자주 쓰이는 시간 표현:

```
yesterday (어제)
last week/month/year (지난 주/월/년)
~ ago (~ 전에)
  - two days ago (이틀 전)
  - a week ago (일주일 전)
in 2022 (2022년에)
when I was ~ (~였을 때)
```

**예문:**
```
I joined the company two years ago.
We had a meeting yesterday.
She worked in New York last year.
I graduated in 2020.
```

## ⚠️ 주의사항

### 1. 불규칙 동사 암기 필수
```
❌ I goed to the office.
✅ I went to the office.

❌ She maked a presentation.
✅ She made a presentation.
```

### 2. 부정문/의문문에서 동사원형
```
❌ Did you went there?
✅ Did you go there?

❌ I didn't saw him.
✅ I didn't see him.
```

### 3. Be동사 과거형 (was/were)
```
I/He/She/It was
You/We/They were

I was busy yesterday.
They were at the meeting.
```

## 🎯 실전 예문

### 업무 관련
```
I worked on the marketing campaign last month.
She managed three projects simultaneously.
We launched the new feature yesterday.
The team completed the sprint on time.
I had a meeting with the client this morning.
```

### 경험 말하기
```
I worked at Samsung for 5 years.
I studied computer science in college.
I lived in the US for two years.
I learned Python last year.
```

### 대화
```
A: What did you do yesterday?
B: I worked from home and attended virtual meetings.

A: Did you finish the report?
B: Yes, I did. I submitted it this morning.

A: When did you join the company?
B: I joined three years ago.
```
