# Be동사 (To Be)

## 📖 개념

Be동사는 영어에서 가장 기본적이고 중요한 동사입니다.
- **의미**: ~이다, ~있다, ~되다
- **역할**: 주어의 상태나 존재를 나타냄

---

## 📊 Be동사의 형태

### 현재형 (Present)

| 주어 | Be동사 | 축약형 | 부정형 | 부정 축약형 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|------------|
| I | am | I'm | am not | I'm not |
| You | are | you're | are not | aren't |
| He/She/It | is | he's/she's/it's | is not | isn't |
| We | are | we're | are not | aren't |
| They | are | they're | are not | aren't |

### 과거형 (Past)

| 주어 | Be동사 | 부정형 | 부정 축약형 |
|------|--------|--------|------------|
| I | was | was not | wasn't |
| You | were | were not | weren't |
| He/She/It | was | was not | wasn't |
| We | were | were not | weren't |
| They | were | were not | weren't |

---

## 💡 사용법

### 1. 상태/특성 설명
```
I am a developer. (나는 개발자다)
She is Korean. (그녀는 한국인이다)
They are busy. (그들은 바쁘다)
The book is interesting. (그 책은 흥미롭다)
```

### 2. 위치/존재 표현
```
I am at home. (나는 집에 있다)
The keys are on the table. (열쇠들이 테이블 위에 있다)
He is in Seoul. (그는 서울에 있다)
```

### 3. 나이/시간/날씨
```
I am 25 years old. (나는 25살이다)
It is 3 o'clock. (3시다)
It is sunny today. (오늘은 화창하다)
```

---

## 📝 문장 구조

### 평서문 (긍정)
```
주어 + be동사 + 보어/부사
I am tired.
She is a teacher.
They are at work.
```

### 부정문
```
주어 + be동사 + not + 보어/부사
I am not tired.
She is not (isn't) a teacher.
They are not (aren't) at work.
```

### 의문문
```
Be동사 + 주어 + 보어/부사?
Are you tired?
Is she a teacher?
Are they at work?
```

### 의문문 답변
```
Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
```

---

## ⚠️ 주의사항

### 1. 주어-동사 일치
```
❌ I is a student.
✅ I am a student.

❌ He are busy.
✅ He is busy.
```

### 2. 축약형 사용
**구어체에서 자주 사용:**
```
I'm ready. (I am ready.)
She's here. (She is here.)
They're late. (They are late.)
```

**부정형 축약:**
```
I'm not ready. (am not은 ain't로 축약 안 됨)
She isn't here. (또는 She's not here.)
They aren't late. (또는 They're not late.)
```

### 3. There is/are 구문
```
There is a problem. (문제가 하나 있다)
There are problems. (문제들이 있다)
There was a meeting. (미팅이 있었다)
There were people. (사람들이 있었다)
```

## 🎯 실전 예문

### 자기소개
```
I'm John, and I'm a software engineer.
I'm from Seoul, but I'm currently in New York.
I'm interested in AI and machine learning.
```

### 일상 대화
```
A: Are you busy?
B: Yes, I am. I'm working on a project.

A: Where is Sarah?
B: She's in a meeting.

A: Are they ready?
B: No, they aren't. They're still preparing.
```

### 과거 이야기
```
Yesterday was Monday.
I was tired after work.
The meeting was productive.
We were satisfied with the results.
```
