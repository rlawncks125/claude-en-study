# 간접화법 (Reported Speech)

## 📌 기본 개념

간접화법은 **다른 사람이 말한 것을 전달**할 때 사용합니다.
직접화법(Direct Speech)의 따옴표를 없애고 시제를 바꿔서 표현합니다.

**비즈니스에서 간접화법을 쓰는 이유**:
- 회의록 작성
- 이메일에서 대화 내용 전달
- 보고서에서 인용
- 고객/동료의 의견 전달

## ✏️ 기본 형태

```
직접화법: He said, "I am busy."
간접화법: He said (that) he was busy.
```

### 주요 동사
- **say**: ~라고 말하다 (간접 목적어 없음)
- **tell**: ~에게 말하다 (tell + 사람 + that)
- **ask**: 묻다, 요청하다

## 🎯 시제 변화 규칙

### 기본 원칙
**말한 시점보다 한 단계 과거로**

| 직접화법 | 간접화법 |
|---------|---------|
| 현재 (am/is/are) | 과거 (was/were) |
| 현재 (do/does) | 과거 (did) |
| 과거 (did/was) | 과거완료 (had done/had been) |
| 현재완료 (have/has done) | 과거완료 (had done) |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| may | might |
| must | had to |

### 예시

#### 현재 → 과거
```
직접: She said, "I am busy."
간접: She said (that) she was busy.
     (그녀는 바쁘다고 말했습니다)

직접: He said, "I work here."
간접: He said (that) he worked there.
     (그는 여기서 일한다고 말했습니다)
```

#### 과거 → 과거완료
```
직접: She said, "I finished the report."
간접: She said (that) she had finished the report.
     (그녀는 보고서를 끝냈다고 말했습니다)

직접: He said, "I met the client."
간접: He said (that) he had met the client.
     (그는 고객을 만났다고 말했습니다)
```

#### will → would
```
직접: She said, "I will call you."
간접: She said (that) she would call me.
     (그녀는 나에게 전화하겠다고 말했습니다)
```

#### can → could
```
직접: He said, "I can help."
간접: He said (that) he could help.
     (그는 도울 수 있다고 말했습니다)
```

## 🔑 대명사와 시간/장소 변화

### 대명사 변화
```
직접: He said, "I will do it."
간접: He said he would do it.
     (I → he, my → his, me → him)

직접: She said, "You should come."
간접: She said I should come.
     (you → I/we/they - 상황에 따라)
```

### 시간 표현 변화
| 직접화법 | 간접화법 |
|---------|---------|
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| this week | that week |
| next week | the following week |
| ago | before |

```
직접: He said, "I will call you tomorrow."
간접: He said he would call me the next day.

직접: She said, "I saw him yesterday."
간접: She said she had seen him the day before.
```

### 장소 표현 변화
```
here → there
this → that
these → those
```

## 💼 Say vs Tell vs Ask

### Say
**사람 없이** 또는 **say to + 사람**
```
He said (that) he was busy.
He said to me (that) he was busy.

❌ He said me that...
```

### Tell
**tell + 사람** (필수)
```
He told me (that) he was busy.
She told us (that) the meeting was cancelled.

❌ He told that...
```

### Ask
**질문이나 요청**
```
He asked if I was busy.
She asked me to help her.
```

## 🗣️ 문장 유형별 간접화법

### 1. 평서문 (Statements)

**say (that) / tell 사람 (that)**
```
직접: "I am tired."
간접: He said (that) he was tired.
     He told me (that) he was tired.
```

### 2. 의문문 (Questions)

#### Yes/No 질문
**ask if/whether**
```
직접: "Are you busy?"
간접: He asked if I was busy.
     He asked whether I was busy.
     (그는 내가 바쁜지 물었습니다)

직접: "Can you help me?"
간접: She asked if I could help her.
     (그녀는 내가 도와줄 수 있는지 물었습니다)
```

#### 의문사 질문
**ask + 의문사**
```
직접: "Where do you work?"
간접: He asked where I worked.
     (그는 내가 어디서 일하는지 물었습니다)

직접: "When will you finish?"
간접: She asked when I would finish.
     (그녀는 내가 언제 끝낼지 물었습니다)
```

**주의**: 간접화법에서는 어순이 평서문 어순
```
✅ He asked where I worked. (주어 + 동사)
❌ He asked where did I work.
```

### 3. 명령문 (Commands)

**tell/ask + 사람 + to + 동사원형**
```
직접: "Please help me."
간접: She asked me to help her.
     (그녀는 나에게 도와달라고 요청했습니다)

직접: "Send the report."
간접: He told me to send the report.
     (그는 나에게 보고서를 보내라고 말했습니다)
```

**부정 명령**:
```
직접: "Don't be late."
간접: He told me not to be late.
     (그는 나에게 늦지 말라고 말했습니다)
```

### 4. 제안/권유 (Suggestions)

**suggest + -ing / suggest (that)**
```
직접: "Let's have a meeting."
간접: He suggested having a meeting.
     He suggested (that) we have a meeting.
     (그는 회의를 하자고 제안했습니다)
```

## 💼 비즈니스 상황별 활용

### 회의록 작성
```
직접 대화:
CEO: "We need to increase sales."
Manager: "I will create a new marketing plan."
Team: "Can we get more budget?"

회의록:
The CEO said that we needed to increase sales.
The manager said he would create a new marketing plan.
The team asked if they could get more budget.
```

### 이메일에서 대화 전달
```
The client said (that) they were satisfied with our service.
(고객은 우리 서비스에 만족한다고 말했습니다)

The supplier told us (that) they would deliver by Friday.
(공급업체는 금요일까지 배송하겠다고 우리에게 말했습니다)

My colleague asked if I could attend the meeting.
(동료가 제가 회의에 참석할 수 있는지 물었습니다)
```

### 보고서에서 인용
```
The survey showed that 80% of customers were satisfied.
(설문조사는 80%의 고객이 만족한다고 보여주었습니다)

Experts suggested that we should invest in R&D.
(전문가들은 우리가 R&D에 투자해야 한다고 제안했습니다)

The analysis indicated that sales had increased by 20%.
(분석은 매출이 20% 증가했다고 나타냈습니다)
```

## 🔑 시제 변화 예외 사항

### 1. 여전히 사실인 경우
```
직접: "The earth is round."
간접: He said the earth is round. (시제 안 바뀜)
     (지구가 둥글다는 것은 여전히 사실)

직접: "I work for Samsung."
간접: She said she works for Samsung.
     (여전히 삼성에서 일하면 시제 안 바뀌어도 됨)
```

### 2. 과거완료는 그대로
```
직접: "I had finished the work."
간접: He said he had finished the work.
     (과거완료는 더 이상 과거로 갈 수 없음)
```

### 3. 조건/가정법은 그대로
```
직접: "If I were you, I would accept."
간접: He said if he were me, he would accept.
     (were, would 그대로)
```

## 💬 대화 예시

### 예시 1: 회의 내용 전달
```
A: What did the manager say in the meeting?
   (미팅에서 매니저가 뭐라고 했어요?)

B: He said that we needed to finish the project by Friday.
   He also told us that the client was very important.
   He asked if we needed more resources.
   (금요일까지 프로젝트를 끝내야 한다고 말했어요.
   또한 고객이 매우 중요하다고 우리에게 말했어요.
   더 많은 자원이 필요한지 물었어요.)
```

### 예시 2: 고객 의견 전달
```
Manager: Did you talk to the client?
        (고객과 이야기했나요?)

Employee: Yes, he said he was very satisfied with our service.
         He told me that he would recommend us to others.
         He also asked if we could provide additional support.
         (네, 우리 서비스에 매우 만족한다고 말했습니다.
         다른 사람들에게 우리를 추천하겠다고 제게 말했습니다.
         또한 추가 지원을 제공할 수 있는지 물었습니다.)
```

### 예시 3: 지시사항 전달
```
A: What did the CEO tell you?
   (CEO가 당신에게 뭐라고 말했어요?)

B: She told me to prepare a presentation for next week.
   She also asked me to invite all team members.
   She said that this was a very important meeting.
   (다음 주 프레젠테이션을 준비하라고 말했어요.
   또한 모든 팀원을 초대하라고 요청했어요.
   이것이 매우 중요한 회의라고 말했어요.)
```

## ⚠️ 주의사항

### 1. say vs tell 혼동
```
❌ He said me that...
✅ He said that... / He told me that...

❌ He told that...
✅ He told me that...
```

### 2. 의문문 어순
```
❌ He asked where did I work.
✅ He asked where I worked.

❌ She asked if was I busy.
✅ She asked if I was busy.
```

### 3. that 생략 가능 (평서문)
```
✅ He said (that) he was busy.
✅ He told me (that) he was busy.

하지만 that을 쓰는 것이 더 명확함
```

### 4. 명령문에서 to 필수
```
❌ He told me send the report.
✅ He told me to send the report.
```
