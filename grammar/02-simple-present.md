# 일반동사 현재형 (Simple Present Tense)

## 📖 개념

일반동사 현재형은 습관, 반복되는 행동, 일반적인 사실을 표현할 때 사용합니다.

---

## 📊 동사 형태

### 기본 규칙

| 주어 | 동사 형태 | 예시 |
|------|----------|------|
| I/You/We/They | 동사원형 | work, study, like |
| He/She/It | 동사원형 + s/es | works, studies, likes |

### 3인칭 단수 (He/She/It) -s/-es 규칙

| 동사 끝 | 변화 | 예시 |
|---------|------|------|
| 일반 | +s | work → works, like → likes |
| -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o | +es | go → goes, watch → watches |
| 자음+y | y→ies | study → studies, try → tries |
| 모음+y | +s | play → plays, enjoy → enjoys |
| have | has | have → has |

---

## 💡 사용법

### 1. 습관적 행동
```
I work from 9 to 6. (나는 9시부터 6시까지 일한다)
She exercises every morning. (그녀는 매일 아침 운동한다)
They commute by subway. (그들은 지하철로 출퇴근한다)
```

### 2. 일반적 사실
```
Water boils at 100°C. (물은 100도에서 끓는다)
The sun rises in the east. (태양은 동쪽에서 뜬다)
English is important for business. (영어는 비즈니스에 중요하다)
```

### 3. 좋아하는 것/싫어하는 것
```
I like coffee. (나는 커피를 좋아한다)
He prefers tea. (그는 차를 선호한다)
She doesn't like spicy food. (그녀는 매운 음식을 좋아하지 않는다)
```

---

## 📝 문장 구조

### 평서문 (긍정)
```
주어 + 동사(원형/s) + 목적어/보어

I work at a tech company.
She manages a team.
They develop software.
```

### 부정문
```
주어 + do/does + not + 동사원형

I don't work on weekends.
He doesn't drink coffee.
They don't live in Seoul.
```

**축약형:**
- do not → don't
- does not → doesn't

### 의문문
```
Do/Does + 주어 + 동사원형?

Do you work here?
Does she speak English?
Do they need help?
```

### 의문문 답변
```
Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Yes, they do. / No, they don't.
```

## 🕐 시간 표현 (빈도부사)

현재형과 자주 함께 쓰이는 시간 표현:

### 빈도부사 위치
```
주어 + 빈도부사 + 동사
I always check my email in the morning.
She usually arrives early.
They often work late.
```

### 빈도부사 종류
```
100% - always (항상)
90%  - usually (보통)
70%  - often (자주)
50%  - sometimes (때때로)
30%  - occasionally (가끔)
10%  - rarely/seldom (드물게)
0%   - never (절대 ~하지 않는다)
```

### 시간 표현
```
every day/week/month/year (매일/주/월/년)
on Mondays (월요일마다)
in the morning/afternoon/evening (아침/오후/저녁에)
at night (밤에)
```

---

## ⚠️ 주의사항

### 1. 3인칭 단수 -s 잊지 않기
```
❌ He work at Google.
✅ He works at Google.

❌ She like pizza.
✅ She likes pizza.
```

### 2. 부정문/의문문에서 동사원형 사용
```
❌ He doesn't works here.
✅ He doesn't work here.

❌ Does she likes it?
✅ Does she like it?
```

### 3. 빈도부사 위치
```
❌ I check always my email.
✅ I always check my email.

But: I am always busy. (be동사 뒤)
```

## 🎯 실전 예문

### 업무 관련
```
I work in IT industry.
She handles customer inquiries.
He develops mobile applications.
We hold meetings every Monday.
The team submits reports weekly.
```

### 일상 루틴
```
I wake up at 7 AM.
I have breakfast at 8.
I commute by bus.
I work from 9 to 6.
I usually go to the gym after work.
I go to bed around midnight.
```

### 대화
```
A: What do you do?
B: I work as a data analyst.

A: Does he speak Korean?
B: Yes, he does. He's fluent.

A: Do they work on weekends?
B: No, they don't. They work Monday to Friday.
```

## ✏️ 연습 문제

### 문제 1: 동사 형태 선택

2. I (don't/doesn't) drink alcohol.
3. They (study/studies) English every day.
4. He (watch/watches) Netflix in the evening.
5. We (has/have) a meeting on Monday.

2. He plays guitar. → _____
3. They work on weekends. → _____

2. She works here. → _____
3. They need help. → _____
