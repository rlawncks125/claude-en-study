## 1️⃣ Communicating with the Board (25분)

### Board Presentation Structure
```
Time: Usually 15-20 minutes + Q&A

Structure:
1. Context Setting (2 min)
   - Market overview
   - Company position

2. Key Achievements (3 min)
   - Metrics that matter
   - Progress on strategic goals

3. Challenges & Risks (3 min)
   - Be transparent
   - Show you're managing risks

4. Strategic Recommendations (5 min)
   - What you're proposing
   - Why now
   - What you need

5. Financial Impact (3 min)
   - Investment required
   - Expected returns
   - ROI timeline

6. Next Steps (2 min)
   - Decision needed
   - Timeline
```

### Example: Board Presentation Opening
```
"Good morning. Today I'll brief you on our Q3 performance, discuss
two emerging risks, and seek your input on our expansion strategy.

First, the context. Our market grew 15% this quarter, driven by
increased enterprise adoption. We're positioned #3 with 18% market
share, up from 15% last quarter.

Key achievements this quarter:
• Revenue: $50M, 120% of target
• New customer acquisition: 450, our best quarter ever
• Net retention: 115%, industry-leading
• Product NPS: 65, up from 58

However, we face two significant challenges I want to address..."
```

### Board Communication Principles
```
DO:
✓ Be concise - they're busy
✓ Lead with recommendations
✓ Quantify everything
✓ Acknowledge risks honestly
✓ Prepare for tough questions
✓ Stay calm under pressure

DON'T:
✗ Sugarcoat problems
✗ Present data without insights
✗ Waste time on minutiae
✗ Defend mistakes - own them
✗ Ask for decisions without options
```

## 2️⃣ Investor Relations (IR) Communication (20분)

### Earnings Call Script
```
Opening Remarks:
"Thank you for joining us today. I'm [Name], CEO of [Company], joined
by [CFO Name], our CFO. We'll review our Q4 results, discuss our
strategic priorities for the coming year, and take your questions.

Before we begin, I'll remind you that today's discussion contains
forward-looking statements subject to risks and uncertainties..."

Results Summary:
"I'm pleased to report strong Q4 performance across all key metrics.
Revenue grew 35% year-over-year to $100M, exceeding our guidance of
$95-98M. Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 25%, up from 20% a year
ago, demonstrating operating leverage as we scale.

Let me highlight three key achievements:

First, enterprise momentum. We added 150 enterprise customers,
bringing our total to 800. Enterprise now represents 70% of revenue,
up from 65% last quarter. These customers have 3x higher lifetime
value and significantly lower churn.

Second, international expansion. International revenue reached 30%
of total, up from 20% a year ago. We're particularly pleased with
traction in EMEA, where we're now #2 in market share.

Third, product innovation. We launched three major features that
directly address our customers' top pain points. Early adoption rates
exceed 60%, and we're seeing strong correlation with expansion revenue.

Looking ahead to 2024, we have three strategic priorities..."

Handling Tough Questions:
Analyst: "Your sales efficiency declined this quarter. What's driving
that and when will it improve?"

You: "That's a great question. You're right that CAC increased 15%
quarter-over-quarter. This is intentional and temporary. We're
investing ahead of the curve in our enterprise sales team to capture
the market opportunity. We hired 20 enterprise reps who are still
ramping—the average ramp time is 6 months. We expect to see payback
starting Q2, with full efficiency returning by Q3. CFO, would you
add context on the LTV:CAC ratio?"
```

## 3️⃣ C-Suite Meeting Language (20분)

### Meeting with CEO
```
Situation: Requesting budget approval

Bad Approach:
"I need more budget for my team."

Good Approach:
"I'd like to discuss a strategic investment in [area]. Based on our
growth trajectory and competitive dynamics, I believe we're
under-invested relative to the opportunity. I've prepared a brief
analysis showing:

1. The market opportunity: $X TAM growing at Y%
2. Our current position: Z% market share
3. The gap: Competitors are investing 2x more in [area]
4. My proposal: $X investment over Y months
5. Expected return: $Z revenue, X month payback

I have a one-pager if you'd like to review it, but wanted to get your
initial reaction first. Does this align with our strategic priorities?"

Key Principles:
- Be strategic, not tactical
- Lead with impact, not activity
- Show you've done the analysis
- Make it easy for them to say yes
- Respect their time
```

### Meeting with CFO
```
Situation: Justifying expense

CFO: "Why did we spend $50K over budget last month?"

Bad Response:
"We needed it for the project."

Good Response:
"I appreciate you flagging that. The $50K overage was driven by two
factors:

First, $30K for expedited hiring. We had an unexpected resignation
in a critical role, and delaying backfill would have put our Q4
deliverables at risk. The alternative was missing our launch date,
which would have cost us an estimated $200K in deferred revenue.

Second, $20K for additional cloud infrastructure. User growth
exceeded forecast by 40%, and we needed to scale to maintain
performance SLAs. This directly enabled $100K in expansion ARR.

Going forward, I've implemented a weekly budget review with my team
to catch variances earlier. I'm also building a 10% contingency
buffer into Q1 planning for similar situations.

Would you like me to send you the detailed variance analysis?"

Key Principles:
- Numbers, numbers, numbers
- Explain ROI on every dollar
- Show you're managing it
- Be accountable
- Look forward, not just backward
```

### Meeting with CTO
```
Situation: Requesting technical resources

You: "I need engineering support for a customer-facing project."

CTO: "Engineering is fully allocated on product roadmap."

Strategic Response:
"I understand roadmap commitments are critical. Let me share context
on why I'm bringing this up.

We have 5 enterprise deals worth $2M total ARR contingent on [feature].
These customers represent our ideal profile and could drive significant
expansion. The sales cycle is already 8 months.

I've done preliminary scoping with Sarah on your team, and she
estimates 2 weeks of engineering time. I'm proposing we treat this as
a strategic investment rather than a customer customization.

Here's how I'd frame the tradeoffs:
- Option A: Delay roadmap feature X by 2 weeks, deliver this
- Option B: I hire a contractor (cost: $15K, risk: quality/integration)
- Option C: We defer these deals (cost: $2M ARR delayed 6+ months)

I'm not saying Option A is obviously right, but I wanted to get your
perspective on the tradeoffs. What do you think?"

Key Principles:
- Understand their constraints
- Quantify the business impact
- Propose options, not demands
- Make it collaborative
- Respect their expertise
```

## 4️⃣ Strategic Decision-Making Discussions (15분)

### Framework: Situation-Complication-Resolution

```
Situation:
"We're currently in 3 markets with 60% market share in our core
segment."

Complication:
"However, two well-funded competitors have entered our space and are
undercutting on price by 30%. We're seeing our win rate decline from
70% to 50% in the past quarter."

Resolution:
"I've analyzed three strategic options:

Option 1: Match on price
- Pros: Defend market share
- Cons: 40% margin erosion, sets bad precedent
- Recommendation: No

Option 2: Differentiate up-market
- Pros: Higher margins, less competition
- Cons: Requires 6-month product investment, smaller TAM
- Recommendation: Maybe

Option 3: Expand to adjacent markets
- Pros: New revenue streams, diversification
- Cons: Execution risk, resource dilution
- Recommendation: Yes, with phased approach

My recommendation is Option 3 with a pilot in Market A, because..."
```

### Asking for Strategic Input
```
"I'd value your perspective on a strategic dilemma we're facing.

Context: [2-3 sentences]

The question: Should we [Option A] or [Option B]?

My analysis: [Key factors]

My instinct: [Your recommendation]

What am I missing? How would you think about this?"

This approach:
- Shows you've done the work
- Respects their experience
- Makes efficient use of time
- Invites genuine input
```

## 5️⃣ Daily Assessment (100점)

### Part A: Board Update (30점)
Write a 5-minute board update on a challenge your company faces
(or create a scenario). Include:
- Context (2-3 sentences)
- The challenge (with data)
- Your recommendation
- Expected outcome

**Length**: 300-400 words

### Part B: Investor Q&A (30점)
Write responses to these investor questions:

1. "Why is your churn increasing?" (100 words)
2. "When will you be profitable?" (100 words)
3. "How do you plan to compete against [bigger competitor]?" (150 words)

### Part C: Executive Meeting (40점)
Write a script for seeking CEO approval for a $500K investment
(500-600 words)

Include:
- Strategic context
- The opportunity/problem
- Your recommendation
- Financial impact
- Risk mitigation
- What you need from them
