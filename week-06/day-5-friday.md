## 1️⃣ Contract Fundamentals (20분)

### Standard Contract Structure
```
1. Title & Parties
2. Recitals / Background ("WHEREAS...")
3. Definitions
4. Scope of Work / Services
5. Term & Termination
6. Payment Terms
7. Intellectual Property Rights
8. Confidentiality
9. Warranties & Representations
10. Liability & Indemnification
11. Dispute Resolution
12. General Provisions
13. Signatures
```

### Key Legal Terminology
```
Party / Parties (당사자)
- "The Company" (provider)
- "The Client" (recipient)
- "Hereinafter referred to as..." (이하 ~로 지칭)

Agreement / Contract (계약)
- "This Agreement" (본 계약)
- "Effective Date" (발효일)
- "Term" (기간)

Obligations (의무)
- "shall" = must (반드시 ~해야 함)
- "may" = can (~할 수 있음)
- "will" = expected to (~ 할 것임)

Conditions (조건)
- "Subject to" (~을 조건으로)
- "Provided that" (~한다는 조건으로)
- "In the event that" (~의 경우)

Scope (범위)
- "Including but not limited to" (다음을 포함하되 이에 한정되지 않음)
- "Solely" (오직)
- "Exclusively" (독점적으로)

Legal Actions (법적 조치)
- "Terminate" (해지하다)
- "Breach" (위반)
- "Remedy" (구제)
- "Indemnify" (면책하다)
- "Waive" (포기하다)
```

## 2️⃣ NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) (15분)

### NDA Template
```
NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
[Date] ("Effective Date") by and between:

[Company A], a [jurisdiction] corporation with its principal place
of business at [address] ("Disclosing Party"), and

[Company B], a [jurisdiction] corporation with its principal place
of business at [address] ("Receiving Party").

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to explore a potential business
relationship; and

WHEREAS, in connection with such discussions, Disclosing Party may
disclose certain confidential and proprietary information to
Receiving Party;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and
agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by
Disclosing Party to Receiving Party, either directly or indirectly,
in writing, orally, or by inspection, that is designated as
"Confidential," "Proprietary," or some similar designation, or that
reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature
of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:
(a) Business plans, strategies, and financial information;
(b) Customer and supplier lists;
(c) Technical data, trade secrets, and know-how;
(d) Product specifications and roadmaps;
(e) Marketing and sales information.

Confidential Information does NOT include information that:
(a) Is or becomes generally available to the public through no act
    or omission of Receiving Party;
(b) Was in Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure;
(c) Is rightfully disclosed to Receiving Party by a third party
    without confidentiality obligations;
(d) Is independently developed by Receiving Party without use of
    Confidential Information.

2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

Receiving Party shall:
(a) Maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information;
(b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without
    prior written consent of Disclosing Party;
(c) Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating
    the potential business relationship;
(d) Limit disclosure to employees and advisors with a need to know;
(e) Protect Confidential Information with the same degree of care
    used to protect its own confidential information, but no less
    than reasonable care.

3. TERM

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for
a period of [2] years, unless earlier terminated by either Party
upon [30] days' written notice.

The obligations regarding Confidential Information shall survive
termination for [5] years.

4. RETURN OF MATERIALS

Upon termination or upon Disclosing Party's request, Receiving Party
shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and
certify such destruction in writing.

5. REMEDIES

Receiving Party acknowledges that breach of this Agreement may cause
irreparable harm for which monetary damages may not be an adequate
remedy. Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek equitable relief,
including injunction and specific performance, in addition to all
other remedies available at law or in equity.

6. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
the laws of [Jurisdiction], without regard to its conflict of law
provisions.

7. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties
concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior
agreements and understandings, whether written or oral.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of
the date first above written.

[Company A]                    [Company B]

By: ___________________        By: ___________________
Name:                          Name:
Title:                         Title:
Date:                          Date:
```

## 3️⃣ LOI & MOU (Letter of Intent & Memorandum of Understanding) (15분)

### LOI Template
```
LETTER OF INTENT

Date: [Date]

To: [Company Name]
Attention: [Name, Title]

Re: Proposed Acquisition of [Target Company]

Dear [Name]:

This letter of intent ("LOI") outlines the principal terms of a
proposed transaction (the "Transaction") between [Buyer] ("Buyer")
and [Seller] ("Seller") regarding the acquisition of [Target Company]
(the "Company").

1. PROPOSED TRANSACTION

Buyer proposes to acquire 100% of the outstanding shares of the
Company for a purchase price of [Amount] (the "Purchase Price"),
subject to customary adjustments for working capital, cash, and debt.

2. STRUCTURE

The Transaction will be structured as a stock purchase, with Buyer
acquiring all issued and outstanding shares of the Company from Seller.

3. PURCHASE PRICE

Total Purchase Price: $[X]
- Cash at closing: $[Y]
- Earn-out: $[Z] over [period], subject to achieving [metrics]

4. DUE DILIGENCE

Buyer shall have [60] days from the date of this LOI (the "Due
Diligence Period") to conduct financial, legal, and operational
due diligence.

5. EXCLUSIVITY

During the Exclusivity Period ([90] days from date of this LOI),
Seller agrees not to solicit, encourage, or engage in discussions
with any other party regarding a sale or merger of the Company.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

The terms of this LOI and all information exchanged shall be kept
strictly confidential, except as required by law or with mutual
written consent.

7. DEFINITIVE AGREEMENTS

This LOI is non-binding (except for Sections 5, 6, 7, and 8) and
is subject to execution of definitive purchase agreements acceptable
to both Parties.

Target Closing Date: [Date]

8. EXPENSES

Each Party shall bear its own expenses in connection with this
Transaction, including legal and advisory fees.

9. GOVERNING LAW

This LOI shall be governed by the laws of [Jurisdiction].

If the foregoing accurately reflects our mutual understanding, please
sign below to acknowledge your agreement.

Sincerely,

[Buyer Company]

By: ___________________
Name:
Title:

ACCEPTED AND AGREED:

[Seller Company]

By: ___________________
Name:
Title:
Date:
```

## 4️⃣ Negotiating Contract Terms (20분)

### Common Negotiation Points

#### Payment Terms
```
Initial Proposal:
"Net 30 days"

Negotiation:
Client: "Can we extend to Net 60?"

You: "Our standard terms are Net 30 to maintain cash flow. However,
if you can commit to a longer contract term or increase the contract
value by 10%, we could consider Net 45. Would either of those work?"

Key Phrases:
- "Subject to receipt of invoice"
- "Payment due upon delivery"
- "Milestone-based payments"
- "Retainage" (보류금)
```

#### Liability Cap
```
Initial Proposal:
"Liability shall not exceed fees paid in the 12 months prior to claim"

Negotiation:
Client: "We need uncapped liability given the critical nature of
services."

You: "I understand your concern about service quality. While we can't
agree to uncapped liability, we could:
- Increase the cap to 2x annual fees
- Exclude certain categories (gross negligence, willful misconduct)
- Add specific insurance requirements
- Include service level credits for performance issues

Which of these would best address your concerns?"

Key Phrases:
- "Liability is limited to..."
- "Except in cases of..."
- "Consequential damages"
- "Indemnification"
```

#### Termination Clauses
```
Initial Proposal:
"Either party may terminate with 30 days' notice"

Negotiation:
Client: "We need ability to terminate for convenience with 7 days'
notice."

You: "A 7-day termination period creates significant business risk for
us. Could we meet in the middle with these options:
- 30 days' notice with no penalty
- 7 days' notice with payment for the full notice period
- Termination for cause (material breach) with 15 days to cure

What matters most to you - flexibility or cost?"

Key Phrases:
- "Termination for convenience"
- "Termination for cause"
- "Notice period"
- "Cure period"
- "Survival provisions"
```

## 5️⃣ Daily Assessment (100점)

### Part A: Contract Clause Analysis (30점)

Read and explain this clause in plain English:

```
"Notwithstanding anything to the contrary herein, Provider's aggregate
liability arising out of or related to this Agreement, whether in
contract, tort, or otherwise, shall not exceed the total fees paid by
Client to Provider in the twelve (12) month period preceding the event
giving rise to such claim; provided, however, that such limitation
shall not apply to Provider's obligations under Section 7 (Indemnification),
breaches of Section 5 (Confidentiality), or Provider's gross negligence
or willful misconduct."
```

**Task**:
1. What does this clause mean? (150 words)
2. What are the exceptions to the liability cap? (50 words)
3. Is this favorable to Provider or Client? Why? (100 words)

### Part B: NDA Drafting (40점)

Draft a one-way NDA for a meeting where you'll discuss your company's
new product with a potential partner (300-400 words).

Include:
- Definition of Confidential Information
- Receiving Party obligations
- Term duration
- Return of materials clause

### Part C: Contract Negotiation (30점)

**Scenario**: You received a contract with these terms:
- Payment: Net 90 days
- Liability cap: $10K (your services are worth $100K)
- Non-compete: 2 years, global

**Task**: Write an email proposing counterterms with justification
(300 words).
