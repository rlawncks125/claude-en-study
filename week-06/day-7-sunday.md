## 1️⃣ Week 6 Mastery Test (400점, 60분)

### Task 1: Mock Job Interview (100점)

**Scenario**: 당신은 Senior [Your Role] 포지션에 면접 중입니다.

**Write answers to** (각 25점, 150-200 words each):

1. "Tell me about yourself"
2. "Describe a time when you led a difficult project" (STAR method)
3. "Why should we hire you over other candidates?"
4. "What are your salary expectations and how did you arrive at that number?"

**평가 기준**:
- Clear structure
- Specific examples with metrics
- Confident tone
- Professional language

---

### Task 2: Executive Proposal (100점)

**Scenario**: Write a proposal to your CEO requesting approval for a
strategic initiative.

**Requirements** (500-600 words):
- Executive summary
- Business case (problem & opportunity)
- Your recommendation with 3 options
- Financial impact
- Implementation plan
- What you need from them

**평가 기준**:
- Strategic thinking (30점)
- Data-driven justification (30점)
- Clear communication (25점)
- Professional format (15점)

---

### Task 3: Negotiation Strategy (100점)

**Scenario**: You're negotiating a $500K software contract.

Vendor's offer:
- Price: $500K/year
- Term: 3 years minimum
- Payment: 100% upfront
- Support: Business hours only

Your target:
- Price: $350K/year
- Term: 1 year pilot
- Payment: Quarterly
- Support: 24/7

**Write** (400-500 words):
1. Your BATNA and ZOPA analysis (150 words)
2. Your negotiation strategy (200 words)
3. Your opening statement (150 words)

**평가 기준**:
- Strategic analysis (40점)
- Persuasive language (30점)
- Realistic approach (30점)

---

### Task 4: Contract & Personal Brand (100점)

**Part A** (50점): Draft a mutual NDA between your company and a
potential partner (250-300 words).

Include:
- Definition of Confidential Information
- Obligations
- Term
- Return of materials

**Part B** (50점): Write a LinkedIn post announcing a professional
achievement (200-250 words).

Include:
- Hook
- Story
- Lesson learned
- CTA

## 2️⃣ 6-Week Capstone Project (400점, 45분)

### The Ultimate Business Challenge

**Scenario**:
You are the newly hired VP of [Your Function] at a growing company.
In your first 90 days, you must:

1. Understand the current state
2. Identify key challenges
3. Develop a strategic plan
4. Present to leadership
5. Begin implementation

**Your Deliverable**: A comprehensive 90-day plan

---

### Deliverable 1: Executive Summary (100점)

Write a 1-page executive summary (400-500 words) covering:

**Current State**:
- Market position
- Key metrics
- Main challenges

**Strategic Priorities** (Top 3):
1. [Priority 1] - Why & Impact
2. [Priority 2] - Why & Impact
3. [Priority 3] - Why & Impact

**90-Day Plan**:
- Month 1: Assessment
- Month 2: Strategy
- Month 3: Initial execution

**Success Metrics**:
- What you'll measure
- Targets for 90 days
- Long-term goals

**Investment Needed**:
- Budget
- Headcount
- Other resources

---

### Deliverable 2: Stakeholder Communication Plan (100점)

**Write 3 communications** (각 150-200 words):

**A. Email to your team** (First day message)
- Introduction
- Your approach
- What you need from them
- How you'll support them

**B. Update to CEO** (30-day progress)
- Key findings
- Challenges discovered
- Initial wins
- Recommendations

**C. Board presentation script** (90-day review)
- Achievements
- Metrics
- Challenges & risks
- Next phase plan

---

### Deliverable 3: Crisis Response (100점)

**Scenario**: On Day 60, a major problem occurs:
[Choose one: Product failure / Customer complaint viral / Competitor
announcement / Key employee resignation / Budget cut]

**Write your response** (400-500 words):
1. Immediate action plan (100 words)
2. Internal communication (150 words)
3. External communication if needed (150 words)
4. Prevention measures (100 words)

---

### Deliverable 4: Personal Development Plan (100점)

**Reflect on 6 weeks and write** (400-500 words):

**What I've Mastered**:
- Top 3 skills acquired
- Biggest improvements
- Confidence areas

**What I Still Need to Improve**:
- Remaining challenges
- Specific gaps
- Learning plan

**My 6-Month English Development Plan**:
- Daily practices (15-30 min)
- Weekly practices (1-2 hours)
- Monthly goals
- Resources I'll use

**How I'll Apply This in My Career**:
- Immediate applications
- 3-month goals
- 1-year vision
