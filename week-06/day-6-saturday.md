## 1️⃣ Personal Branding Strategy (20분)

### Define Your Brand
```
1. Your Expertise (전문성)
What are you known for?
Example: "Data-driven marketing strategist specializing in B2B SaaS
growth"

2. Your Unique Value (독특한 가치)
What makes you different?
Example: "I combine 10 years of technical background with marketing
expertise to bridge the gap between product and growth"

3. Your Target Audience (타겟)
Who do you want to reach?
- Potential employers
- Clients
- Peers in your industry
- Speaking opportunities

4. Your Message (메시지)
What do you want to be known for saying?
Example: "Marketing without data is guessing. I help companies turn
analytics into actionable growth strategies."
```

### Personal Brand Statement
```
Template:
"I help [target audience] achieve [outcome] through [unique approach]."

Examples:
✓ "I help B2B SaaS companies scale from $1M to $10M ARR through
   data-driven growth strategies and team building."

✓ "I partner with enterprise CTOs to modernize legacy systems and
   build cloud-native architectures that reduce costs by 40%."

✓ "I transform traditional sales teams into consultative selling
   powerhouses, increasing win rates by an average of 35%."
```

## 2️⃣ LinkedIn Strategy (25분)

### Optimized LinkedIn Profile

#### Headline (220 characters)
```
Bad:
"Marketing Manager at TechCorp"

Good:
"B2B SaaS Growth Leader | Scaled 3 companies from $5M to $50M+ ARR |
Speaker on Data-Driven Marketing | Ex-Google, HubSpot"

Formula: [Role] | [Achievement] | [Credibility] | [Companies]
```

#### About Section (2,600 characters)
```
Structure:
1. Hook (1-2 sentences)
2. Who I am (current role)
3. What I've done (key achievements)
4. How I can help you
5. Call to action

drive revenue. I help companies fix that.

Before GrowthCo, I built the marketing function at two successful

acquisitions. At TechStart, I grew MQLs from 50/month to 500/month
in 18 months. At DataCorp, I reduced CAC by 40% while tripling lead
volume.

My approach is simple: ruthless focus on what drives revenue, not
vanity metrics. I've helped 50+ B2B companies optimize their marketing
through data-driven strategies, and I share these learnings through:
- Speaking at 15+ industry conferences annually
- Writing for publications like TechCrunch and Forbes
- Teaching growth marketing at Stanford Continuing Studies

I specialize in:
• Demand generation & lead nurturing
• Marketing analytics & attribution
• Team building & management
• Go-to-market strategy
• Marketing operations & tech stack

If you're a B2B SaaS company looking to scale predictably and
profitably, let's connect. I'm always happy to exchange ideas with
fellow growth professionals.

📧 reach me at: [email]
📝 Read my articles: [blog link]
🎤 Book me to speak: [calendar link]"
```

### LinkedIn Content Strategy

#### Post Types
```
1. Personal Story (월 1-2회)
   "3 years ago, I made the worst career decision of my life..."
   → Share lesson learned

2. Industry Insight (주 2-3회)
   "I analyzed 100 failed product launches. Here's what they had
   in common..."
   → Provide value

3. Quick Tip (주 2-3회)
   "Most teams waste 10 hours/week on meetings. Here's how we cut
   ours in half:"
   → Actionable advice

4. Question/Poll (주 1회)
   "What's the biggest challenge in your role right now?"
   → Drive engagement

5. Thought Leadership (월 2회)
   "The future of [industry] isn't what you think. Here's why..."
   → Establish expertise
```

#### High-Engagement Post Format
```
Hook (first line - visible without "see more"):
"I just turned down a $500K job offer. Here's why:"

Body:
[Story with line breaks for readability]

- Use bullet points
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences)
- Add white space
- Use emojis strategically

Conclusion:
[Key takeaway]

Call to Action:
"What's your take? Drop a comment below."
"Repost if you found this valuable."
"Follow for more insights on [topic]."

Example Post:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
I failed my first product launch spectacularly. Here's what I learned:

3 years ago, I launched a feature I was sure would be a game-changer.

I spent 6 months building it.
I convinced the executive team to bet on it.
I pushed the engineering team hard to deliver.

Launch day came.

Adoption rate: 8% (we needed 40%)
Customer feedback: "This isn't what we asked for"
My confidence: shattered

Here's what I did wrong:

❌ I fell in love with MY idea
❌ I did minimal user research
❌ I ignored warning signs
❌ I shipped before validating

What I do differently now:

✅ Talk to 20+ users before writing a single requirement
✅ Build MVPs to test assumptions
✅ Measure everything
✅ Kill projects that don't show early traction

That failed launch was the best thing that happened to my career.

My next 3 launches?
- Feature A: 65% adoption in month 1
- Feature B: 80% adoption, drove 30% revenue growth
- Feature C: Industry award winner

Failure isn't the end. It's data.

What's the biggest failure that taught you the most?

#ProductManagement #Lessons #Growth
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

## 3️⃣ Conference Speaking (20분)

### Getting Speaking Opportunities

#### CFP (Call for Proposals) Response
```
Conference: [Name]
Talk Title: "From $1M to $10M: A Playbook for B2B SaaS Growth"

Abstract (150 words):
Scaling from $1M to $10M ARR is where most B2B SaaS companies stumble.
I've done it three times. In this talk, I'll share the exact playbook
we used to achieve predictable, profitable growth.

You'll learn:
- The 3 metrics that matter (and 20 that don't)
- How to build a scalable go-to-market engine
- When to specialize vs. generalize your product
- How to avoid the "$5M plateau" trap
- Real examples and mistakes from my journey

This isn't theory—it's battle-tested strategies from taking
TechStart from $1M to $12M in 24 months and scaling two other
companies past $10M.

You'll leave with a framework you can implement immediately.

Speaker Bio (100 words):
[Your Name] is VP of Growth at [Company], where she's led the company
from $3M to $25M ARR in 3 years. Previously, she scaled marketing at
TechStart (Series A to acquisition) and DataCorp (2x ARR growth).
She's spoken at 20+ conferences including SaaStr, Inbound, and SalesConf.
Her articles on growth have been featured in TechCrunch and Forbes.
She advises early-stage B2B startups and teaches at Stanford.
```

### Presentation Structure
```
TEDx-Style Talk (18 minutes):

Opening (2 min):
- Hook with story or surprising stat
- "I'm going to share something controversial..."

Problem (3 min):
- Paint the pain
- Make it relatable
- Show the cost of status quo

Journey (8 min):
- Your experience/research
- What you learned
- Turning points
- Specific examples

Solution (4 min):
- Your framework/approach
- How it works
- Proof points

Close (1 min):
- Call to action
- Inspiring finish
- Memorable one-liner

Example Opening:
"By show of hands, how many of you track your company's NPS score?"
[Pause for hands]

"Now keep your hand up if you've changed your product roadmap based
on that score."
[Most hands go down]

"That's the problem. We're drowning in data but starving for insight.
Today, I'm going to show you the 3 metrics that actually drive growth,
and how we used them to 10x revenue in 2 years."
```

## 4️⃣ Building Influence (15분)

### Content Creation

#### Blog Post Structure
```
Title: "The [X] Framework for [Outcome]"

Introduction (100 words):
- Hook
- Problem
- Promise

Body (800-1000 words):
- Framework overview
- Element 1 with example
- Element 2 with example
- Element 3 with example
- How to implement

Conclusion (100 words):
- Summary
- Next steps
- CTA

Example Title:
"The 5-3-1 Framework for Effective 1-on-1 Meetings"

Framework:
5 = Preparation (5 minutes before)
3 = Structure (3 agenda items max)
1 = Action (1 clear outcome)
```

### Newsletter Strategy
```
Weekly Newsletter: "[Your Name]'s Growth Insights"

Structure:
1. Personal update (50 words)
   "This week I..."

2. Main insight (300 words)
   Deep dive on one topic

3. Quick wins (3-5 bullets)
   Actionable tips

4. What I'm reading (2-3 links)
   Curated content

5. Ask/Offer
   How you can help readers or how they can help you

Tone: Professional but personal, like emailing a friend
```
