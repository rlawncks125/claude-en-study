## 1️⃣ 프레젠테이션 구조 (10분)

### 표준 프레젠테이션 구조
```
1. Opening (5-10%)
2. Introduction (10-15%)
3. Main Body (60-70%)
4. Conclusion (10-15%)
5. Q&A (5-10%)
```

### 1) Opening - 주의 끌기
```
Good morning/afternoon, everyone. Thank you for being here today.
I'm [Name], [Position] at [Company].

Starting with a question:
- How many of you have experienced [problem]?
- What if I told you that [surprising fact]?

Starting with a story:
- Let me start with a brief story about...
- A few months ago, we faced a challenge...

Starting with a statistic:
- According to recent research, [statistic]...
- Studies show that [data]...
```

### 2) Introduction - 주제 소개
```
Today, I'm going to talk about [topic].
My presentation will cover three main areas:
1. First, I'll discuss...
2. Then, I'll explain...
3. Finally, I'll demonstrate...

The presentation will take approximately [time] minutes, and 
I'll be happy to answer questions at the end.
```

### 3) Main Body - 핵심 내용
```
Transitioning:
- Let's move on to the first point...
- Now, I'd like to turn to...
- This brings me to my next point...

Emphasizing:
- I'd like to emphasize that...
- It's important to note that...
- The key point here is...

Referring to visuals:
- As you can see on this slide...
- This chart clearly demonstrates...
- Let me draw your attention to...

Providing examples:
- For example...
- To illustrate this point...
- A good case in point is...
```

### 4) Conclusion - 요약 및 행동 촉구
```
To summarize, we've discussed...
The key takeaways are:
1. [Point 1]
2. [Point 2]
3. [Point 3]

In conclusion, [main message].
I recommend that we [call to action].

Thank you for your attention. Are there any questions?
```

### 5) Q&A - 질의응답
```
Inviting questions:
- I'd be happy to answer any questions you may have.
- Does anyone have any questions or concerns?

Handling questions:
- That's a great question. [Answer]
- I'm glad you asked that. [Answer]
- I don't have that information right now, but I'll find out 
  and get back to you.

Difficult questions:
- That's beyond the scope of today's presentation, but...
- Let's discuss that in more detail after the presentation.
```

## 2️⃣ 시각 자료 활용 표현 (15분)

### 차트 및 그래프
```
Line graphs:
- As you can see, sales have increased steadily since...
- There was a sharp rise/decline in [period].
- The trend shows...

Bar charts:
- This chart compares [X] and [Y].
- Product A outperformed Product B by...
- The data indicates that...

Pie charts:
- This pie chart shows the breakdown of...
- [Category] accounts for X% of the total.
- The largest segment represents...
```

### 테이블 및 데이터
```
According to this table...
The data reveals that...
If we look at the numbers...
The figures demonstrate...
```

### 이미지 및 다이어그램
```
This image illustrates...
Here's a visual representation of...
This diagram shows the process of...
As depicted in this flowchart...
```

## 3️⃣ 청중 참여 기술 (10분)

### 질문하기
```
Rhetorical questions:
- Why is this important?
- What does this mean for us?

Direct questions:
- Has anyone here experienced this?
- Show of hands - how many of you agree?
- Can someone share an example from their experience?
```

### 상호작용 촉진
```
Let's do a quick poll...
Take a moment to discuss with your neighbor...
I'd like to hear your thoughts on this...
Who would like to share their perspective?
```

### 반응 확인
```
Does this make sense so far?
Are you with me?
Any questions before I continue?
Is everyone able to see this clearly?
```

## 4️⃣ 프레젠테이션 시나리오 예시 (10분)

### 제품 출시 프레젠테이션
```
Opening:
Good morning, everyone. Imagine a product that could save your 
team 10 hours a week. Today, I'm excited to introduce exactly that.

Introduction:
I'm Sarah Kim, Product Manager at TechCorp. For the past year, 
we've been developing a solution that addresses the productivity 
challenges many of you face daily. Today, I'll present our new 
product, explain its key features, and demonstrate how it can 
transform your workflow.

Main Body:
First, let's talk about the problem we're solving. [Data and examples]

Second, our solution. As you can see on this slide, our product 
offers three main features... [Details with visuals]

Third, let me show you real results. This chart demonstrates that 
our beta users saw a 40% increase in productivity. [Case studies]

Conclusion:
To summarize, our product saves time, reduces costs, and improves 
quality. I believe it's the solution you've been looking for.

We're offering an exclusive pre-launch discount to early adopters. 
I encourage you to take advantage of this opportunity.

Thank you. I'm happy to answer any questions.
```

## 5️⃣ 프레젠테이션 팁 (5분)

### DO's ✅
```
✅ Practice multiple times
✅ Know your audience
✅ Use clear, simple language
✅ Make eye contact
✅ Speak confidently and clearly
✅ Use pauses effectively
✅ Prepare for technical issues
✅ Stay within time limit
```

### DON'Ts ❌
```
❌ Read from slides
❌ Use too much text on slides
❌ Turn your back to audience
❌ Speak too fast
❌ Go over time
❌ Use jargon without explanation
❌ Apologize unnecessarily
```
