## 1️⃣ 회의 진행 표현 (15분)

### 회의 시작 (Opening)
```
Welcome everyone and thank you for joining today's meeting.
Let's get started. First, let me introduce today's agenda.
Before we begin, does anyone have any additions to the agenda?
```

### 안건 소개 (Introducing Agenda)
```
Today we'll be discussing three main topics:
First, we'll review...
Second, we'll look at...
Finally, we'll discuss...
```

### 의견 요청 (Asking for Opinions)
```
What are your thoughts on this?
[Name], would you like to share your perspective?
Does anyone have any concerns or questions?
I'd like to hear everyone's input on this matter.
```

### 논의 진행 (Facilitating Discussion)
```
That's an interesting point. Let's explore that further.
Let's hear from others who haven't had a chance to speak yet.
Can we move on to the next item on the agenda?
Let's table that discussion for now and come back to it later.
```

### 의견 표현 (Expressing Opinions)
```
In my opinion/view...
From my perspective...
I think/believe that...
Based on my experience...
If I may suggest...

I agree/disagree with [Name] because...
That's a valid point, but have we considered...?
I see what you're saying, however...
```

### 명확히 하기 (Clarifying)
```
Could you clarify what you mean by...?
Just to make sure I understand correctly...
Are you suggesting that...?
Let me rephrase that to ensure we're all on the same page.
```

### 합의 도출 (Reaching Consensus)
```
It seems we all agree that...
Can we all support this decision?
Let's vote on this. All in favor?
Is anyone opposed to this plan?
```

### 회의 마무리 (Closing)
```
To summarize, we've decided to...
The action items are as follows...
[Name] will be responsible for [task] by [date].
Thank you all for your participation and valuable input.
Our next meeting will be on [date].
```

## 2️⃣ 회의 시나리오 (20분)

### 시나리오 1: 프로젝트 킥오프 미팅
```
Manager: Good morning, team. Thank you all for being here. 
        Today we're officially kicking off the new product 
        launch project. Let me start by outlining our goals.

Sarah: Before we dive in, could we clarify the timeline?

Manager: Good question. The target launch date is September 
        15th, which gives us 12 weeks. Let's break down the 
        major milestones. First, Sarah, could you talk about 
        the design phase?

Sarah: Certainly. Design will take 4 weeks. We'll need input 
      from marketing by week 2 to ensure alignment.

Tom (Marketing): That works for us. We can have initial concepts 
               ready by then.

Manager: Excellent. Now, regarding budget allocation...
```

### 시나리오 2: 문제 해결 회의
```
Manager: We've gathered because the client reported quality issues. 
        Let's diagnose the problem. What have we found so far?

QA Lead: Our investigation shows the issue originated in the 
        manufacturing process. Specifically, [technical details].

Manager: What's the impact? How many units are affected?

QA Lead: Approximately 500 units. We've already halted production.

Sales Rep: This is concerning. The client is considering canceling 
          their order. We need a solution immediately.

Manager: I understand the urgency. What are our options?

Engineer: We can fix this by [solution]. It will take 3 days.

Manager: That's acceptable. Let's implement that solution. Sales, 
        please communicate this timeline to the client and offer 
        a discount as compensation.

Sales Rep: Will do. I'll reach out to them this afternoon.
```

### 시나리오 3: 전략 회의
```
CEO: Thank you all for coming. Our sales have plateaued, and 
    we need to discuss strategies for growth. I'd like to hear 
    your ideas.

Marketing Director: We should increase our digital marketing 
                   budget by 30%. Our online presence is weak.

CFO: That's a significant investment. What's the expected ROI?

Marketing Director: Based on industry benchmarks, we project a 
                   25% increase in leads within 6 months.

Sales Director: I think we should also focus on expanding to 
               new markets. The Asia-Pacific region shows 
               promise.

CEO: Both ideas have merit. However, given our budget constraints, 
    we need to prioritize. Let's evaluate both proposals and 
    reconvene next week with detailed cost-benefit analyses.

All: Agreed.
```
