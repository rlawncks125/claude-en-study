## 1️⃣ 협상 영어 (20분)

### 협상 단계 및 표현

#### 1) Opening - 관계 형성
```
It's a pleasure to meet you.
Thank you for taking the time to meet with us.
I'm looking forward to a productive discussion.
I hope we can reach a mutually beneficial agreement.
```

#### 2) Presenting Position - 입장 제시
```
We're proposing...
Our position is that...
We believe that...
From our perspective...
We value... and therefore suggest...
```

#### 3) Exploring Options - 옵션 탐색
```
What if we...?
Have you considered...?
Would you be open to...?
Another option might be...
Let's explore different possibilities.
```

#### 4) Making Concessions - 양보하기
```
We could be flexible on...
If you can..., we would be willing to...
We're prepared to...
In exchange for..., we could...
```

#### 5) Addressing Concerns - 우려 해결
```
I understand your concern about...
Let me address that issue...
What if we added...?
We can guarantee...
```

#### 6) Closing - 합의 도출
```
So, we've agreed on...
Let's summarize what we've decided...
I think this is a fair agreement for both parties.
Shall we move forward with this plan?
I'll have the contract prepared.
```

### 협상 전략 표현

#### 강경한 입장
```
That's not acceptable to us.
We need to see movement on this issue.
This is our bottom line.
We can't go lower than...
Take it or leave it.
```

#### 유연한 입장
```
We're willing to discuss that.
There may be some room for negotiation.
Let's see if we can find common ground.
We want to work with you on this.
```

#### 시간 요청
```
We need some time to consider this.
Let me consult with my team.
Can we revisit this tomorrow?
I'd like to review the numbers before committing.
```

## 2️⃣ 네트워킹 영어 (15분)

### 자기소개
```
Hi, I'm [Name]. I work in [industry/field].
Nice to meet you. What brings you here today?
I'm with [Company]. We specialize in [area].
What line of work are you in?
```

### 스몰톡 (Small Talk)
```
Weather:
- Beautiful weather we're having, isn't it?
- Can you believe this heat/cold?

Event:
- Are you enjoying the conference?
- Have you attended any interesting sessions?
- What did you think of the keynote speaker?

Background:
- How long have you been in this industry?
- Where are you based?
- How's business these days?
```

### 대화 유지
```
Showing interest:
- That's interesting. Tell me more about...
- Really? How did that come about?
- I'd love to hear more about...

Finding common ground:
- We have something in common. I also...
- That reminds me of...
- I've had a similar experience with...

Sharing:
- In my experience...
- At my company, we...
- I recently worked on...
```

### 연락처 교환
```
It's been great talking with you.
I'd love to continue this conversation. 
Do you have a business card?
Could we connect on LinkedIn?
Perhaps we could schedule a call to discuss this further?
Let's stay in touch.
```

### 대화 종료
```
It was a pleasure meeting you.
I hope we can work together in the future.
Enjoy the rest of the event!
I'll reach out to you next week.
```

## 3️⃣ 협상 시나리오 (10분)

### 시나리오: 가격 협상
```
Buyer: Thank you for the proposal. However, the price of $100 
      per unit is higher than our budget. We were hoping for 
      $85.

Seller: I appreciate your feedback. While $85 is below our cost 
       threshold, I'd like to understand your needs better. What 
       quantity are you looking to order?

Buyer: We're planning to order 1,000 units initially, with 
      potential for ongoing orders if the partnership works out.

Seller: That's significant volume. Given the potential for a 
       long-term partnership, here's what I can offer: For an 
       initial order of 1,000 units, we could do $92 per unit. 
       If you commit to quarterly orders, we could reduce that 
       to $90.

Buyer: That's closer, but still a bit high. What if we committed 
      to 2,000 units for the first order?

Seller: For 2,000 units with a one-year commitment, I could offer 
       $88 per unit. That's the best I can do while maintaining 
       quality standards.

Buyer: If you can include free shipping and extend payment terms 
      to 60 days, we have a deal at $88.

Seller: I can agree to 45-day payment terms and free shipping for 
       orders over $50,000. Given your volume, that would apply.

Buyer: That works for us. Let's move forward.

Seller: Excellent. I'll have the contract prepared by tomorrow.
```

## 4️⃣ 네트워킹 시나리오 (5분)

### 시나리오: 컨퍼런스에서
```
A: Hi, I don't think we've met. I'm Sarah from ABC Tech.

B: Nice to meet you, Sarah. I'm John from XYZ Solutions. 
   How are you finding the conference?

A: It's been great so far. The session on AI was particularly 
   interesting. Did you attend it?

B: I did! The speaker made some excellent points about 
   implementation challenges.

A: Absolutely. We're actually facing some of those challenges 
   at my company. What does XYZ Solutions do?

B: We provide consulting services for digital transformation. 
   It sounds like we might have some overlap with your needs.

A: That's interesting. We're in the process of upgrading our 
   systems. Would you be open to a call next week to discuss?

B: Definitely. Let me give you my card.

A: Great. I'll send you an email to set up a time. It was nice 
   meeting you, John.

B: Likewise, Sarah. Look forward to our call!
```
