## 1️⃣ 전화 영어 기본 (10분)

### 전화 받기
```
Answering:
- Good morning/afternoon, [Company Name], [Your Name] speaking. How may I help you?
- Thank you for calling [Company]. This is [Name]. How can I assist you today?

Taking a message:
- I'm afraid he/she is not available at the moment. May I take a message?
- Would you like to leave a message?
- Could I have your name and number, please?

Transferring:
- Let me transfer you to [department/person].
- Please hold while I connect you.
- I'll put you through to [Name].
```

### 전화 걸기
```
Introducing yourself:
- Hello, this is [Name] calling from [Company].
- Good morning/afternoon, my name is [Name] from [Company].

Stating purpose:
- I'm calling regarding...
- The reason for my call is...
- I'd like to speak to [Name] about...

Requesting:
- Could I speak to [Name], please?
- Is [Name] available?
- When would be a good time to call back?
```

### 통화 중 유용한 표현
```
Asking to repeat:
- I'm sorry, could you repeat that, please?
- Could you speak a bit more slowly, please?
- I didn't quite catch that. Could you say it again?

Confirming:
- Let me confirm that... (repeat information)
- Just to make sure I understand correctly...
- So, you're saying that...

Ending the call:
- Thank you for calling. Have a great day.
- It was nice speaking with you.
- I'll follow up with an email.
```

## 2️⃣ 화상회의 영어 (15분)

### 회의 시작
```
Opening:
- Good morning/afternoon, everyone. Thank you for joining.
- Let's get started. Can everyone see/hear me okay?
- Before we begin, let me do a quick roll call.

Technical check:
- Can you hear me alright?
- Is my screen visible to everyone?
- Please mute your microphones when not speaking.
```

### 회의 중 표현
```
Getting attention:
- Excuse me, may I add something?
- If I could just jump in here...
- Sorry to interrupt, but...

Asking for opinions:
- What do you think, [Name]?
- [Name], would you like to share your thoughts?
- Does anyone have any questions or comments?

Agreeing/Disagreeing:
- I completely agree with [Name].
- That's a good point, but have we considered...?
- I see your point, however...

Clarifying:
- Just to clarify...
- What do you mean by...?
- Could you elaborate on that?
```

### 회의 마무리
```
Summarizing:
- Let me summarize the key points...
- To recap, we've decided to...
- The action items are...

Next steps:
- [Name] will handle [task] by [date].
- We'll reconvene on [date] to review progress.
- I'll send out meeting minutes by end of day.

Closing:
- Thank you all for your time and input.
- If there are no further questions, let's conclude.
- Have a great rest of your day, everyone.
```

## 3️⃣ 5가지 전화 시나리오 (20분)

### 시나리오 1: 약속 잡기
```
Caller: Good morning, this is Sarah Kim from ABC Company. 
       May I speak to Mr. Johnson, please?

Receptionist: Good morning, Ms. Kim. I'm afraid Mr. Johnson 
             is in a meeting at the moment. May I take a message?

Caller: Yes, please. I'm calling to schedule a meeting to 
       discuss our proposal. Could you ask him to call me 
       back when he's available?

Receptionist: Certainly. May I have your contact number?

Caller: It's 02-1234-5678. He also has my email.

Receptionist: Perfect. I'll make sure he gets your message.

Caller: Thank you very much. Have a good day.
```

### 시나리오 2: 정보 확인
```
Employee: Hello, this is John Park from XYZ Corp. I'm calling 
         to confirm the delivery schedule for order #12345.

Supplier: One moment, please. Let me pull up that order... 
         Yes, order 12345. The delivery is scheduled for 
         June 15th.

Employee: June 15th, got it. And will it arrive in the morning 
         or afternoon?

Supplier: The delivery window is between 9am and 12pm.

Employee: Perfect. Just to confirm - that's 100 units to our 
         Gangnam office, correct?

Supplier: That's correct. Is there anything else I can help 
         you with?

Employee: No, that's all. Thank you for your help.
```

### 시나리오 3: 문제 해결
```
Customer: Hello, I'm calling about an issue with my recent order.

Support: I'm sorry to hear that. May I have your order number?

Customer: It's OR-98765. I received the wrong item.

Support: I apologize for the inconvenience. Let me look into 
        this right away... I see the issue. We'll send the 
        correct item immediately and arrange a pickup for 
        the wrong item. No charge, of course.

Customer: How long will it take?

Support: The replacement will be shipped today and should 
        arrive within 2 business days. Is that acceptable?

Customer: Yes, that works. Thank you for resolving this quickly.

Support: You're welcome. Is there anything else I can assist 
        you with today?
```

### 시나리오 4: 가격 협상
```
Buyer: Good afternoon, I'm calling regarding your quotation 
      for 500 units. The price seems a bit higher than we 
      expected.

Seller: I understand your concern. However, this price reflects 
       the premium quality and includes free shipping. May I 
       ask what price range you had in mind?

Buyer: We were hoping for around $45 per unit.

Seller: I see. While I can't go that low for 500 units, if you 
       could increase your order to 1,000 units, I could offer 
       $47 per unit.

Buyer: Let me discuss this with my team and get back to you.

Seller: Of course. Please feel free to call me anytime. My 
       direct line is...
```

### 시나리오 5: 화상회의 - 프로젝트 업데이트
```
Manager: Good morning, everyone. Let's start with a quick 
        update on the project status. Sarah, could you begin?

Sarah: Sure. We've completed phase one and are now 60% through 
      phase two. The timeline is on track.

Manager: Excellent. Any roadblocks?

Tom: Actually, we're facing a technical issue with the 
    integration. It might delay us by a few days.

Manager: I see. What support do you need?

Tom: If we could get assistance from the IT team, we could 
    resolve it faster.

Manager: Understood. I'll coordinate with IT today. Anything 
        else we need to discuss?

All: No, that's all.

Manager: Great. Let's reconvene next Monday. Have a productive 
        week, everyone.
```

## 4️⃣ 전화/화상회의 에티켓 (5분)

### DO's ✅
```
✅ 조용한 장소에서 통화
✅ 명확하고 천천히 말하기
✅ 적극적으로 경청
✅ 핵심 정보 메모하기
✅ 시간 엄수
✅ 배경 소음 최소화
✅ 전문적인 배경 (화상회의)
```

### DON'Ts ❌
```
❌ 동시에 여러 작업하기 (멀티태스킹)
❌ 다른 사람 말 자르기
❌ 소음 많은 곳에서 통화
❌ 비디오 끄고 장시간 침묵
❌ 회의 중 이메일 확인
❌ 부적절한 배경이나 복장
```
