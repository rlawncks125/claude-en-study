## 📚 오늘의 학습 목표
- 10가지 비즈니스 이메일 유형 마스터하기

## 1️⃣ 비즈니스 이메일 기본 구조 (5분)

### 표준 이메일 형식
```
Subject: [명확하고 구체적인 제목]

Dear [Name], / Hello [Name],

[인사 및 목적]
I hope this email finds you well.
I am writing to...

[본문 - 2-3 문단]
[핵심 내용을 명확하게]

[마무리 및 행동 요청]
Please let me know if you have any questions.
I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards, / Kind regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Position]
[Contact Information]
```

## 2️⃣ 10가지 이메일 유형 및 템플릿 (30분)

### 1) 첫 연락 이메일 (First Contact)
```
Subject: Introduction - [Your Company] Partnership Opportunity

Dear Mr./Ms. [Name],

I hope this email finds you well. My name is [Your Name], and I am 
the [Your Position] at [Your Company].

I am reaching out to introduce our company and explore potential 
partnership opportunities. We specialize in [brief description], 
and I believe there could be synergies between our organizations.

Would you be available for a brief call next week to discuss this 
further? I am flexible with timing and happy to work around your 
schedule.

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to the possibility of 
working together.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**핵심 표현**:
- I am reaching out to...
- I believe there could be...
- Would you be available for...?
- I look forward to...

### 2) 회의 요청 (Meeting Request)
```
Subject: Meeting Request - [Topic] Discussion

Dear [Name],

I hope you are doing well. I would like to schedule a meeting to 
discuss [specific topic/project].

The meeting would take approximately [duration], and I propose the 
following times:
- Option 1: [Date] at [Time]
- Option 2: [Date] at [Time]
- Option 3: [Date] at [Time]

Please let me know which time works best for you, or suggest an 
alternative if none of these are convenient.

The agenda will include:
1. [Topic 1]
2. [Topic 2]
3. [Topic 3]

Looking forward to our discussion.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**핵심 표현**:
- I would like to schedule...
- The meeting would take approximately...
- Please let me know which time works best...

### 3) 정보 요청 (Information Request)
```
Subject: Request for Information - [Specific Topic]

Dear [Name],

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to request information 
about [specific topic/product/service].

Specifically, I would like to know:
1. [Question 1]
2. [Question 2]
3. [Question 3]

This information will help us [purpose/reason]. If possible, I would 
appreciate receiving this by [date] as we need to [reason for deadline].

Thank you in advance for your assistance. Please let me know if you 
need any additional details from my end.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 4) 견적 요청 (Quotation Request)
```
Subject: Quotation Request for [Product/Service]

Dear [Name],

I hope you are well. We are interested in [product/service] and would 
like to request a quotation.

Our requirements are as follows:
- Item: [Description]
- Quantity: [Number]
- Delivery Date: [Date]
- Delivery Location: [Address]

Could you please provide:
1. Unit price and total cost
2. Payment terms
3. Delivery schedule
4. Warranty information

We would appreciate receiving your quotation by [date]. If you need 
any clarification, please don't hesitate to contact me.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 5) 제안 이메일 (Proposal Email)
```
Subject: Proposal for [Project/Partnership]

Dear [Name],

Following our recent conversation, I am pleased to submit our proposal 
for [project/service].

Our proposal includes:
1. [Key point 1]
2. [Key point 2]
3. [Key point 3]

The proposed timeline is [timeframe], and the investment required is 
[amount]. We believe this solution will [benefit/result].

I have attached a detailed proposal document for your review. I would 
be happy to schedule a call to discuss any questions or concerns you 
may have.

I look forward to the opportunity to work with you on this project.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

Attachment: [Proposal_Document.pdf]
```

### 6) 감사 이메일 (Thank You Email)
```
Subject: Thank You - [Event/Meeting/Assistance]

Dear [Name],

I wanted to take a moment to thank you for [specific action/meeting/help].

Your [assistance/insights/time] was incredibly valuable, and I 
particularly appreciated [specific detail]. The information you 
provided will be very helpful as we [next steps].

If there is anything I can do to assist you in return, please don't 
hesitate to let me know. I look forward to continuing our collaboration.

Thank you once again.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 7) 팔로우업 이메일 (Follow-up Email)
```
Subject: Following Up - [Previous Topic]

Dear [Name],

I hope this email finds you well. I wanted to follow up on my previous 
email sent on [date] regarding [topic].

I understand you may be busy, but I wanted to check if you had a 
chance to review my request/proposal. [Briefly restate key points]

If you need any additional information or have any questions, I would 
be happy to provide clarification.

I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 8) 사과 이메일 (Apology Email)
```
Subject: Apology for [Issue/Delay]

Dear [Name],

I am writing to sincerely apologize for [specific issue]. I understand 
that this has caused [impact], and I take full responsibility.

To address this situation, we have:
1. [Action taken 1]
2. [Action taken 2]
3. [Action taken 3]

We are committed to ensuring this does not happen again, and we have 
implemented [preventive measures].

If you have any concerns or if there is anything else we can do to 
rectify this situation, please let me know.

Thank you for your understanding and patience.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
```

### 9) 거절/거부 이메일 (Declining Email)
```
Subject: RE: [Original Subject]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your [proposal/invitation/offer]. I appreciate you 
thinking of us/me.

After careful consideration, we have decided to [decline/not proceed] 
at this time due to [brief, diplomatic reason].

However, we value our relationship and would like to keep the door 
open for future opportunities. Please feel free to contact us if you 
have other proposals that might be a better fit.

Thank you again for your understanding.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 10) 확인 이메일 (Confirmation Email)
```
Subject: Confirmation - [Meeting/Order/Arrangement]

Dear [Name],

I am writing to confirm [meeting/order/arrangement] as follows:

Details:
- Date: [Date]
- Time: [Time]
- Location/Platform: [Address/Zoom/Teams]
- Participants: [Names]
- Agenda: [Topics]

Please confirm that these details are correct. If you need to make 
any changes, please let me know as soon as possible.

I look forward to [meeting/receiving/etc.].

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## 3️⃣ 이메일 작성 팁 (5분)

### DO's ✅
```
✅ 명확하고 구체적인 제목 사용
✅ 간결하고 요점 있게 작성 (3문단 이내)
✅ 전문적이고 정중한 어조 유지
✅ 행동 요청 명확히 하기 (call to action)
✅ 발송 전 철자와 문법 확인
✅ 24-48시간 내 응답
```

### DON'Ts ❌
```
❌ 너무 길거나 복잡한 문장
❌ 비격식적 언어나 슬랭
❌ 전체 대문자 사용 (소리지르는 것처럼 보임)
❌ 과도한 느낌표 사용
❌ 중요한 정보 누락
❌ 감정적인 표현
```

### 유용한 표현
```
시작:
- I hope this email finds you well.
- Thank you for your email regarding...
- Further to our conversation...

본문:
- I am writing to inquire about...
- I would like to bring to your attention...
- Please find attached...
- As discussed...

마무리:
- Please let me know if you need any further information.
- I look forward to hearing from you.
- Thank you for your time and consideration.
- Should you have any questions, please feel free to contact me.
```
