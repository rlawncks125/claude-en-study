## 1️⃣ Week 3 문법 총정리 (15분)

### Day 1: 수동태
```
형태: be + 과거분사

현재: is/are made
과거: was/were launched  
미래: will be completed
완료: has/have been sold
진행: is being reviewed
조동사: should be checked

The product was launched in 2020.
The meeting will be held next week.
```

### Day 2: 관계대명사
```
who (사람), which (사물), that (모두)
whose (소유), where (장소), when (시간)

The person who called is the CEO.
The product which we sell is popular.
The company whose CEO is young...
The office where I work is in Seoul.
```

### Day 3-4: 가정법
```
1st (가능): If you order, we will give...
2nd (비현실): If I were you, I would...
3rd (과거 후회): If I had known, I would have...
```

### Day 4: 간접화법
```
시제 한 단계 과거로:
직접: "I am busy."
간접: He said he was busy.

say (사람 없음), tell (사람 필수), ask (질문)
```

## 3️⃣ 통합 실전 예시 (10분)

### 비즈니스 보고서 (모든 문법 통합)
```
Annual Performance Report

The company has achieved significant growth this year.
Sales have been increased by 30%, which exceeds our target.
The product that was launched in March has been very
successful. If we continue this trend, we will reach
$10M in revenue next year.

According to customer feedback, 85% of clients who
purchased our products were satisfied. The survey showed
that quality was the most important factor. Several
customers suggested that we should expand our service
offering.

The factory where our products are manufactured has
received ISO certification. This achievement, which was
announced last month, demonstrates our commitment to quality.

If we had invested more in marketing earlier, we would
have achieved these results sooner. However, the current
strategy has been proven effective.

The CEO said that he was very pleased with the team's
performance. He told us that bonuses would be distributed
next month. He also asked if we needed additional resources
for the next quarter.

In conclusion, this has been a successful year. If we
maintain our current momentum, the future looks promising.
```

## 4️⃣ 종합 연습 문제 (10분)

### 문제 1: 적절한 문법 선택
1. The report _____ (submit) yesterday. (수동태)
2. The person _____ designed this is on our team. (관계대명사)
3. If you _____ (order) 100 units, we will give a discount. (1st)
4. He said he _____ (be) busy. (간접화법)

### 문제 2: 오류 수정
1. The meeting will held next week.
2. The person which I met was the CEO.
3. If you will need help, call me.
4. He said me that he was busy.

---

## 📝 정답

### 문제 1
1. was submitted
2. who/that
3. order
4. was

### 문제 2
1. will be held (수동태)
2. who/that (사람)
3. If you need (if절에 will 불가)
4. told me / said (say는 사람 불필요)
