# Week 3 - Day 2 (화요일): 관계대명사

## 📚 오늘의 학습 목표
- 관계대명사로 두 문장 연결하기
- who, which, that, whose, where, when 사용법 익히기
- 복잡하고 정확한 문장 만들기
- 제품/서비스를 상세하게 설명하기

**학습 시간**: 45분

---

## 1️⃣ 관계대명사 핵심 (10분)

### 기본 개념
**두 문장을 하나로 연결**하여 명사를 더 자세히 설명

```
두 문장: The manager works here. He is very experienced.
연결: The manager who works here is very experienced.
```

### 주요 관계대명사

| 관계대명사 | 선행사 | 예시 |
|-----------|--------|------|
| **who** | 사람 | The person who called... |
| **which** | 사물 | The product which we sell... |
| **that** | 사람/사물 | The man that I met... |
| **whose** | 소유 | The company whose CEO... |
| **where** | 장소 | The office where I work... |
| **when** | 시간 | The day when we met... |

---

## 2️⃣ 관계대명사 사용법 (15분)

### Who - 사람

**주격**:
```
The employee who designed this is on our team.
(이것을 디자인한 직원은 우리 팀에 있습니다)

The manager who works here is very experienced.
(여기서 일하는 매니저는 매우 경험이 많습니다)
```

**목적격** (생략 가능):
```
The person (who) I met yesterday called me.
(어제 만난 사람이 전화했습니다)

The client (who) we hired is starting next week.
(우리가 고용한 사람은 다음 주에 시작합니다)
```

### Which - 사물

**주격**:
```
The product which won the award is very popular.
(상을 받은 제품은 매우 인기가 있습니다)

The system which was installed last year is working well.
(작년에 설치된 시스템이 잘 작동하고 있습니다)
```

**목적격** (생략 가능):
```
The report (which) I wrote was approved.
(제가 작성한 보고서가 승인되었습니다)

The software (which) we use is very efficient.
(우리가 사용하는 소프트웨어는 매우 효율적입니다)
```

### That - 사람/사물 (모두 가능)

```
The manager that works here... (= who works)
The product that we sell... (= which we sell)

최상급/all/only와 함께 선호:
This is the best product that we have.
This is the only solution that works.
```

### Whose - 소유격

```
The company whose CEO is very young is successful.
(CEO가 매우 젊은 회사가 성공적입니다)

The employee whose performance was excellent got promoted.
(성과가 뛰어난 직원이 승진했습니다)
```

### Where - 장소

```
The office where I work is in Seoul.
(제가 일하는 사무실은 서울에 있습니다)

This is the building where the meeting will be held.
(이것은 회의가 열릴 건물입니다)
```

### When - 시간

```
I remember the day when we first met.
(우리가 처음 만난 날을 기억합니다)

2020 was the year when we launched our first product.
(2020년은 우리가 첫 제품을 출시한 해였습니다)
```

## 3️⃣ 비즈니스 활용 (10분)

### 제품/서비스 설명
```
✅ We offer products that meet international standards.
✅ This is a service which helps businesses grow.
✅ The software that we developed has won several awards.
✅ Companies whose revenue exceeds $1M can apply.
```

### 사람 소개
```
✅ This is Mr. Kim, who is our new CEO.
✅ The person who will lead this project is very experienced.
✅ We hired a consultant whose expertise is in marketing.
```

### 장소/시간 설명
```
✅ The conference room where we usually meet is on the 5th floor.
✅ This is the factory where our products are made.
✅ March is the month when we launch new products.
```

### 조건/자격 설명
```
✅ Applicants who have 5+ years of experience are preferred.
✅ Projects that are completed on time receive bonuses.
✅ The team whose proposal is selected will get funding.
```

## 4️⃣ 실전 대화 (5분)

### 대화: 제품 소개
```
Customer: What kind of products do you sell?
         (어떤 종류의 제품을 판매하나요?)

Sales: We sell products that are environmentally friendly
      and made with sustainable materials. The products
      which we launched this year have been very popular
      with customers who care about the environment.
      (친환경적이고 지속 가능한 재료로 만든 제품을
      판매합니다. 올해 출시한 제품들은 환경을 생각하는
      고객들에게 매우 인기가 있었습니다.)

Customer: Where are they manufactured?
         (어디서 제조되나요?)

Sales: In the factory where we use 100% renewable energy.
      The facility, which is located in Busan, has won
      several environmental awards.
      (100% 재생 에너지를 사용하는 공장에서요.
      부산에 위치한 그 시설은 여러 환경상을 받았습니다.)
```

## 5️⃣ 연습 문제 (5분)

### 문제 1: 적절한 관계대명사 선택
1. The employee _____ designed this is very talented.
2. The product _____ we launched is selling well.
3. The company _____ CEO is very young is successful.
4. The office _____ I work is in Gangnam.
5. 2020 was the year _____ we started the company.

### 문제 2: 두 문장을 하나로 연결
1. The manager is on vacation. He works in sales.
   → _________________________________

2. The report was detailed. I wrote it yesterday.
   → _________________________________

3. I know a person. His company is very successful.
   → _________________________________

---

## 📝 정답

### 문제 1
1. who/that
2. which/that (생략 가능)
3. whose
4. where
5. when

### 문제 2
1. The manager who works in sales is on vacation.
2. The report (which/that) I wrote yesterday was detailed.
3. I know a person whose company is very successful.
