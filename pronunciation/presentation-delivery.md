# 프레젠테이션 전달 발음 가이드

## Introduction | 소개

Effective presentation delivery goes beyond correct pronunciation. This guide teaches you how to use your voice as a tool for professional communication, with specific techniques for Korean speakers delivering English business presentations.

효과적인 프레젠테이션 전달은 올바른 발음을 넘어섭니다. 이 가이드는 영어 비즈니스 프레젠테이션을 하는 한국인 화자를 위한 구체적인 기법과 함께 목소리를 전문적 의사소통의 도구로 사용하는 방법을 가르칩니다.

## Part 1: Speaking Pace and Rhythm | 말하기 속도와 리듬

### 1.1 Understanding Optimal Pace | 최적 속도 이해하기

**Common Problem for Korean Speakers:**
Korean speakers often speak either too fast (nervousness) or too slowly (overthinking pronunciation).

**한국인의 흔한 문제:**
한국인 화자는 너무 빠르게(긴장) 또는 너무 느리게(발음 과도한 신경) 말하는 경향이 있습니다.

**Optimal Speaking Rate:**
- **Conversational:** 140-160 words per minute
- **Presentations:** 120-150 words per minute
- **Key points:** 100-120 words per minute (slower for emphasis)

**최적 속도:**
- **대화:** 분당 140-160 단어
- **프레젠테이션:** 분당 120-150 단어
- **핵심 포인트:** 분당 100-120 단어 (강조를 위해 느리게)

### 1.2 Rhythm Patterns in English | 영어의 리듬 패턴

**Key Concept: Stress-Timed Language**
English is stress-timed (stressed syllables occur at regular intervals), while Korean is syllable-timed (each syllable gets equal time).

**핵심 개념: 강세박자 언어**
영어는 강세박자 언어 (강세 음절이 규칙적인 간격으로 발생), 한국어는 음절박자 언어 (각 음절이 동일한 시간)입니다.

**English Rhythm Pattern:**
```
Content Words (Stressed):
- Nouns: report, strategy, profit
- Main verbs: increase, analyze, implement
- Adjectives: important, significant, crucial
- Adverbs: quickly, effectively, clearly
- Question words: what, when, where, why, how
- Negative words: not, never, no

Function Words (Unstressed):
- Articles: a, an, the
- Prepositions: in, on, at, to, for
- Pronouns: I, you, we, they, it
- Auxiliary verbs: am, is, are, was, were
- Conjunctions: and, but, or
```

**Practice Examples:**
```
Sentence: "The SALES team NEEDS to FOCUS on KEY METRICS."
Strong: SALES, NEEDS, FOCUS, KEY, METRICS
Weak: The, team, to, on

Sentence: "We MUST ANALYZE the DATA CAREFULLY."
Strong: MUST, ANALYZE, DATA, CAREFULLY
Weak: We, the
```

### 1.3 Pace Variation for Impact | 영향력을 위한 속도 변화

**Technique: Vary your pace strategically**

```
Introduction (Medium pace):
"Good morning, everyone. Today, I'm going to present our quarterly results."
(140-150 wpm - establishes presence)

Important Point (Slow pace):
"Our revenue... increased... by thirty-five percent."
(100-120 wpm - creates emphasis)

Supporting Details (Normal-Fast pace):
"This growth was driven by our new product line, expanded market reach, and improved customer retention strategies."
(150-160 wpm - shows confidence)

Transition (Medium pace):
"Now, let's move on to our cost analysis."
(140-150 wpm - maintains flow)
```

**Korean Speakers: Common Mistakes | 한국인 화자: 흔한 실수**

❌ **Mistake 1:** Speaking each word with equal emphasis
```
Wrong: "We-need-to-in-crease-our-mar-ket-share"
(Equal stress on each syllable)
```

✅ **Correct:** Using natural stress pattern
```
Right: "We NEED to inCREASE our MARket SHARE"
(Stress on content words)
```

❌ **Mistake 2:** Rushing through nervousness
```
Wrong: "TodayI'mgoingtopresentourquarterlyresultswhichshowsignificantgrowthinrevenue"
(Too fast, words run together)
```

✅ **Correct:** Appropriate pacing with pauses
```
Right: "Today [pause] I'm going to present our quarterly results, [pause] which show significant growth in revenue."
(120-140 wpm with strategic pauses)
```

## Part 2: Emphasis and Stress Techniques | 강조와 강세 기법

### 2.1 Word-Level Stress | 단어 수준 강세

**Multi-Syllable Word Stress Rules:**

```
Word              Stress Pattern    Use in Sentence
PRESent (n)       ●○                "The PRESent shows our data."
preSENT (v)       ○●                "Let me preSENT the findings."

PROject (n)       ●○                "This PROject is crucial."
proJECT (v)       ○●                "We proJECT 20% growth."

INcrease (n)      ●○                "We see an INcrease in sales."
inCREASE (v)      ○●                "Sales will inCREASE next quarter."

PROgress (n)      ●○                "The PROgress is excellent."
proGRESS (v)      ○●                "We will proGRESS rapidly."
```

**Practice Sentences:**
```
1. "The PROject will inCREASE our REVenue by Twenty PERcent."
   (●○ ○● ●○○ ●○ ○●)

2. "Our STRATegy FOcuses on CUStomer SATisfaction."
   (●○○ ●○○ ●○○ ○○●○)

3. "We NEED to ANalyze the DATA more CAREfully."
   (● ○ ●○○ ●○ ●○○)
```

### 2.2 Sentence-Level Stress | 문장 수준 강세

**Technique: Contrastive Stress**

Change meaning by changing which word you stress:

```
1. "WE need to increase revenue." (Not them, but us)
   우리가 수익을 늘려야 합니다. (다른 사람이 아니라)

2. "We NEED to increase revenue." (It's necessary)
   우리는 반드시 수익을 늘려야 합니다. (필요성 강조)

3. "We need to INCREASE revenue." (Not decrease)
   우리는 수익을 늘려야 합니다. (줄이는 게 아니라)

4. "We need to increase REVENUE." (Not costs)
   우리는 수익을 늘려야 합니다. (비용이 아니라)
```

**New Information Stress:**

Stress words that contain NEW information:

```
Q: "What did sales do last quarter?"
A: "Sales INCREASED by 15%."
   (New info: increased, 15%)

Q: "By how much did sales increase?"
A: "Sales increased by FIFTEEN percent."
   (New info: fifteen percent)

Q: "Which team exceeded their target?"
A: "The MARKETING team exceeded their target."
   (New info: marketing team)
```

### 2.3 Emphasis for Persuasion | 설득을 위한 강조

**Power Words - Always Emphasize:**

```
Positive Impact:
- SIGnificant INcrease
- REmarkable GROWTH  - EXcellent RESults
- OUTstanding PERformance
- IMpressive AchieveVment

Urgency:
- IMmediately
- CRItical
- URgent
- ESSential
- MUST (strongest modal verb)

Certainty:
- DEFinitely
- CERtainly
- ABsolutely
- GUAranteed
- WILL (strong future)
```

**Emphasis Technique: Three-Part Rule**

Make your point three times with increasing emphasis:

```
"This is important. [pause] This is REALLY important. [pause] This is ABSOLUTELY CRITICAL."

"We need to act. [pause] We need to act SOON. [pause] We need to act NOW."

"Our strategy works. [pause] Our strategy REALLY works. [pause] Our strategy CONSISTENTLY delivers results."
```

### 2.4 De-emphasis: What NOT to Stress | 비강세: 강조하지 말아야 할 것

**Korean Speakers Often Wrongly Emphasize:**

❌ Don't stress function words unless contrasting:
```
Wrong: "THE report IS on THE table."
Right: "The rePORT is on the TAble."

Wrong: "WE ARE going TO analyze THE data."
Right: "We're going to ANalyze the DAta."
```

❌ Don't stress every word equally:
```
Wrong: "THIS-YEAR-WE-WILL-GROW-BY-THIR-TY-PER-CENT"
Right: "This YEAR we will GROW by THIRTY perCENT."
```

### 2.5 Practice Exercises: Emphasis | 강조 연습

**Exercise 1: Stress Shifting**

Read each sentence 4 times, stressing a different word each time:

```

1. OUR team achieved excellent results this quarter. (우리 팀이)
2. Our TEAM achieved excellent results this quarter. (팀이)
3. Our team achieved EXCELLENT results this quarter. (훌륭한)
4. Our team achieved excellent RESULTS this quarter. (결과를)
5. Our team achieved excellent results THIS quarter. (이번)
```

**Exercise 2: Question-Answer Stress**

Practice with a partner or record yourself:

```

A: "The project will be complete in JUNE." (June = new info)

Q: "Which project will be complete in June?"
A: "The ALPHA project will be complete in June." (Alpha = new info)

Q: "Will the Alpha project be complete in May?"
A: "No, it will be complete in JUNE." (Contrastive stress on June)
```

## Part 3: Strategic Pausing | 전략적 쉼

### 3.1 Types of Pauses | 쉼의 종류

**1. Grammatical Pauses (기본 쉼)**

```
After introductory phrases:
"First of all, [pause] let me explain our strategy."

Between clauses:
"We analyzed the data, [pause] and the results were impressive."

After transitions:
"However, [pause] we face some challenges."

Before important points:
"The most critical factor is [pause] customer satisfaction."
```

**2. Dramatic Pauses (극적 쉼)**

```
Before key numbers:
"Our revenue increased by [2-second pause] thirty-five percent."

Before surprising information:
"And the winner of this quarter's award is [2-second pause] the Seoul team."

After rhetorical questions:
"What makes us different from competitors? [2-second pause] Innovation."
```

**3. Processing Pauses (이해 쉼)**

```
After complex information:
"Our EBITDA margin improved from 12.3% to 18.7% year-over-year. [3-second pause]
This represents significant operational efficiency."

After lists:
"We expanded into Japan, Korea, Singapore, and Thailand. [2-second pause]
Each market showed strong growth potential."
```

### 3.2 Pause Duration Guidelines | 쉼 지속 시간 안내

```
Comma pause:           0.5-1 second
Period pause:          1-2 seconds
Paragraph break:       2-3 seconds
Major transition:      3-4 seconds
Dramatic effect:       2-4 seconds (silence is powerful!)

Example paragraph with pauses:

"Good morning everyone. [2-second pause]
Today I'm excited to share our quarterly results. [1-second pause]
As you'll see, [0.5-second pause] we've exceeded all our targets. [2-second pause]
Let's begin with revenue. [3-second pause - major transition]"
```

### 3.3 Common Pausing Mistakes | 흔한 쉼 실수

**Korean Speakers Often:**

❌ **Mistake 1:** No pauses (run-on speaking)
```
Wrong: "GoodmorningeveroneI'mpleasedtopresentourresultstodaywestartedthisprojectlastyearanditwasverychallenginbutwesucceeded"

Right: "Good morning everyone. [pause] I'm pleased to present our results today. [pause] We started this project last year, [pause] and it was very challenging, [pause] but we succeeded."
```

❌ **Mistake 2:** Filling pauses with "uh", "um", "어", etc.
```
Wrong: "Our strategy, um, focuses on, uh, customer satisfaction, 어, and, um, innovation."

Right: "Our strategy [pause] focuses on customer satisfaction [pause] and innovation."
```

❌ **Mistake 3:** Pausing mid-phrase
```
Wrong: "We need to [pause] increase our [pause] market share."

Right: "We need to increase [pause] our market share."
```

### 3.4 Practice Exercises: Pausing | 쉼 연습

**Exercise 1: Pause Placement**

```

```

```

```

**Exercise 3: Filler Word Elimination**

Record yourself speaking for 2 minutes on a business topic. Count your filler words (um, uh, like, 어, 그). Practice again, replacing each filler with a brief pause.

## Part 4: Voice Projection and Modulation | 음성 투사와 조절

### 4.1 Voice Projection Basics | 음성 투사 기초

**Understanding Projection vs. Shouting:**
- Projection: Speaking from your diaphragm, clear and carrying
- Shouting: Speaking from your throat, strained and harsh

**투사 vs. 고함 이해:**
- 투사: 횡격막에서 말하기, 명확하고 전달력 있음
- 고함: 목에서 말하기, 긴장되고 거친 소리

**Diaphragmatic Breathing Technique:**

```
1. Place hand on stomach
2. Breathe in deeply - stomach should expand
3. Speak while slowly releasing air
4. Your voice should feel supported, not strained

횡격막 호흡 기법:
1. 배에 손을 올리기
2. 깊게 숨쉬기 - 배가 팽창해야 함
3. 천천히 공기를 내보내며 말하기
4. 목소리가 지지되는 느낌, 긴장되지 않음
```

**Projection Practice:**

```
Quiet room: "Good morning, everyone."
(Conversational volume, relaxed)

Medium room: "GOOD morning, EVERYone."
(Project to back row, more diaphragm support)

Large hall: "GOOD MORning, EVeryONE!"
(Full projection, strong diaphragm support, clear enunciation)
```

### 4.2 Voice Modulation for Engagement | 참여를 위한 음성 조절

**Vary Your Pitch:**

```
High Pitch: Excitement, questions, emphasis
"This is FANTASTIC news!" ↗
"Can you believe these results?" ↗

Mid Pitch: Normal delivery, facts
"Our revenue was $5 million." →

Low Pitch: Seriousness, authority, conclusions
"We must address this immediately." ↘
```

**Korean Speakers: Pitch Challenges | 한국인 화자: 음높이 어려움**

Korean is a relatively flat-pitched language. English uses wider pitch variation:

```
Korean tendency: "We-achieved-our-target" (flat, minimal variation)
English pattern: "We aCHIEVED our TARget!" (varied pitch on stressed syllables)

Korean tendency: "This-is-important" (flat)
English pattern: "This is imPORtant!" (rising pitch on stressed syllable)
```

### 4.3 Volume Control Techniques | 볼륨 조절 기법

**Strategic Volume Variation:**

```
LOUD (강하게):
- Opening statements: "GOOD MORNING EVERYONE!"
- Key points: "This is CRITICALLY important!"
- Call to action: "Let's make this HAPPEN!"

Medium (보통):
- Normal delivery: "Our strategy focuses on three areas."
- Explanations: "Let me explain the process."

Soft (부드럽게):
- Sharing concerns: "I must admit, this is challenging."
- Creating intimacy: "Let me share something personal."
- Building suspense: "And the result was..." (then pause)

VERY SOFT then LOUD (극적 효과):
- "We were down to our last chance. [soft] And then... [pause] we SUCCEEDED!" [loud]
```

### 4.4 Energy and Enthusiasm | 에너지와 열정

**Techniques to Sound Engaged:**

```
1. Smile while speaking (changes voice tone)
   "I'm THRILLED to share these results!" (smile genuinely)

2. Use hand gestures (naturally increases vocal energy)
   "We grew by THIS much!" (hands showing large gap)

3. Lean forward slightly (shows engagement)
   "This next point is crucial." (lean in)

4. Make eye contact (connects with audience)
   Scan the room, pause on individuals

5. Vary your energy levels
   High: "This is EXCITING!"
   Medium: "Let me explain the details."
   Low: "We face some challenges."
   Back to High: "But we WILL overcome them!"
```

**Korean Speakers: Cultural Adjustment | 한국인 화자: 문화적 조정**

Korean business culture often values:
- Humility and modesty
- Controlled emotions
- Formal, reserved presentation style

English business presentations often require:
- Confidence and enthusiasm
- Expressive delivery
- Dynamic, engaging style

**Practice showing appropriate enthusiasm without feeling "over the top":**

```
Korean style: "저희 결과가 좋습니다." (modest, understated)
English style: "We achieved OUTSTANDING results!" (confident, enthusiastic)

Korean style: "제안을 검토해 주시기 바랍니다." (formal, indirect)
English style: "I strongly ENCOURAGE you to approve this proposal." (direct, persuasive)
```

## Part 7: Self-Assessment Checklist | 자가 평가 체크리스트

### Pace & Rhythm | 속도와 리듬
- [ ] I speak at 120-150 words per minute for presentations
- [ ] I slow down to 100-120 wpm for key points
- [ ] I stress content words and reduce function words
- [ ] I don't add extra syllables or equal stress to each word
- [ ] My speech has natural rhythm, not robotic delivery

### Emphasis & Stress | 강조와 강세
- [ ] I know which words to stress in sentences
- [ ] I use contrastive stress when needed
- [ ] I emphasize new information appropriately
- [ ] I don't stress every word equally
- [ ] I can shift stress to change meaning

### Pausing | 쉼
- [ ] I pause appropriately between ideas
- [ ] I use dramatic pauses before key information
- [ ] I don't fill pauses with "um", "uh", "어"
- [ ] I give the audience time to process information
- [ ] I pause for 2-3 seconds at major transitions

### Voice Projection | 음성 투사
- [ ] I speak from my diaphragm, not my throat
- [ ] My voice carries to the back of the room
- [ ] I don't strain or shout
- [ ] I can adjust volume based on room size
- [ ] My voice sounds confident and clear

### Voice Modulation | 음성 조절
- [ ] I vary my pitch to show emotion and emphasis
- [ ] I use different volumes strategically
- [ ] I show appropriate energy and enthusiasm
- [ ] I sound engaged, not monotone
- [ ] I can modulate voice to match content (serious, exciting, etc.)

### Overall Delivery | 전반적 전달
- [ ] I sound natural, not scripted
- [ ] I make eye contact with the audience
- [ ] I use hand gestures appropriately
- [ ] I smile when appropriate
- [ ] I project confidence and professionalism

## Part 8: Daily Practice Routine | 일일 연습 루틴

### 10-Minute Morning Routine | 10분 아침 루틴

**Minutes 1-3: Breathing & Projection**
```
1. Deep breathing exercises (5 breaths)
2. Humming to warm up voice
3. Counting 1-10 with increasing volume
4. Say "Good morning!" at different volumes
```

**Minutes 4-6: Pace & Rhythm**
```
1. Read a paragraph at 120 wpm
2. Read the same paragraph at 150 wpm
3. Read again with proper stress on content words
```

**Minutes 7-9: Emphasis & Pausing**
```
1. Practice a business sentence with different stress patterns
2. Read a passage inserting strategic pauses
3. Record yourself and listen back
```

**Minute 10: Full Practice**
```
Deliver a 1-minute presentation summary with everything combined:
- Proper pace
- Strategic emphasis
- Effective pauses
- Good projection
- Voice modulation
```

### Weekly Practice Goals | 주간 연습 목표

**Monday:** Focus on pace and rhythm
**Tuesday:** Practice emphasis and stress
**Wednesday:** Work on pausing techniques
**Thursday:** Improve voice projection
**Friday:** Practice voice modulation
**Saturday:** Run through complete practice scripts
**Sunday:** Record a 3-5 minute presentation and self-assess

## Conclusion | 결론

Effective presentation delivery combines:
1. **Appropriate pace** - Not too fast, not too slow
2. **Strategic emphasis** - Stress the right words
3. **Purposeful pausing** - Give time for processing
4. **Clear projection** - Speak from the diaphragm
5. **Dynamic modulation** - Vary pitch, volume, and energy

효과적인 프레젠테이션 전달의 조합:
1. 적절한 속도
2. 전략적 강조

4. 명확한 투사
5. 역동적인 조절

**Remember:**
- Practice daily, even if just 10 minutes
- Record yourself regularly
- Get feedback from others
- Be patient with yourself
- Confidence comes with practice
