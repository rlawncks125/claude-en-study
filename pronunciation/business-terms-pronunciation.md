# Business Terms Pronunciation Guide
# 비즈니스 용어 발음 가이드

## Introduction | 소개

This comprehensive guide provides pronunciation guidance for 500+ essential business terms. Each entry includes IPA notation, Korean approximation, stress patterns, and common mispronunciation warnings.

이 종합 가이드는 500개 이상의 필수 비즈니스 용어에 대한 발음 안내를 제공합니다.

---

## How to Use This Guide | 가이드 사용법

### Notation System | 표기 체계

**IPA:** International Phonetic Alphabet (국제 음성 기호)
**Stress:** ● = primary stress, ○ = unstressed (● = 주 강세, ○ = 비강세)
**Korean:** Approximation only - use proper English sounds (근사음일 뿐 - 올바른 영어 발음 사용)

### Common Mispronunciation Patterns | 흔한 발음 실수 패턴

1. **Wrong syllable stress** - Most common error
2. **Adding extra vowels** - Korean tendency
3. **Dropping final consonants** - Clarity issue
4. **Confusing similar sounds** - R/L, F/V, TH

## Tips for Mastering Business Pronunciation | 비즈니스 발음 마스터 팁

### 1. Learn Stress Patterns by Category | 카테고리별 강세 패턴 학습

**-tion/-sion endings:** Stress the syllable BEFORE
- presentation, communication, decision

**-ic endings:** Stress the syllable BEFORE
- strategic, economic, specific

**-ity endings:** Stress the syllable BEFORE
- responsibility, productivity, opportunity

### 2. Practice with Real Business Content | 실제 비즈니스 콘텐츠로 연습

- Read business news articles aloud
- Listen to earnings calls
- Watch business presentations
- Shadow business podcasts

### 3. Record and Compare | 녹음하고 비교하기

1. Find a native speaker pronunciation (online dictionary)
2. Record yourself saying the word
3. Compare and identify differences
4. Practice until similar

### 4. Focus on High-Frequency Terms First | 고빈도 용어 우선 집중

Start with the 100 most common terms in your industry, then expand.

### 5. Use Pronunciation in Context | 맥락 속에서 발음 사용

Don't just practice individual words - use them in sentences and presentations.

## Quick Reference: Pronunciation Rules | 빠른 참조: 발음 규칙

### Syllable Stress Patterns

```
2-syllable nouns/adjectives: Usually ● ○ (PRESent, PROject)
2-syllable verbs: Usually ○ ● (preSENT, proJECT)
3+ syllable words ending in -tion/-sion: Stress before ending
3+ syllable words ending in -ic: Stress before ending
3+ syllable words ending in -ity: Stress before ending
```
